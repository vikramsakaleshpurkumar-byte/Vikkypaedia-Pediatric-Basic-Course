### Card 7 (Core Pediatric Concept)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In the pediatric evaluation of **Pneumothorax** (*Pneumothorax*), how is **PATHOGENESIS** characterized?</summary>

**Answer:**
PATHOGENESIS The tendency of the lung to collapse, or elastic recoil, is balanced in the normal resting state by the inherent tendency of the chest wall to expand outward, creating negative pressure in the intra pleural space.

</details>
