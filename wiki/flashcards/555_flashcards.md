### Card 4 (Pediatric Genetics & Pathogenesis)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In **Rare Tumors**, what clinical phenotype or molecular mechanism is associated with **`RET`**?</summary>

**Answer:**
MTC is an uncommon disease in childhood that usually occurs in the context of multiple endocrine neoplasia type 2a (MEN2A) or type 2b (MEN2B), autosomal dominant, hereditary endocrine tumor syndromes that arise secondary to activating variants in the RET proto-oncogene.
