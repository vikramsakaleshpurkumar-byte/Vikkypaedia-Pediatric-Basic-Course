**Associated Gene(s):** `PMP22, CMT1A, CMT3` · **Category / Inheritance:** Neuromuscular Disorders **Clinical Synthesis:** PMP22 (usually duplication); Most common (70% of all CMT1); Pathogenic variants also cause CMT3
