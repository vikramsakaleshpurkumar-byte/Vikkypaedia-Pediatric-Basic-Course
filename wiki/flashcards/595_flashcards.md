### Card 4 (Pediatric Genetics & Pathogenesis)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In **Hypopituitarism**, what clinical phenotype or molecular mechanism is associated with **`POU1F1`**?</summary>

**Answer:**
The PROP1 and POU1F1 genes are expressed later in pituitary development only in cells of the anterior pituitary and result in hypopituitarism without anomalies in other organ systems.

</details>

### Card 5 (Pediatric Genetics & Pathogenesis)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In **Hypopituitarism**, what clinical phenotype or molecular mechanism is associated with **`HESX1`**?</summary>

**Answer:**
The HESX1, LHX3, LHX4, OTX2, SOX3, and PITX2 genes are expressed at earlier stages and are not restricted to the pituitary.

</details>
