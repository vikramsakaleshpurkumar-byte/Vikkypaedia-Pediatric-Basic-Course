### Card 2 (Pediatric Therapeutics & Management)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> What is the clinical management or therapeutic approach for **Hyperphosphatasia**?</summary>

**Answer:**
**Therapy / Protocol:** Management **Clinical Application:** Given the rarity of this disorder, there is no strong evidence surrounding optimal clinical management; however, antiresorptive therapy with bisphosphonates is associated with clinical, biochemical, and radiographic improvement.
