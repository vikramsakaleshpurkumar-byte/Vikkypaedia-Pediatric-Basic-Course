<details><summary><b>Q:</b> What are the hallmark clinical features and diagnostic profile of **STRUCTURAL (Nonimmune Hydrops)**?</summary>

**Answer:**
**Category / Inheritance:** The Fetus and the Neonatal I **Clinical Synthesis:** • Hypoplasias (left or right heart); • AV canal defect; • Single ventricle; • Transposition of great arteries; • Septal defects (VSD/ASD); • Tetralogy of Fallot; • Ebstein anomaly; • Ductus arteriosus closure; • Truncus arteriosus; • Valvular (stenosis/insufficiency)
