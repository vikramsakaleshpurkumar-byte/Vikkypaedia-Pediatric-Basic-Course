**Answer:**

### Card 5 (Core Pediatric Concept)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In the pediatric evaluation of **Spinal Cord Injuries in Children** (*Spinal Cord Injuries in Children*), how is **adolescent** characterized?</summary>

**Answer:**
adolescent form, also called transient neurapraxia, is assumed to be a spinal cord concus sion or mild contusion, as opposed to the severe spinal cord injury related to the mobility of the spine in small children.

</details>
