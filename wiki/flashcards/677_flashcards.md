### Card 7 (Core Pediatric Concept)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In the pediatric evaluation of **Hearing Loss** (*Hearing Loss*), how is **Conductive hearing loss (CHL)** characterized?</summary>

**Answer:**
Conductive hearing loss (CHL) is the most common type of hearing loss in children and occurs when sound transmission is physically impeded in the exter nal and/or middle ear, most commonly by otitis media (OM) with effusion.

</details>
