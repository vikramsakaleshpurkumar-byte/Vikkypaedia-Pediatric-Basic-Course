### Card 6 (Pediatric Therapeutics & Management)

### Card 7 (Pediatric Therapeutics & Management)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> What is the clinical management or therapeutic approach for **Physiology of the Adrenal Gland (614.2 Adrenal Steroid Biosynthesis Perrin C. White)**?</summary>

**Answer:**
**Therapy / Protocol:** Surfactant **Clinical Application:** Cortisol activity is low during the second trimester, which might serve to prevent premature secretion of surfactant by the developing fetal lungs; surfactant levels can affect the timing of parturition.

</details>
