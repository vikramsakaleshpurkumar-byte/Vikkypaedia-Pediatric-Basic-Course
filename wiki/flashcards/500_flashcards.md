### Card 4 (Pediatric Therapeutics & Management)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> What is the clinical management or therapeutic approach for **Anemia of Chronic Disease and Renal Disease**?</summary>

**Answer:**
**Therapy / Protocol:** Immunoglobulin **Clinical Application:** In chronically infected patients, the disease may be treated with high doses of intravenous immunoglobulin, which contains neutralizing antibody to parvovirus and is effective in the short term.

### Card 6 (Core Pediatric Concept)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In the pediatric evaluation of **Anemia of Chronic Disease and Renal Disease** (*500.1 Anemia of Chronic Disease Courtney D. Thornburg*), how is **ETIOLOGY** characterized?</summary>

**Answer:**
ETIOLOGY Decreased red cell life span, impaired erythropoiesis, and an increased uptake of iron in the reticuloendothelial system are important mecha nisms contributing to ACD.
