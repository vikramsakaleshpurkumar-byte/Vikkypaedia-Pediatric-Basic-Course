### Card 3 (Syndrome & Clinical Profile)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> What are the hallmark clinical features and diagnostic profile of **DIAGNOSES INCLUDE (Marfan Syndrome)**?</summary>

**Answer:**
**Associated Gene(s):** `FBN1` · **Category / Inheritance:** Bone and Joint Disorders **Clinical Synthesis:** 1. Ectopia lentis ± systemic score AND FBN1 pathogenic variant not; known to associate with aortic aneurysm or no FBN1 pathogenic; variant = Ectopia lentis syndrome; 2. ### Card 4 (Pediatric Genetics & Pathogenesis)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In **Marfan Syndrome**, what clinical phenotype or molecular mechanism is associated with **`FBN1`**?</summary>

**Answer:**
Marfan syndrome (MFS) is an inherited systemic, connective tissue disorder caused by pathogenic variants in the FBN1 gene encoding the extracellular matrix (ECM) protein fibrillin-1.

</details>
