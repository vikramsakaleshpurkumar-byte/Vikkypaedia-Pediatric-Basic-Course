### Card 2 (Pediatric Genetics & Pathogenesis)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In **Genetics in Pediatric Medicine**, what clinical phenotype or molecular mechanism is associated with **`SLC26A4`**?</summary>

**Answer:**
Pendred syndrome is caused by pathogenic variants in SLC26A4 and is characterized by both hearing loss and thyroid enlargement (goiter).

</details>
