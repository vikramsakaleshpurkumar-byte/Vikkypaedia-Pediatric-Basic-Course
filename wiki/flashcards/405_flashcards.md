### Card 1 (Syndrome & Clinical Profile)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> What are the hallmark clinical features and diagnostic profile of **Crigler-Najjar Type 1 vs Type 2 (UGT1A1)**?</summary>

**Answer:**
**Associated Gene(s):** `UGT1A1` · **Category / Inheritance:** The Digestive System **Clinical Synthesis:** CN1: 0% UGT1A1, unconjugated bilirubin 20–45 mg/dL, no response to phenobarbital, needs phototherapy/liver transplant; CN2: <10% UGT1A1, responds to phenobarbital

</details>

### Card 2 (Syndrome & Clinical Profile)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> What are the hallmark clinical features and diagnostic profile of **Dubin-Johnson vs Rotor Syndrome**?</summary>

**Answer:**
**Associated Gene(s):** `ABCC2, SLCO1B1` · **Category / Inheritance:** The Digestive System **Clinical Synthesis:** Both cause benign conjugated hyperbilirubinemia with normal ALT/AST/GGT; Dubin-Johnson (ABCC2/MRP2) has black liver & >80% coproporphyrin I; Rotor (SLCO1B1/1B3) has normal liver pigment

</details>

### Card 7 (Core Pediatric Concept)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In the pediatric evaluation of **Metabolic Diseases of the Liver** (*Metabolic Diseases of the Liver*), how is **Crigler-Najjar type 2** characterized?</summary>

**Answer:**
Crigler-Najjar type 2 (OMIM #606785; <10% activity, bilirubin 6–20 mg/dL, decreases ≥25% with phenobarbital), and Gilbert syndrome (OMIM #143500; benign UGT1A1 TA7 promoter polymorphism causing mild unconjugated hyperbilirubinemia during fasting/illness).

</details>

### Card 8 (Core Pediatric Concept)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In the pediatric evaluation of **Metabolic Diseases of the Liver** (*Metabolic Diseases of the Liver*), how is **Dubin-Johnson syndrome** characterized?</summary>

**Answer:**
Dubin-Johnson syndrome (ABCC2 / MRP2 defect [237500]; grossly black liver, elevated urinary coproporphyrin I >80%) and Rotor syndrome (SLCO1B1 + SLCO1B3 digenic defect [237450]; normal liver color).

</details>
