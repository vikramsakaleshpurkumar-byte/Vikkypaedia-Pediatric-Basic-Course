### Card 6 (Core Pediatric Concept)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In the pediatric evaluation of **Burn Injuries** (*Burn Injuries*), how is **Thermal burns** characterized?</summary>

**Answer:**
Thermal burns are caused when an external heat source comes in contact with the skin and tissues causing injury; thermal burns may occur from contact with hot surfaces, hot liquids, steam, or flame.

</details>

### Card 7 (Core Pediatric Concept)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In the pediatric evaluation of **Burn Injuries** (*Burn Injuries*), how is **Scald burns** characterized?</summary>

**Answer:**
Scald burns are most prevalent in children ≤4 years old and account for 65% of burn-related admissions in this age-group.

</details>
