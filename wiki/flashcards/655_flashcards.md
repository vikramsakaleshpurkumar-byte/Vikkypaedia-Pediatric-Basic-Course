### Card 7 (Core Pediatric Concept)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In the pediatric evaluation of **Autonomic Neuropathies** (*655.1 Familial Dysautonomia Monique M. Ryan*), how is **PATHOLOGY** characterized?</summary>

**Answer:**
PATHOLOGY This disease of the peripheral nerves is characterized pathologically by a reduced number of the small unmyelinated nerve fibers that carry pain, temperature, and taste sensation and mediate autonomic func tions, including baroreceptors.
