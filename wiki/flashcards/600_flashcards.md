### Card 4 (Pediatric Genetics & Pathogenesis)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In **Disorders of Pubertal Development**, what clinical phenotype or molecular mechanism is associated with **`GNAS1`**?</summary>

**Answer:**
An activating missense pathogenic variant in the GNAS1 gene encoding the α-subunit of GS, the G protein that stimulates cyclic adenosine monophosphate (cAMP) formation, results in activation of receptors (adrenocorticotropic hormone [ACTH], TSH, FSH, and LH receptors) that operate via a cAMP-dependent mechanism, as well as cell proliferation.

</details>

### Card 5 (Pediatric Genetics & Pathogenesis)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In **Disorders of Pubertal Development**, what clinical phenotype or molecular mechanism is associated with **`FGF23`**?</summary>

**Answer:**

</details>
