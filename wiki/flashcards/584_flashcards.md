### Card 7 (Core Pediatric Concept)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In the pediatric evaluation of **Urinary Lithiasis** (*Urinary Lithiasis*), how is **Matrix** characterized?</summary>

**Answer:**
Matrix is a mixture of protein, nonamino sugars, glucosamine, water, and organic ash that makes up 2–9% of the dry weight of urinary stones and is arranged within the stones in orga nized concentric laminations.

</details>
