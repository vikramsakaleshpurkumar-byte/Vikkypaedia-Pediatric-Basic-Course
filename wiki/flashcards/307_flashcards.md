### Card 2 (Pediatric Therapeutics & Management)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> What is the clinical management or therapeutic approach for **Respiratory Syncytial Virus**?</summary>

**Answer:**
**Therapy / Protocol:** Immunoglobulin **Clinical Application:** Transplacentally acquired anti-RSV maternal immunoglobulin G (IgG) serum antibodies, if present in high concentration, appear to provide partial protection for the neonate.

</details>
