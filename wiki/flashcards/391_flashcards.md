### Card 6 (Core Pediatric Concept)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In the pediatric evaluation of **Acute Appendicitis** (*Acute Appendicitis*), how is **EPIDEMIOLOGY** characterized?</summary>

**Answer:**
EPIDEMIOLOGY The incidence of acute appendicitis increases with age, from a rate of 1-2 per 10,000 children from birth to 4 years of age, to a rate of 19-28 per 10,000 children younger than age 14 years annually.

</details>
