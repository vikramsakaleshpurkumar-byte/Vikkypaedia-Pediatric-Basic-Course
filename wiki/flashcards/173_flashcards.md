### Card 4 (Pediatric Genetics & Pathogenesis)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In **Complement System**, what clinical phenotype or molecular mechanism is associated with **`CR1`**?</summary>

**Answer:**
Once C3b is fixed to a complex or dead or dying host cell, it can bind to cells with receptors for C3b (complement receptor 1, CR1), including B lymphocytes, erythrocytes, and phagocytic cells (neutrophils, monocytes, and macrophages).
