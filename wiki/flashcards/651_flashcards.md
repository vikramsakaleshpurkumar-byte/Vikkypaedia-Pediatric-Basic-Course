<details><summary><b>Q:</b> What are the hallmark clinical features and diagnostic profile of **INFLAMMATORY CAUSES**?</summary>

### Card 2 (Syndrome & Clinical Profile)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> What are the hallmark clinical features and diagnostic profile of **NONINFLAMMATORY NECROTIZING OR VACUOLAR CAUSES**?</summary>

**Answer:**
**Category / Inheritance:** Neuromuscular Disorders **Clinical Synthesis:** Cholesterol-lowering agents (statins); Chloroquine; Colchicine; Emetine; ε-Aminocaproic acid; Labetalol; Cyclosporine and tacrolimus; Isotretinoic acid (vitamin A analog); Vincristine; Statins; Alcohol

</details>
