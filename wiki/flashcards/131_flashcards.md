### Card 6 (Core Pediatric Concept)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In the pediatric evaluation of **Diaphragmatic Hernia** (*CONGENITAL DIAPHRAGMATIC HERNIA (BOCHDALEK)*), how is **Epidemiology** characterized?</summary>

**Answer:**
Epidemiology The incidence of CDH is between 1 in 2,000 and 1 in 3,000 live births, with males affected more often than females.
