### Card 5 (Core Pediatric Concept)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In the pediatric evaluation of **Other Abnormalities of Arginine Vasopressin Metabolism and Action** (*Other Abnormalities of Arginine Vasopressin Metabolism and Action*), how is **CAUSES OF HYPONATREMIA Systemic Dehydration** characterized?</summary>

**Answer:**
CAUSES OF HYPONATREMIA Systemic Dehydration The initial manifestation of systemic dehydration is often hypernatremia and hyperosmolality, which subsequently lead to the activation of vasopressin secretion and a decrease in water excretion.

</details>

### Card 6 (Core Pediatric Concept)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In the pediatric evaluation of **Other Abnormalities of Arginine Vasopressin Metabolism and Action** (*Other Abnormalities of Arginine Vasopressin Metabolism and Action*), how is **Decreased Effective Plasma Volume** characterized?</summary>

**Answer:**
Decreased Effective Plasma Volume Hyponatremia can result from decreased effective plasma volume, as found in congestive heart failure, cirrhosis, nephrotic syndrome, positive pressure mechanical ventilation, severe burns, bronchopulmonary dysplasia in neonates, cystic fibrosis with obstruction, and severe asthma.

</details>

### Card 7 (Core Pediatric Concept)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In the pediatric evaluation of **Other Abnormalities of Arginine Vasopressin Metabolism and Action** (*Other Abnormalities of Arginine Vasopressin Metabolism and Action*), how is **Syndrome of Inappropriate Antidiuresis** characterized?</summary>

**Answer:**
Syndrome of Inappropriate Antidiuresis The syndrome of inappropriate antidiuresis (SIAD) is characterized by hyponatremia, an inappropriately concentrated urine (often >800 mOsm/kg H2O), normal or slightly elevated plasma volume, normalto-high urine sodium, and low serum uric acid.

</details>
