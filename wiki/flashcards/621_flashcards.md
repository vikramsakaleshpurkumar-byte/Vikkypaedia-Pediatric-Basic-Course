### Card 1 (Syndrome & Clinical Profile)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> What are the hallmark clinical features and diagnostic profile of **Pheochromocytoma**?</summary>

**Answer:**
**Associated Gene(s):** `NF1, RET, VHL` · **Category / Inheritance:** AD **Clinical Synthesis:** An autosomal dominant pattern of inheritance should raise suspicion for the latter disorder.

### Card 2 (Pediatric Genetics & Pathogenesis)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In **Pheochromocytoma**, what clinical phenotype or molecular mechanism is associated with **`VHL`**?</summary>

**Answer:**
Germline variants in the VHL tumor-suppressor gene on chromosome 3p25-26 have been identified in patients with this syndrome.

</details>

### Card 3 (Pediatric Genetics & Pathogenesis)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In **Pheochromocytoma**, what clinical phenotype or molecular mechanism is associated with **`RET`**?</summary>

**Answer:**
Variants of the RET protooncogene on chromosome 10q11.2 have been found in families with MEN2A and MEN2B.

</details>

### Card 4 (Pediatric Therapeutics & Management)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> What is the clinical management or therapeutic approach for **Pheochromocytoma**?</summary>

**Answer:**
**Therapy / Protocol:** Fludrocortisone **Clinical Application:** Aldosterone does not decrease with administration of saline solution or fludrocortisone, and renin does not respond to salt and fluid restriction.

</details>

### Card 5 (Core Pediatric Concept)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In the pediatric evaluation of **Pheochromocytoma** (*Pheochromocytoma*), how is **Glucocorticoid- remediable aldosteronism** characterized?</summary>

**Answer:**
Glucocorticoid- remediable aldosteronism is diagnosed by dexamethasone suppression tests or by specific genetic testing.

</details>

### Card 6 (Core Pediatric Concept)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In the pediatric evaluation of **Pheochromocytoma** (*Pheochromocytoma*), how is **TREATMENT** characterized?</summary>

**Answer:**
TREATMENT The treatment of an aldosterone-producing adenoma is laparoscopic adrenalectomy; successful enucleation of aldosterone-producing adenomas has also been reported.

</details>
