### Card 4 (Pediatric Genetics & Pathogenesis)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In **Neoplasms of the Kidney**, what clinical phenotype or molecular mechanism is associated with **`WT1`**?</summary>

**Answer:**
The first identified Wilms tumor gene, WT1, located at 11p13, is a homo zygous gene variant in 10–15% of tumors, resulting in loss of function of the encoded zinc finger transcription factor.

</details>
