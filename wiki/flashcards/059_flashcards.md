### Card 3 (Pediatric Genetics & Pathogenesis)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In **Fragile X Syndromes**, what clinical phenotype or molecular mechanism is associated with **`FMR1`**?</summary>

**Answer:**
In FXS an expansion of >200 CGG repeats on the distal long arm of chromosome Xq27.3 silences the FMR1 gene, which leads to a deficiency in fragile X mental retardation protein (FMRP) and affects synaptic function throughout much of the brain.

</details>
