### Card 1 (Syndrome & Clinical Profile)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> What are the hallmark clinical features and diagnostic profile of **Pyogenic Liver Abscess (PLA)**?</summary>

**Answer:**
**Category / Inheritance:** The Digestive System **Clinical Synthesis:** S. aureus (#1 in children & CGD), E. coli, Klebsiella, anaerobes; US/CT-guided percutaneous drainage + 4–6 wk IV/PO antibiotics; screen for CGD with DHR test

</details>

### Card 2 (Syndrome & Clinical Profile)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> What are the hallmark clinical features and diagnostic profile of **Amebic Liver Abscess (Entamoeba histolytica)**?</summary>

**Answer:**
**Category / Inheritance:** The Digestive System **Clinical Synthesis:** Solitary right-lobe cavity ('anchovy paste'); E. histolytica serum IgG + (>95%); metronidazole/tinidazole PLUS luminal agent (paromomycin or iodoquinol); avoid drainage unless imminent rupture

</details>

### Card 3 (Syndrome & Clinical Profile)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> What are the hallmark clinical features and diagnostic profile of **Hepatosplenic Candidiasis**?</summary>

**Answer:**
**Category / Inheritance:** The Digestive System **Clinical Synthesis:** Neutropenic recovery in oncology patients; 'bull's-eye' target microabscesses on CT/US; liposomal amphotericin B or echinocandin (micafungin/caspofungin) followed by fluconazole

</details>

### Card 8 (Core Pediatric Concept)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In the pediatric evaluation of **Liver Abscess** (*Liver Abscess*), how is **Staphylococcus aureus** characterized?</summary>

**Answer:**
Staphylococcus aureus is the single most common pediatric pathogen (especially in CGD), followed by Escherichia coli, Klebsiella pneumoniae, anaerobes (Bacteroides), and Streptococcus anginosus group.

</details>
