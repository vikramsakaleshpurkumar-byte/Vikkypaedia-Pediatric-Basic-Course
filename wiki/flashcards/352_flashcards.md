### Card 7 (Core Pediatric Concept)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In the pediatric evaluation of **Major Symptoms and Signs of Digestive Tract Disorders** (*Major Symptoms and Signs of Digestive Tract Disorders*), how is **Jaundice** characterized?</summary>

**Answer:**
Jaundice is common in neonates, especially among premature infants, and usually results from the inability of an immature liver to conjugate bilirubin, leading to an elevated indirect component.

</details>

### Card 8 (Core Pediatric Concept)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In the pediatric evaluation of **Major Symptoms and Signs of Digestive Tract Disorders** (*Major Symptoms and Signs of Digestive Tract Disorders*), how is **Oropharyngeal dysphagia** characterized?</summary>

**Answer:**
Oropharyngeal dysphagia occurs when the transfer of the food bolus from the mouth to the esophagus is impaired (also termed transfer dysphagia).

</details>
