### Card 6 (Core Pediatric Concept)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In the pediatric evaluation of **Torsional and Angular Deformities of the Limb** (*716.2 Limb Evaluation Brendan A. Williams, Jennifer J. Winell, and Lawrence Wells*), how is **FOOT PROGRESSION ANGLE** characterized?</summary>

**Answer:**
FOOT PROGRESSION ANGLE Limb position during gait is expressed as the foot progression angle and represents the angular difference between the axis of the foot with the direction in which the child is walking.

</details>
