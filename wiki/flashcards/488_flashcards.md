### Card 5 (Pediatric Genetics & Pathogenesis)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In **Diseases of the Myocardium**, what clinical phenotype or molecular mechanism is associated with **`TAZ`**?</summary>

**Answer:**
Elevated serum lactate and urine 3-methylglutaconic acid may be seen in Barth syndrome, an X-linked disorder of phospholipid metabolism caused by a pathogenic variant in the TAZ gene.
