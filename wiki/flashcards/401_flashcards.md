### Card 4 (Pediatric Genetics & Pathogenesis)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In **Pancreatic Tumors**, what clinical phenotype or molecular mechanism is associated with **`APC`**?</summary>

**Answer:**
Pancreatoblastoma is the classic pancreatic malignancy of early childhood (median age 4–5 years), exhibits characteristic squamoid corpuscles on histology, is associated with Beckwith-Wiedemann syndrome (11p15.5) and *familial adenomatous polyposis (APC), and secretes alpha-fetoprotein (AFP) in ~65% of cases.

</details>

### Card 5 (Pediatric Genetics & Pathogenesis)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In **Pancreatic Tumors**, what clinical phenotype or molecular mechanism is associated with **`MEN1`**?</summary>

**Answer:**
Functioning PanNETs include insulinoma (Whipple triad: fasting/exercise hypoglycemia <50 mg/dL, neuroglycopenic symptoms, relief with glucose; associated with MEN1 [131100]) and gastrinoma* (Zollinger-Ellison syndrome: refractory peptic ulcer disease and secretory diarrhea).

</details>
