### Card 7 (Core Pediatric Concept)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In the pediatric evaluation of **Systemic Hypertension** (*Systemic Hypertension*), how is **Elevated** characterized?</summary>

**Answer:**
Elevated blood pressure is considered systolic BP of 120-129 and diastolic BP <80 mm Hg.

</details>

### Card 8 (Core Pediatric Concept)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In the pediatric evaluation of **Systemic Hypertension** (*Systemic Hypertension*), how is **Stage 1** characterized?</summary>

**Answer:**
Stage 1 hypertension is systolic 130-139 or diastolic 80-89 mm Hg; Stage 2 hypertension is systolic ≥140 or diastolic ≥90 mm Hg.

</details>
