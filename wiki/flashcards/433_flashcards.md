**Answer:**
croup refers to a heterogeneous group of mainly acute and infectious processes that are characterized by a bark like or metallic/brassy cough and may be associated with hoarseness, inspiratory stridor, and respiratory distress.

**Answer:**
Stridor is a harsh, highpitched respiratory sound that is usually inspiratory but can be bipha sic and is produced by turbulent airflow; it is not a diagnosis, but a sign of upper airway obstruction.
