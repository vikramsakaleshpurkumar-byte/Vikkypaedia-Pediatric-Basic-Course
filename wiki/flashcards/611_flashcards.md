### Card 4 (Pediatric Genetics & Pathogenesis)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In **Hypoparathyroidism**, what clinical phenotype or molecular mechanism is associated with **`TBX1`**?</summary>

**Answer:**
In 90% of patients, the condition is caused by a deletion of chromosome 22q11.2 with involvement of the TBX1 gene.

</details>

### Card 5 (Pediatric Genetics & Pathogenesis)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In **Hypoparathyroidism**, what clinical phenotype or molecular mechanism is associated with **`GCM2`**?</summary>

**Answer:**
Loss-of-function pathogenic variants in the GCM2 gene, which is a key regulator of parathyroid gland development, leads to isolated parathyroid aplasia and hypoparathyroidism.

</details>
