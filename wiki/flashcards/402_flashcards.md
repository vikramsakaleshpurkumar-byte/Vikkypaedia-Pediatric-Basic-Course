### Card 3 (Syndrome & Clinical Profile)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> What are the hallmark clinical features and diagnostic profile of **Alagille Syndrome (Morphogenesis of the Liver and Biliary System)**?</summary>

**Answer:**
**Associated Gene(s):** `JAG1, NOTCH2` · **Category / Inheritance:** The Digestive System **Clinical Synthesis:** JAG1 (94%) or NOTCH2 (2–4%) variants; bile duct paucity, butterfly vertebrae, posterior embryotoxon, peripheral pulmonary stenosis

</details>

### Card 4 (Pediatric Genetics & Pathogenesis)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In **Morphogenesis of the Liver and Biliary System**, what clinical phenotype or molecular mechanism is associated with **`HNF1B`**?</summary>

**Answer:**
Intrahepatic bile ducts develop from the ductal plate—a double-layered cylindrical sleeve of hepatoblasts surrounding portal vein branches that undergoes remodeling regulated by the *Jagged1 (JAG1)–Notch2 (NOTCH2) signaling pathway and HNF1B.

</details>

### Card 5 (Pediatric Genetics & Pathogenesis)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In **Morphogenesis of the Liver and Biliary System**, what clinical phenotype or molecular mechanism is associated with **`PKHD1`**?</summary>

**Answer:**
Arrest or maldevelopment of ductal plate remodeling (ductal plate malformation) underlies Alagille syndrome (JAG1 [118450], NOTCH2 [610205]), congenital hepatic fibrosis, Caroli disease/syndrome (PKHD1* [263200]), and biliary atresia splenic malformation (BASM) syndrome.

</details>
