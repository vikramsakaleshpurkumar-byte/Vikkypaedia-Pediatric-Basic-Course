### Card 1 (Syndrome & Clinical Profile)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> What are the hallmark clinical features and diagnostic profile of **Primary Aldosteronism**?</summary>

**Answer:**
**Associated Gene(s):** `APC, ATP1A1, CACNA1D, CACNA1H, CLCN2` · **Category / Inheritance:** The Endocrine System **Clinical Synthesis:** Primary aldosteronism encompasses disorders caused by excessive aldosterone secretion independent of the renin-angiotensin system.

</details>

### Card 2 (Pediatric Genetics & Pathogenesis)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In **Primary Aldosteronism**, what clinical phenotype or molecular mechanism is associated with **`CACNA1H`**?</summary>

**Answer:**
Germline variants have also been reported in CYP11B2 (encoding aldosterone synthase), CLCN2 (encoding voltage-gated chloride channel ClC-2), and CACNA1H (encoding a subunit of the T-type voltage-gated calcium channel CaV3.2).

</details>

### Card 3 (Pediatric Genetics & Pathogenesis)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In **Primary Aldosteronism**, what clinical phenotype or molecular mechanism is associated with **`ATP1A1`**?</summary>

**Answer:**
Germline and somatic variants have also been reported in CACNA1D encoding a voltage-sensitive calcium channel and somatic variants in ATP1A1 and ATP2B3, respectively, encoding sodium-potassium and calcium ATPases.

</details>

### Card 4 (Pediatric Therapeutics & Management)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> What is the clinical management or therapeutic approach for **Primary Aldosteronism**?</summary>

**Answer:**
**Therapy / Protocol:** Dexamethasone **Clinical Application:** ACTH, Adreno- corticotropic hormone; BIPSS, bilateral inferior petrosal sinus sampling; CRH, corticotropin-releasing hormone; HDDSST, high-dose dexamethasone suppression test. (From Lacroix A, Feelders RA, Stratakis CA, et al.

</details>

### Card 5 (Core Pediatric Concept)

EPIDEMIOLOGY These conditions are thought to be rare in children, but they may account for 5–10% of cases of hypertension in adults.
