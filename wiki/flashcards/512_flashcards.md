### Card 4 (Pediatric Genetics & Pathogenesis)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In **Enzymatic Defects**, what clinical phenotype or molecular mechanism is associated with **`G6PD`**?</summary>

**Answer:**
Glucose-6-phosphate dehydrogenase (G6PD) deficiency, the most frequent disease involving enzymes of the hexose monophosphate pathway, is responsible for two clinical syndromes: episodic acute hemolytic anemia and chronic nonspherocytic hemolytic anemia.
