### Card 1 (Syndrome & Clinical Profile)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> What are the hallmark clinical features and diagnostic profile of **Congenital Dyserythropoietic Anemias**?</summary>

**Answer:**
**Associated Gene(s):** `CDAN1, CDIN1, GATA1, KIF23, KLF1` · **Category / Inheritance:** Diseases of the Blood **Clinical Synthesis:** These rare conditions are characterized by variable degrees of anemia, ineffective erythropoiesis, hyperbilirubinemia, gallstones, splenomegaly, and secondary hemochromatosis (iron overload).

</details>
