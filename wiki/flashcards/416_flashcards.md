### Card 2 (Syndrome & Clinical Profile)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> What are the hallmark clinical features and diagnostic profile of **PELD Score (<12 Years Old)**?</summary>

**Answer:**
**Category / Inheritance:** The Digestive System **Clinical Synthesis:** Incorporates Total Bilirubin, INR, Serum Albumin, Age <1 yr, and Growth Failure (weight/height z-score < −2 SD); MELD used ≥12 yr

</details>

### Card 3 (Syndrome & Clinical Profile)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> What are the hallmark clinical features and diagnostic profile of **Hepatic Artery Thrombosis (HAT)**?</summary>

**Answer:**
**Category / Inheritance:** The Digestive System **Clinical Synthesis:** Most common & severe early vascular complication (<14 days) in small pediatric grafts; causes biliary necrosis/graft loss; screen with daily Doppler US

</details>

### Card 4 (Pediatric Therapeutics & Management)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> What is the clinical management or therapeutic approach for **EBV-Associated PTLD**?</summary>

**Answer:**
**Therapy / Protocol:** Key Clinical, Diagnostic, and Therapeutic Summary for Liver Transplantation **Clinical Application:** B-cell lymphoproliferation in EBV-naive pediatric recipients; monitor serial blood EBV PCR; first-line therapy: reduce immunosuppression + Rituximab (anti-CD20)

</details>

### Card 5 (Pediatric Therapeutics & Management)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> What is the clinical management or therapeutic approach for **Liver Transplantation**?</summary>

**Answer:**
**Therapy / Protocol:** Tacrolimus **Clinical Application:** Baseline maintenance immunosuppression relies on the calcineurin inhibitor tacrolimus (monitoring trough levels) + corticosteroids ± mycophenolate mofetil.

</details>

### Card 6 (Core Pediatric Concept)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In the pediatric evaluation of **Liver Transplantation** (*Liver Transplantation*), how is **Liver transplantation** characterized?</summary>

**Answer:**
Liver transplantation has transformed survival for children with end-stage chronic liver disease, pediatric acute liver failure (PALF), non-resectable hepatic malignancies, and liver-based inborn errors of metabolism, achieving 1-year survival >93–95% and 5-year survival >85–90%.

</details>

### Card 7 (Core Pediatric Concept)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In the pediatric evaluation of **Liver Transplantation** (*Liver Transplantation*), how is **unresectable hepatoblastoma** characterized?</summary>

**Answer:**
unresectable hepatoblastoma (PRETEXT III/IV) (~8–10%), and autoimmune/cholestatic cirrhosis (Alagille syndrome, PSC).

</details>

### Card 8 (Core Pediatric Concept)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In the pediatric evaluation of **Liver Transplantation** (*Liver Transplantation*), how is **PELD (Pediatric End-Stage Liver Disease) score** characterized?</summary>

**Answer:**
PELD (Pediatric End-Stage Liver Disease) score for children <12 years old (calculated from total bilirubin, INR, serum albumin, age <1 year, and growth failure [< −2 SD]) and the MELD-Na score for adolescents ≥12 years old.

</details>
