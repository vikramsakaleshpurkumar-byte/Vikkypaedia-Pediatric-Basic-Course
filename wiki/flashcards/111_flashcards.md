### Card 4 (Pediatric Genetics & Pathogenesis)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In **Hutchinson-Gilford Progeria Syndrome (Progeria)**, what clinical phenotype or molecular mechanism is associated with **`LMNA`**?</summary>

**Answer:**
Progeria is caused by a single-base pathogenic variant in the LMNA gene, which results in the production of an abnormal lamin A protein called progerin.
