### Card 7 (Core Pediatric Concept)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In the pediatric evaluation of **Contraception** (*Contraception*), how is **Tier 1** characterized?</summary>

**Answer:**
Tier 1 methods, the most effective, include those with failure rates of <1 pregnancy per 100 women in a year of typical use, and reversible Tier 1 methods include intrauterine devices (IUDs) and implants.

</details>

### Card 8 (Core Pediatric Concept)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In the pediatric evaluation of **Contraception** (*Contraception*), how is **Tier 2** characterized?</summary>

**Answer:**
Tier 2 methods have failure rates of 4-7 pregnancies per 100 women in a year of typical use and include injectable contraception, oral contraceptive pills, contraceptive patches, and the vaginal ring.

</details>
