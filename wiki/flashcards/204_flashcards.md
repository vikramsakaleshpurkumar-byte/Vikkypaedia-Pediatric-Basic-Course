### Card 4 (Pediatric Genetics & Pathogenesis)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In **Hereditary Periodic Fever Syndromes and Other Systemic Autoinflammatory Diseases**, what clinical phenotype or molecular mechanism is associated with **`MEFV`**?</summary>

**Answer:**
FMF is caused by autosomal recessive pathogenic variants in MEFV, a gene encoding a 781–amino acid protein denoted pyrin (Greek for “fever”).

### Card 8 (Core Pediatric Concept)

**Answer:**
Familial Mediterranean Fever FMF is a recessively inherited autoinflammatory disease usually charac terized by recurrent, short-lived (1-3 days), self-limited episodes of fever, serositis, monoarticular or pauciarticular arthritis, or an erysipeloid rash, sometimes complicated by AA amyloidosis.

</details>
