### Card 4 (Pediatric Genetics & Pathogenesis)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In **Liver Disease Associated with Systemic Disorders**, what clinical phenotype or molecular mechanism is associated with **`PNPLA3`**?</summary>

**Answer:**
Pediatric MASH frequently exhibits a distinct Type 2 (periportal / Zone 1) histologic pattern in younger children (especially boys) with portal inflammation and fibrosis without ballooning, in contrast to adult Type 1 (centrilobular / Zone 3) steatohepatitis; PNPLA3 (rs738409 I148M) is the strongest genetic risk modifier.

</details>
