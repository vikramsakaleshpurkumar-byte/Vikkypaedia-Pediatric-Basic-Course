<details><summary><b>Q:</b> What are the hallmark clinical features and diagnostic profile of **Intestinal Atresia, Stenosis, and Malrotation**?</summary>

**Answer:**
**Associated Gene(s):** `GUCY2C, TTC7A` · **Category / Inheritance:** The Digestive System **Clinical Synthesis:** Among these, congenital disruption of the tubular structure is most common and can manifest as obliteration (atresia) or narrowing (stenosis) of the intestinal lumen.

### Card 5 (Pediatric Therapeutics & Management)

### Card 7 (Core Pediatric Concept)
