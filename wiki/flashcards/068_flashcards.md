### Card 2 (Pediatric Therapeutics & Management)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> What is the clinical management or therapeutic approach for **Vitamin C (Ascorbic Acid) Deficiency and Excess**?</summary>

**Answer:**
**Therapy / Protocol:** Mg/kg **Clinical Application:** For patients receiving total parenteral nutrition (TPN), 80 mg/day is recommended for full-term infants and 25 mg/kg/day for preterm infants.

### Card 5 (Core Pediatric Concept)

**Answer:**
Clinical Features The early manifestations of vitamin C deficiency are irritability, loss of appetite, low-grade fever, musculoskeletal pain, and tenderness in the legs.

</details>
