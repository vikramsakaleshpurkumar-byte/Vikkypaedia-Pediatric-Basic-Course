### Card 6 (Core Pediatric Concept)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In the pediatric evaluation of **The High-Risk Infant** (*The High-Risk Infant*), how is **highest risk** characterized?</summary>

**Answer:**
highest risk of neonatal and infant mortality occurs in infants with birthweight <1,000 g and/or with gestational age <28 weeks.

</details>

### Card 7 (Core Pediatric Concept)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In the pediatric evaluation of **The High-Risk Infant** (*The High-Risk Infant*), how is **lowest risk** characterized?</summary>

**Answer:**
lowest risk of neonatal mortality occurs in infants with birthweight of 3,000- 4,000 g and a gestational age of 39-41 weeks.

</details>
