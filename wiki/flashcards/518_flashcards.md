### Card 8 (Core Pediatric Concept)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In the pediatric evaluation of **Acquired Pancytopenias** (*Acquired Pancytopenias*), how is **Severe aplastic anemia** characterized?</summary>

**Answer:**
Severe aplastic anemia is defined as a condition in which ≥2 cell components have become seriously compromised (absolute neutrophil count <500/mm3, platelet count <20,000/ mm3, reticulocyte count <1% after correction for hematocrit) in a patient whose bone marrow biopsy material has <30% cellular ity.

</details>
