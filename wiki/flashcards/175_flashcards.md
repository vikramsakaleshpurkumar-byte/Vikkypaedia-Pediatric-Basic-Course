### Card 7 (Core Pediatric Concept)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In the pediatric evaluation of **Defects of Innate Immunity** (*175.1 Predisposition to Fungal Infections Jenna R.E. Bergerson and Alexandra F. Freeman*), how is **Chronic mucocutaneous candidiasis** characterized?</summary>

**Answer:**
Chronic mucocutaneous candidiasis is characterized by severe, persistent, or recurrent symptomatic infection of the nails, skin, oral, or genital mucosa by the Candida genus.

</details>
