### Card 1 (Syndrome & Clinical Profile)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> What are the hallmark clinical features and diagnostic profile of **Todani Type I Choledochal Cyst (80–90%)**?</summary>

**Answer:**
**Category / Inheritance:** The Digestive System **Clinical Synthesis:** Fusiform/cystic dilatation of extrahepatic CBD; associated with anomalous pancreaticobiliary junction; treat with complete cyst excision + Roux-en-Y hepaticojejunostomy

</details>

### Card 2 (Syndrome & Clinical Profile)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> What are the hallmark clinical features and diagnostic profile of **Caroli Disease (Todani Type V) vs Caroli Syndrome**?</summary>

**Answer:**
**Associated Gene(s):** `PKHD1` · **Category / Inheritance:** The Digestive System **Clinical Synthesis:** Caroli disease: isolated saccular intrahepatic duct dilatation ('central dot sign'); Caroli syndrome: intrahepatic ectasia + congenital hepatic fibrosis + ARPKD (PKHD1)

</details>

### Card 3 (Syndrome & Clinical Profile)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> What are the hallmark clinical features and diagnostic profile of **Congenital Hepatic Fibrosis (CHF)**?</summary>

**Answer:**
**Category / Inheritance:** The Digestive System **Clinical Synthesis:** Ductal plate malformation causing presinusoidal portal hypertension (splenomegaly, esophageal varices) with normal hepatocellular synthetic function

</details>

### Card 4 (Pediatric Genetics & Pathogenesis)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In **Cystic Diseases of the Biliary Tract and Liver**, what clinical phenotype or molecular mechanism is associated with **`PKHD1`**?</summary>

**Answer:**
When Type V intrahepatic biliary ectasia is combined with congenital hepatic fibrosis (CHF) and autosomal recessive polycystic kidney disease (ARPKD, PKHD1 [263200]), it is termed Caroli syndrome.

</details>

### Card 6 (Core Pediatric Concept)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In the pediatric evaluation of **Cystic Diseases of the Biliary Tract and Liver** (*Cystic Diseases of the Biliary Tract and Liver*), how is **ductal plate malformations** characterized?</summary>

**Answer:**
ductal plate malformations of the primary cilium (congenital hepatic fibrosis, Caroli disease, Caroli syndrome, and polycystic liver disease).

</details>

### Card 7 (Core Pediatric Concept)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In the pediatric evaluation of **Cystic Diseases of the Biliary Tract and Liver** (*Cystic Diseases of the Biliary Tract and Liver*), how is **Type III** characterized?</summary>

**Answer:**
Type III (choledochocele within the duodenal wall), Type IV (multiple extrahepatic and intrahepatic cysts [IVA] or multiple extrahepatic cysts [IVB]), and Type V (Caroli disease): multifocal segmental saccular ectasia of the large intrahepatic bile ducts.

</details>
