### Card 7 (Core Pediatric Concept)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In the pediatric evaluation of **Developmental and Behavioral Theories** (*Developmental and Behavioral Theories*), how is **Attachment** characterized?</summary>

**Answer:**
Attachment refers to a biologically determined tendency of a young child to seek proximity to the parent during times of stress and to the relationship that allows securely attached children to use their parents to reestablish a sense of well-being after a stressful experience.

</details>
