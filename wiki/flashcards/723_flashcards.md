### Card 2 (Syndrome & Clinical Profile)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> What are the hallmark clinical features and diagnostic profile of **DISTAL ARTHROGRYPOSIS SYNDROMES**?</summary>

**Answer:**
**Category / Inheritance:** Bone and Joint Disorders **Clinical Synthesis:** • Type I dominant distal; • Type IIa dominant distal (Freeman-Sheldon syndrome); • Digitotalar dysmorphism; • Trismus pseudocamptodactyly; • Distal distribution, type not specified

</details>

### Card 3 (Syndrome & Clinical Profile)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> What are the hallmark clinical features and diagnostic profile of **PTERYGIUM SYNDROMES**?</summary>

**Answer:**
**Category / Inheritance:** Bone and Joint Disorders **Clinical Synthesis:** • Multiple pterygium syndrome; • Lethal multiple pterygium syndrome; • Popliteal pterygium syndrome; • Ptosis, scoliosis, pterygia; • Antecubital webbing syndrome (Liebenberg)

</details>
