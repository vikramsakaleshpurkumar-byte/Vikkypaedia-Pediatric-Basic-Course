**Answer:**

### Card 6 (Core Pediatric Concept)

**Answer:**
PATHOGENESIS The biochemical features of Bartter syndrome, such as hypokalemic hypochloremic metabolic alkalosis with hypercalciuria, resemble those seen with chronic use of loop diuretics and reflect a defect in sodium, chloride, and potassium transport in the ascending loop of Henle.
