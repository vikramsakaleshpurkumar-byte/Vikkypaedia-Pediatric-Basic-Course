### Card 2 (Pediatric Genetics & Pathogenesis)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In **Disorders of the Mucous Membranes**, what clinical phenotype or molecular mechanism is associated with **`PTEN`**?</summary>

**Answer:**
PTEN HAMARTOMA SYNDROME (COWDEN SYNDROME) PTEN hamartoma syndrome is an autosomal dominant condition caused by loss-of-function pathogenic variants in the PTEN tumorsuppressor gene.

</details>

### Card 3 (Pediatric Therapeutics & Management)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> What is the clinical management or therapeutic approach for **Disorders of the Mucous Membranes**?</summary>

**Answer:**
**Therapy / Protocol:** Metronidazole **Clinical Application:** Care consists of nutritional sup port, conservative debridement of necrotic soft tissues, empirical broad-spectrum antibiotics such as penicillin and metronidazole, and, in the case of noma neonatorum, antipseudomonal antibiotics.

</details>
