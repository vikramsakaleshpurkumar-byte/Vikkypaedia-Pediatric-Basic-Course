### Card 3 (Pediatric Genetics & Pathogenesis)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In **Coccidioidomycosis (Coccidioides Species)**, what clinical phenotype or molecular mechanism is associated with **`STAT1`**?</summary>

**Answer:**
Children with congenital primary immunodeficiency disorders may be at increased risk for infection; these disorders include interleukin- 12Rβ1 deficiency, interferon-γR1 deficiency, and STAT1 gain-offunction mutations.

</details>

### Card 4 (Pediatric Therapeutics & Management)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> What is the clinical management or therapeutic approach for **Coccidioidomycosis (Coccidioides Species)**?</summary>

**Answer:**
**Therapy / Protocol:** Amphotericin **Clinical Application:** Newborns with blastomycosis should be treated with amphotericin B deoxycholate 1 mg/kg/day.

</details>

### Card 7 (Core Pediatric Concept)

**Answer:**
