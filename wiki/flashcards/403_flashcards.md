### Card 1 (Syndrome & Clinical Profile)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> What are the hallmark clinical features and diagnostic profile of **Hepatocellular Injury Pattern**?</summary>

**Answer:**
**Category / Inheritance:** The Digestive System **Clinical Synthesis:** Predominant ALT and AST elevation; check viral hepatitis panel, acetaminophen level, ceruloplasmin (Wilson disease), autoimmune markers (ANA, SMA, LKM-1), CK (rule out myopathy)

</details>

### Card 2 (Syndrome & Clinical Profile)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> What are the hallmark clinical features and diagnostic profile of **High-GGT Cholestasis**?</summary>

**Answer:**
**Associated Gene(s):** `ABCB4, JAG1` · **Category / Inheritance:** The Digestive System **Clinical Synthesis:** Conjugated bilirubin >1.0 mg/dL + elevated GGT (>100–300 U/L); biliary atresia, Alagille syndrome (JAG1), PFIC3 (ABCB4), alpha-1 antitrypsin deficiency, choledochal cyst

</details>

### Card 3 (Syndrome & Clinical Profile)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> What are the hallmark clinical features and diagnostic profile of **Low/Normal-GGT Cholestasis**?</summary>

**Answer:**
**Associated Gene(s):** `ABCB11, TJP2, ATP8B1` · **Category / Inheritance:** The Digestive System **Clinical Synthesis:** Conjugated bilirubin >1.0 mg/dL + GGT <100 U/L; PFIC1 (ATP8B1), PFIC2 (ABCB11), PFIC4 (TJP2), bile acid synthesis defects (HSD3B7, AKR1D1), panhypopituitarism

</details>
