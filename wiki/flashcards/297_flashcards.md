### Card 5 (Core Pediatric Concept)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In the pediatric evaluation of **Nonpolio Enteroviruses** (*Nonpolio Enteroviruses*), how is **ETIOLOGY** characterized?</summary>

**Answer:**
ETIOLOGY Enteroviruses are nonenveloped, single-stranded, positive-sense viruses in the Picornaviridae (small RNA virus) family, which also includes the rhinoviruses, hepatitis A virus, and parechoviruses.

</details>
