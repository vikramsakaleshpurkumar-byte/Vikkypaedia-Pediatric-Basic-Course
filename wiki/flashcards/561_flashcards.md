### Card 6 (Core Pediatric Concept)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In the pediatric evaluation of **Tubulointerstitial Disease Associated with Hematuria** (*561.2 Tubulointerstitial Nephritis Prasad Devarajan*), how is **Acute TIN** characterized?</summary>

**Answer:**
Acute TIN is charac terized by an acute extensive lymphocytic inflammatory response and a rapid decline in renal function.

### Card 7 (Core Pediatric Concept)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In the pediatric evaluation of **Tubulointerstitial Disease Associated with Hematuria** (*561.2 Tubulointerstitial Nephritis Prasad Devarajan*), how is **Chronic TIN** characterized?</summary>

**Answer:**
Chronic TIN usually displays a pro tracted onset and a chronic patchy lymphocytic infiltrate, interstitial fibrosis, and a slow deterioration in renal function.

</details>

### Card 8 (Core Pediatric Concept)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In the pediatric evaluation of **Tubulointerstitial Disease Associated with Hematuria** (*561.2 Tubulointerstitial Nephritis Prasad Devarajan*), how is **ACUTE TUBULOINTERSTITIAL NEPHRITIS** characterized?</summary>

**Answer:**
ACUTE TUBULOINTERSTITIAL NEPHRITIS The hallmarks of acute TIN are an extensive lymphocytic infiltration of the tubulointerstitium, interstitial edema, and varying degrees of tubu lar necrosis and regeneration.

</details>
