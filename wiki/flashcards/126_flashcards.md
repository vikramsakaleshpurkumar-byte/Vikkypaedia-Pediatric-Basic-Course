### Card 4 (Pediatric Genetics & Pathogenesis)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In **Respiratory Distress Syndrome (Hyaline Membrane Disease)**, what clinical phenotype or molecular mechanism is associated with **`ABCA3`**?</summary>

**Answer:**
SP-B and SP-C are trafficked from the endoplasmic reticulum (ER) to lamellar bodies via the Golgi complex and multivesicular body (MVB); in contrast, surfactant phospholipids are likely directly transported from the ER to specific lipid importers (ABCA3) in the lamellar body–limiting membrane.

</details>
