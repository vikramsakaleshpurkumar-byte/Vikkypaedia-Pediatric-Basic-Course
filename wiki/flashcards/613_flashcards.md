### Card 8 (Core Pediatric Concept)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In the pediatric evaluation of **Hyperparathyroidism** (*Hyperparathyroidism*), how is **Acroscyphodysplasia** characterized?</summary>

**Answer:**
Acroscyphodysplasia is a distinctive form of metaphyseal dysplasia characterized by the distal femoral and proximal tibial epiphyses embedded in cup-shaped, large metaphyses known as metaphyseal scypho or cup deformity and is a phenotypic variation of PHP and acrodysostosis.

</details>
