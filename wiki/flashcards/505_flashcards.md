**Answer:**
The most common type of nonsyndromic CSA is an X-linked disorder and is most often a result of pathogenic variants in ALAS2 that encodes erythrocytic isozyme 5-aminolevulinic acid synthetase, the rate-limiting enzyme reaction in heme synthesis.
