### Card 1 (Syndrome & Clinical Profile)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> What are the hallmark clinical features and diagnostic profile of **COMMON (>50%) (Human Metapneumovirus)**?</summary>

**Answer:**
**Category / Inheritance:** Infectious Diseases **Clinical Synthesis:** Fever >38°C (100.4°F); Cough; Rhinitis, coryza; Wheezing; Tachypnea, retractions; Hypoxia (O2 saturation <94%); Chest radiograph demonstration of infiltrates or hyperinflation

**Answer:**
ETIOLOGY HMPV is an enveloped, single-stranded, nonsegmented, negativesense RNA genome of the family Pneumoviridae, which comprises large enveloped negative-sense RNA viruses.
