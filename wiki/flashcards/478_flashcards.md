**Answer:**
CARDIAC DISEASE LEADING TO CYANOSIS CHD produces cyanosis when obstruction to right ventricular inflow or outflow causes intracardiac right-to-left shunting or when complex anatomic defects cause an admixture of pulmonary (deoxygenated) and systemic (oxygenated) venous return somewhere in the heart.
