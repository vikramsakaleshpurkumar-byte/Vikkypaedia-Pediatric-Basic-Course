### Card 7 (Core Pediatric Concept)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In the pediatric evaluation of **Diabetes Mellitus** (*629.1 Classification of Diabetes Mellitus David R. Weber*), how is **type 2 diabetes mellitus (T2DM)** characterized?</summary>

**Answer:**
type 2 diabetes mellitus (T2DM) is a consequence of insulin resistance occurring at the level of skeletal muscle, liver, and adipose tissue, with various degrees of β-cell impairment.

</details>

### Card 8 (Core Pediatric Concept)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In the pediatric evaluation of **Diabetes Mellitus** (*629.1 Classification of Diabetes Mellitus David R. Weber*), how is **TYPE 2 DIABETES MELLITUS** characterized?</summary>

**Answer:**
TYPE 2 DIABETES MELLITUS Formerly known as adult-onset diabetes mellitus or non–insulin- dependent diabetes mellitus, T2DM develops because of insulin resistance and progressive non-autoimmune β-cell failure.

</details>
