### Card 1 (Syndrome & Clinical Profile)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> What are the hallmark clinical features and diagnostic profile of **Hepatitis A Virus (HAV)**?</summary>

**Answer:**
**Category / Inheritance:** The Digestive System **Clinical Synthesis:** Fecal-oral Picornavirus; anti-HAV IgM diagnostic; 0% chronicity; prevent with universal 2-dose HAV vaccine starting at 12–23 months

</details>

### Card 2 (Syndrome & Clinical Profile)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> What are the hallmark clinical features and diagnostic profile of **Hepatitis B Virus (HBV) Perinatal Prophylaxis**?</summary>

**Answer:**
**Category / Inheritance:** The Digestive System **Clinical Synthesis:** 90% neonatal chronicity without prophylaxis; give HBV vaccine + HBIG 0.5 mL IM within 12 hr of birth for HBsAg+ mothers; entecavir/tenofovir for chronic active HBV

</details>

### Card 3 (Syndrome & Clinical Profile)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> What are the hallmark clinical features and diagnostic profile of **Hepatitis C Virus (HCV) in Children**?</summary>

**Answer:**
**Category / Inheritance:** The Digestive System **Clinical Synthesis:** Vertical transmission ~5%; check HCV RNA PCR at 2–6 mo or anti-HCV ≥18 mo; cure at ≥3 yr with 8–12 wk pan-genotypic DAAs (glecaprevir/pibrentasvir or sofosbuvir/velpatasvir)

</details>

### Card 4 (Pediatric Therapeutics & Management)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> What is the clinical management or therapeutic approach for **Viral Hepatitis**?</summary>

**Answer:**
**Therapy / Protocol:** Pediatric Clinical Management **Clinical Application:** HBV (Hepadnaviridae, partially double-stranded DNA virus) transmission in pediatrics is predominantly perinatal/vertical from HBsAg+/HBeAg+ mothers; chronicity is inverse to age (90% chronicity in neonates, 25–30% in children 1–5 years, <5% in adolescents/adults).

</details>

### Card 6 (Core Pediatric Concept)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In the pediatric evaluation of **Viral Hepatitis** (*Viral Hepatitis*), how is **Gianotti-Crosti syndrome** characterized?</summary>

**Answer:**

### Card 8 (High-Yield Board Pearl)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> What is a high-yield clinical or diagnostic principle regarding **Viral Hepatitis** (*Viral Hepatitis*)?</summary>

**Answer:**
HBV serology distinguishes acute infection (HBsAg+, anti-HBc IgM+), chronic infection (HBsAg+ >6 months, anti-HBc IgG+), immune-tolerant phase (HBeAg+, HBV DNA >10^7 IU/mL, normal ALT), immune-active phase (elevated ALT, elevated HBV DNA), window period (anti-HBc IgM+ only), and vaccine immunity (anti-HBs+ only).

</details>
