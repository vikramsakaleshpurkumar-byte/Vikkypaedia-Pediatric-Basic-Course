### Card 1 (Syndrome & Clinical Profile)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> What are the hallmark clinical features and diagnostic profile of **Schistosomiasis (Schistosoma)**?</summary>

**Answer:**
**Category / Inheritance:** Infectious Diseases **Clinical Synthesis:** Intestinal as well as hepatic granulomas underlie the pathologic sequelae of the other schistosome infections: ulcerations and fibrosis of intestinal wall, hepatosplenomegaly, and portal hypertension caused by presinusoidal obstruction of blood flow.

### Card 2 (Pediatric Therapeutics & Management)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> What is the clinical management or therapeutic approach for **Schistosomiasis (Schistosoma)**?</summary>

**Answer:**
**Therapy / Protocol:** Pediatric Clinical Management **Clinical Application:** Five schistosome species are known to infect humans: Schistosoma haematobium, S. mansoni, S. japonicum, S. intercalatum, and S. mekongi.
