**Answer:**
TREATMENT Most cases of actinomycosis can be managed with antibiotics, although surgery can be adjunctive and may shorten the duration of antibiotic use.

### Card 4 (Core Pediatric Concept)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In the pediatric evaluation of **Nocardia** (*Nocardia*), how is **PROGNOSIS** characterized?</summary>

**Answer:**
PROGNOSIS The prognosis is excellent with early diagnosis, prompt initiation of antibiotic therapy, adherence to a prolonged course of antibiotics, and adequate surgical debridement, if necessary.

</details>
