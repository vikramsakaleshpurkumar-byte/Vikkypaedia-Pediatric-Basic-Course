### Card 6 (Core Pediatric Concept)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In the pediatric evaluation of **Epstein-Barr Virus** (*Epstein-Barr Virus*), how is **ETIOLOGY** characterized?</summary>

**Answer:**
ETIOLOGY EBV is a double-stranded DNA virus that is a member of the gamma herpesviruses and causes >90% of cases of infectious mononucleosis.
