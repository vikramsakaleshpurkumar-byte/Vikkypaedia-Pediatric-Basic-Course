### Card 4 (Pediatric Therapeutics & Management)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> What is the clinical management or therapeutic approach for **Ingestions (373.1 Foreign Bodies in the Esophagus)**?</summary>

**Answer:**
**Therapy / Protocol:** Mg/kg **Clinical Application:** In patients without prior esophageal surgeries, glucagon (0.05 mg/kg intravenously; maximum pediatric dose, 0.5 mg; adult dose, 1-2 mg) can sometimes be useful in facilitating passage of distal esopha geal food boluses by decreasing the lower esophageal sphincter pressure.

</details>

### Card 6 (Core Pediatric Concept)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In the pediatric evaluation of **Ingestions** (*373.2 Caustic Ingestions Seema Khan*), how is **Acidic agents** characterized?</summary>

**Answer:**
