### Card 8 (Core Pediatric Concept)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In the pediatric evaluation of **Obstruction of the Urinary Tract** (*Obstruction of the Urinary Tract*), how is **Chronic ureteral obstruction** characterized?</summary>

**Answer:**
Chronic ureteral obstruction in late fetal life or after birth results in dilation of the ureter, renal pelvis, and calyces with alterations of renal parenchyma ranging from minimal tubular changes to dilation of Bowman’s space, glomerular fibrosis, and interstitial fibrosis.

</details>
