### Card 2 (Syndrome & Clinical Profile)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> What are the hallmark clinical features and diagnostic profile of **Neonatal PALF (<28 Days) Must-Not-Miss**?</summary>

**Answer:**
**Category / Inheritance:** The Digestive System **Clinical Synthesis:** HSV (empiric IV acyclovir 20 mg/kg q8h!), GALD (IVIG + exchange transfusion), Galactosemia (soy formula), Tyrosinemia type 1 (urine succinylacetone → nitisinone)

</details>
