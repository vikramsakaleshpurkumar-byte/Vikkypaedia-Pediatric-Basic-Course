### Card 1 (Syndrome & Clinical Profile)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> What are the hallmark clinical features and diagnostic profile of **Acetaminophen (Intrinsic Zone 3 Necrosis)**?</summary>

**Answer:**
**Associated Gene(s):** `CYP2E1` · **Category / Inheritance:** The Digestive System **Clinical Synthesis:** CYP2E1 → NAPQI depletes glutathione; AST/ALT >3,000 U/L; Rumack-Matthew nomogram at ≥4 hr; antidote: N-acetylcysteine (NAC)

</details>

### Card 2 (Syndrome & Clinical Profile)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> What are the hallmark clinical features and diagnostic profile of **Valproic Acid (Mitochondrial Microvesicular Steatosis)**?</summary>

**Answer:**
**Associated Gene(s):** `POLG, CPS1` · **Category / Inheritance:** mtDNA **Clinical Synthesis:** Inhibits mitochondrial β-oxidation & CPS1; highest risk <2 yr or with POLG variants; antidote: IV L-carnitine (100 mg/kg/day)

</details>

### Card 3 (Syndrome & Clinical Profile)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> What are the hallmark clinical features and diagnostic profile of **Lamotrigine / Carbamazepine / Phenytoin (DRESS)**?</summary>

**Answer:**
**Category / Inheritance:** The Digestive System **Clinical Synthesis:** Aromatic anticonvulsant hypersensitivity 2–8 wk after start: fever, eosinophilia, facial edema, hepatitis; stop drug + systemic corticosteroids

</details>

### Card 6 (Core Pediatric Concept)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In the pediatric evaluation of **Drug- and Toxin-Induced Liver Injury** (*Drug- and Toxin-Induced Liver Injury*), how is **Drug- and toxin-induced liver injury (DILI)** characterized?</summary>

**Answer:**
Drug- and toxin-induced liver injury (DILI) in children ranges from asymptomatic transaminase elevations to pediatric acute liver failure (PALF) (~15–20% of all PALF cases).

</details>

### Card 7 (Core Pediatric Concept)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In the pediatric evaluation of **Drug- and Toxin-Induced Liver Injury** (*Drug- and Toxin-Induced Liver Injury*), how is **CYP2E1** characterized?</summary>

**Answer:**
CYP2E1 to the hepatotoxic electrophile NAPQI, which depletes glutathione and causes centrilobular (Zone 3) coagulative necrosis with extreme AST/ALT elevations (often >3,000–10,000 U/L), mild hyperbilirubinemia initially, and severe coagulopathy.

</details>

<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In the pediatric evaluation of **Drug- and Toxin-Induced Liver Injury** (*Drug- and Toxin-Induced Liver Injury*), how is **antineoplastics** characterized?</summary>

**Answer:**
antineoplastics (6-mercaptopurine, methotrexate, L-asparaginase, busulfan/gemtuzumab [causing sinusoidal obstruction syndrome / veno-occlusive disease (SOS/VOD) with tender hepatomegaly, weight gain, ascites, and jaundice]), and amatoxin (Amanita phalloides mushroom poisoning).
