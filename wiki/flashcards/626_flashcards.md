### Card 4 (Pediatric Genetics & Pathogenesis)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In **Hypofunction of the Ovaries**, what clinical phenotype or molecular mechanism is associated with **`SHOX`**?</summary>

**Answer:**
SHOX, a homeobox-containing gene of 170 kb of DNA within the PAR1, is thought to be important for controlling growth in children with Turner syndrome, with Leri-Weill syndrome, and, rarely, patients with idiopathic short stature.
