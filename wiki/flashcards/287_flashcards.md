### Card 2 (Pediatric Therapeutics & Management)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> What is the clinical management or therapeutic approach for **Paracoccidioides brasiliensis**?</summary>

**Answer:**
**Therapy / Protocol:** Fluconazole **Clinical Application:** After completion of therapy, patients should be evaluated yearly for at least 2 years because some patients relapse (28% of adults who completed 12 months of fluconazole, 18% for itraconazole).
