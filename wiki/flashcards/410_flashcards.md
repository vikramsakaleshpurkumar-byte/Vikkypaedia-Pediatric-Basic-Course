### Card 1 (Syndrome & Clinical Profile)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> What are the hallmark clinical features and diagnostic profile of **Autoimmune Hepatitis Type 1 (AIH-1)**?</summary>

**Answer:**
**Category / Inheritance:** The Digestive System **Clinical Synthesis:** 80% of pediatric AIH; older children/adolescents; ANA+ and/or anti-smooth muscle antibody (ASMA / anti-actin)+; associated with HLA-DRB10301 / 0401

</details>

### Card 2 (Syndrome & Clinical Profile)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> What are the hallmark clinical features and diagnostic profile of **Autoimmune Hepatitis Type 2 (AIH-2)**?</summary>

**Answer:**
**Associated Gene(s):** `AIRE` · **Category / Inheritance:** The Digestive System **Clinical Synthesis:** Younger children/infants; anti-LKM-1+ (anti-CYP2D6) and/or anti-LC-1+; higher risk of acute liver failure; associated with autoimmune polyendocrinopathy (APECED / AIRE)

</details>

### Card 3 (Syndrome & Clinical Profile)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> What are the hallmark clinical features and diagnostic profile of **Autoimmune Sclerosing Cholangitis (ASC)**?</summary>

**Answer:**
**Category / Inheritance:** The Digestive System **Clinical Synthesis:** Overlap of AIH + bile duct sclerosis on MRCP in 20–30% of pediatric cases; strongly linked to ulcerative colitis; add ursodeoxycholic acid (UDCA) to immunosuppression

</details>

### Card 5 (Pediatric Therapeutics & Management)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> What is the clinical management or therapeutic approach for **Standard Immunosuppressive Regimen**?</summary>

**Answer:**
**Therapy / Protocol:** Key Clinical, Diagnostic, and Therapeutic Summary for Autoimmune Hepatitis **Clinical Application:** Prednisone 1–2 mg/kg/day (max 60 mg) + Azathioprine 1–2 mg/kg/day (verify normal TPMT/NUDT15 first); liver biopsy showing interface plasma-cell hepatitis required

</details>

### Card 6 (Core Pediatric Concept)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In the pediatric evaluation of **Autoimmune Hepatitis** (*Autoimmune Hepatitis*), how is **interface hepatitis** characterized?</summary>

**Answer:**
interface hepatitis on liver histology, and a dramatic response to immunosuppressive therapy.

</details>

### Card 7 (Core Pediatric Concept)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In the pediatric evaluation of **Autoimmune Hepatitis** (*Autoimmune Hepatitis*), how is **percutaneous liver biopsy** characterized?</summary>

**Answer:**
percutaneous liver biopsy showing interface hepatitis (piece-meal necrosis) with a dense portal lymphoplasmacytic infiltrate, emperipolesis, and hepatocyte rosettes, and (4) MRCP in all pediatric AIH patients to detect concomitant autoimmune sclerosing cholangitis (ASC).

</details>

### Card 8 (Core Pediatric Concept)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In the pediatric evaluation of **Autoimmune Hepatitis** (*Autoimmune Hepatitis*), how is **azathioprine (1–2 mg/kg/day)** characterized?</summary>

**Answer:**
azathioprine (1–2 mg/kg/day) as a steroid-sparing maintenance agent (check TPMT and NUDT15 genotype/activity before starting azathioprine to prevent life-threatening myelosuppression; delay azathioprine until jaundice resolves in severely icteric patients).

</details>
