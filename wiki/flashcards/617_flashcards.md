### Card 4 (Pediatric Genetics & Pathogenesis)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In **Adrenocortical Tumors and Masses**, what clinical phenotype or molecular mechanism is associated with **`KCNQ1OT1`**?</summary>

**Answer:**
Overexpression of insulin-like growth factor (IGF) 2 (encoded by IGF2, on chromosome 11p15.5) occurs in 80% of sporadic childhood adrenocortical tumors and in those associated with Beckwith- Wiedemann syndrome, in which there is loss of the normal imprinting of genes in this chromosomal region, including H19, CDKN1C, KCNQ1, and KCNQ1OT1.

</details>

### Card 7 (Core Pediatric Concept)
