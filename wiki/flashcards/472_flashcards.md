**Answer:**
maximal chest width is obtained by drawing a horizontal line between the right and left inner borders of the rib cage at the level of the top of the right diaphragm.

### Card 7 (Core Pediatric Concept)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In the pediatric evaluation of **Laboratory Cardiac Evaluation** (*472.1 Radiologic Cardiac Assessment Daniel Bernstein*), how is **cardiothoracic ratio** characterized?</summary>

**Answer:**
cardiothoracic ratio is a less useful index of cardiac enlarge ment in infants than in older children because the horizontal position of the heart may increase the ratio to about 60% in the absence of true enlargement.

</details>
