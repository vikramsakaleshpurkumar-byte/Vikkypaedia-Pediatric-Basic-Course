### Card 3 (Pediatric Genetics & Pathogenesis)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In **Chromatin Regulatory Disorders**, what clinical phenotype or molecular mechanism is associated with **`KMT2D`**?</summary>

**Answer:**
Molecular Etiology Heterozygous KMT2D pathogenic variants (∼90%), and hemizygous (in males) or heterozygous (in females) KDM6A pathogenic variants (∼10%) cause classic Kabuki syndrome; KMT2D encodes a histone methyltransferase and KDM6A encodes a histone demethylase.

</details>
