### Card 4 (Pediatric Genetics & Pathogenesis)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In **Escherichia coli**, what clinical phenotype or molecular mechanism is associated with **`EAST1`**?</summary>

**Answer:**
Some strains produce toxins, including the plasmid-encoded enterotoxin EAST1 (encoded by astA), a homolog of the ETEC ST; an autotransporter toxin called Pet; other STATE toxins; and the chromo somally encoded enterotoxin ShET1 (encoded by setA and setB).

</details>
