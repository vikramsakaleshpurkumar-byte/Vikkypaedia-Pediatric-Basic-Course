### Card 4 (Pediatric Genetics & Pathogenesis)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In **Guillain-Barré Syndrome**, what clinical phenotype or molecular mechanism is associated with **`GM1`**?</summary>

**Answer:**
Serum antiganglioside antibodies against GM1 and GD1 are sometimes elevated in GBS, par ticularly in cases with primarily axonal rather than demyelinating neu ropathy, suggesting that they might play a role in disease propagation and/or recovery in some cases.

</details>

### Card 8 (Core Pediatric Concept)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In the pediatric evaluation of **Guillain-Barré Syndrome** (*Guillain-Barré Syndrome*), how is **Miller Fisher syndrome (MFS)** characterized?</summary>

**Answer:**
Miller Fisher syndrome (MFS) is an uncommon GBS variant associ ated with acute external (and occasionally internal) ophthalmoplegia, ataxia, and areflexia.
