### Card 2 (Syndrome & Clinical Profile)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> What are the hallmark clinical features and diagnostic profile of **Central Airway Abnormalities**?</summary>

**Answer:**
**Category / Inheritance:** The Respiratory System **Clinical Synthesis:** Malacia of the larynx, trachea, and/or bronchi; Laryngeal or tracheal web; Tracheoesophageal fistula (specifically H-type fistula); Laryngeal cleft (resulting in aspiration)

</details>

### Card 3 (Syndrome & Clinical Profile)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> What are the hallmark clinical features and diagnostic profile of **Extrinsic Airway Anomalies Resulting in Airway Compression**?</summary>

**Answer:**
**Category / Inheritance:** The Respiratory System **Clinical Synthesis:** Vascular ring or sling; Mediastinal lymphadenopathy from infection or tumor; Mediastinal mass or tumor; Esophageal foreign body

</details>
