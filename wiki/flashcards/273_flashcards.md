### Card 2 (Pediatric Therapeutics & Management)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> What is the clinical management or therapeutic approach for **Psittacosis (Chlamydia psittaci)**?</summary>

**Answer:**
**Therapy / Protocol:** Azithromycin **Clinical Application:** For treatment of C. tracho matis infection in pregnant women, the CDC currently recommends either azithromycin (1 g PO as a single dose) or amoxicillin (500 mg PO 3 times a day for 7 days) as first-line regimens.

</details>

### Card 3 (Pediatric Therapeutics & Management)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> What is the clinical management or therapeutic approach for **Psittacosis (Chlamydia psittaci) (272.4 Lymphogranuloma Venereum Margaret R. Hammerschlag)**?</summary>

**Answer:**
**Therapy / Protocol:** Doxycycline **Clinical Application:** Trying to ascertain the C. trachomatis serovar for confirmation of LGV has therapeutic implications, as LGV needs to be treated with a 3-week course of doxycycline; a single-dose of azithromycin will not eradicate the infection.

</details>
