### Card 2 (Pediatric Genetics & Pathogenesis)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In **Disorders of the Exocrine Pancreas**, what clinical phenotype or molecular mechanism is associated with **`SBDS`**?</summary>

**Answer:**
SDS is an autosomal recessive syndrome (1/20,000 births) caused by a pathogenic variant of the Shwachman-Bodian-Diamond syndrome (SBDS) gene on chromosome 7, which causes ribosomal dysfunction in 90–95% of patients.

</details>
