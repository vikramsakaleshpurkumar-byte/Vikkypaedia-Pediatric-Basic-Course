### Card 4 (Pediatric Genetics & Pathogenesis)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In **Cardiac Development**, what clinical phenotype or molecular mechanism is associated with **`ISL1`**?</summary>

**Answer:**
There are two distinct cell lineages: the first heart field (regulated mainly by NKX2.5) provides precursor cells for the left ventricle and parts of the atria; the second heart field (regulated mainly by ISL1) provides precursors for the right ventricle, outflow tracts, and caudal aspects of the atria.

</details>
