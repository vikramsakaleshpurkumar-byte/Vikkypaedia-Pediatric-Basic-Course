### Card 4 (Pediatric Genetics & Pathogenesis)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In **Developmental Disorders of Muscle**, what clinical phenotype or molecular mechanism is associated with **`TPM3`**?</summary>

**Answer:**
Pathogenic variants of the TPM3 gene are one of the well-documented etiologies of nemaline myopathy, but identical genetic variants of this gene are also shown to be capable of causing isolated congenital fiber–type disproportion without nema line rods, cap myopathy, centronuclear (“myotubular”) myopathy, and central core/minicore disease.

</details>
