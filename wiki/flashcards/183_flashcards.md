### Card 4 (Pediatric Therapeutics & Management)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> What is the clinical management or therapeutic approach for **Diagnosis of Allergic Disease**?</summary>

**Answer:**
**Therapy / Protocol:** Epinephrine **Clinical Application:** Medications with antihistaminic properties, in addition to adrenergic agents such as ephedrine and epinephrine, suppress skin test responses and should be avoided for appropriate intervals (approximately five half-lives) before skin testing.

</details>

### Card 7 (Core Pediatric Concept)
