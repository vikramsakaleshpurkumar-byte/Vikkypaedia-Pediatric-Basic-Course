### Card 5 (Pediatric Genetics & Pathogenesis)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In **Infections in Immunocompromised Persons**, what clinical phenotype or molecular mechanism is associated with **`STAT3`**?</summary>

**Answer:**
Hyper-IgE syndrome (HIES) is characterized by elevated levels of IgE, infections, and eczema, with the most common cause being the result of dominant negative gene variation in STAT3.

</details>

### Card 6 (Pediatric Therapeutics & Management)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> What is the clinical management or therapeutic approach for **Infections in Immunocompromised Persons**?</summary>

**Answer:**
**Therapy / Protocol:** Trimethoprim **Clinical Application:** Prophylaxis with trimethoprim-sulfamethoxazole (TMP-SMX), recombinant human interferon-γ, and oral antifungal agents with activity against Aspergillus spp., such as itraconazole or newer azoles, substantially reduces the incidence of severe infections.

</details>
