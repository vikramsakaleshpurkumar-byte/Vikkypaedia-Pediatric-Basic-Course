<details><summary><b>Q:</b> What are the hallmark clinical features and diagnostic profile of **Anomalies of the Bladder**?</summary>

**Answer:**
**Category / Inheritance:** Urologic Disorders in Infant **Clinical Synthesis:** The diagnosis is made by a retrograde urethrogram, in which contrast is injected toward the bladder through a catheter inserted into the distal urethra.

### Card 3 (Core Pediatric Concept)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In the pediatric evaluation of **Anomalies of the Bladder** (*Anomalies of the Bladder*), how is **Urethral Hypoplasia** characterized?</summary>

**Answer:**
Urethral Hypoplasia Urethral hypoplasia is a rare form of obstructive uropathy in males that is less severe than urethral atresia.

### Card 4 (Core Pediatric Concept)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In the pediatric evaluation of **Anomalies of the Bladder** (*Anomalies of the Bladder*), how is **Urethral Stricture** characterized?</summary>

**Answer:**
Urethral Stricture Urethral strictures in males usually result from urethral trauma, either iatrogenic (catheterization, endoscopic procedures, previous urethral reconstruction) or accidental (straddle injuries, pelvic fractures).

</details>

### Card 5 (Core Pediatric Concept)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In the pediatric evaluation of **Anomalies of the Bladder** (*Anomalies of the Bladder*), how is **BLADDER EXSTROPHY** characterized?</summary>

**Answer:**
BLADDER EXSTROPHY Exstrophy of the urinary bladder occurs in approximately 1 in 35,000- 40,000 births with a male:female ratio of 2:1.

</details>
