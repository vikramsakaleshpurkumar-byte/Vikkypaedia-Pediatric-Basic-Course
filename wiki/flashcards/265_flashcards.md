### Card 6 (Core Pediatric Concept)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In the pediatric evaluation of **Nonvenereal Treponemal Infections** (*265.1 Yaws (Treponema pertenue) Stephen K. Obaro and H. Dele Davies*), how is **secondary-stage** characterized?</summary>

**Answer:**
secondary-stage lesions can erupt anywhere on the body before or after the healing of the mother yaw and may be accompanied by lymphadenopathy, anorexia, and malaise.

</details>

### Card 7 (Core Pediatric Concept)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In the pediatric evaluation of **Nonvenereal Treponemal Infections** (*265.2 Bejel (Endemic Syphilis; Treponema pallidum endemicum)*), how is **secondary stage** characterized?</summary>

**Answer:**

### Card 8 (Core Pediatric Concept)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In the pediatric evaluation of **Nonvenereal Treponemal Infections** (*265.3 Pinta (Treponema carateum) Stephen K. Obaro and H. Dele Davies*), how is **pri mary** characterized?</summary>

**Answer:**
