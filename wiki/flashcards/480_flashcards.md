### Card 6 (Pediatric Therapeutics & Management)

**Answer:**
**Therapy / Protocol:** Prostaglandin **Clinical Application:** TREATMENT When transposition is suspected, an infusion of prostaglandin E1 (PGE1, 0.05-0.1 μg/kg/min) should be initiated immediately to maintain patency of the ductus arteriosus and improve oxygenation.
