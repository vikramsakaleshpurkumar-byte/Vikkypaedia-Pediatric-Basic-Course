### Card 4 (Pediatric Therapeutics & Management)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> What is the clinical management or therapeutic approach for **Lower Urinary Tract Causes of Hematuria (564.2 Hemorrhagic Cystitis Prasad Devarajan)**?</summary>

**Answer:**
**Therapy / Protocol:** Cyclophosphamide **Clinical Application:** For chemical irritation related to the use of cyclophosphamide, hydration, bladder washes, and the use of mesna disulfide, which inactivates urinary cyclophosphamide metabolites, helps to protect the bladder.

</details>
