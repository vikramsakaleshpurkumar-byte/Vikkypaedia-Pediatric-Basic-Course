### Card 6 (Core Pediatric Concept)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In the pediatric evaluation of **Influenza Viruses** (*Influenza Viruses*), how is **ETIOLOGY** characterized?</summary>

**Answer:**
ETIOLOGY Influenza viruses are large, single-stranded RNA viruses belonging to the family Orthomyxoviridae, which includes three genera (or types): A, B, and C.

</details>
