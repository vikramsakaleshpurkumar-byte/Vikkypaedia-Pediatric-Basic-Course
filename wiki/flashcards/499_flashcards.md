### Card 4 (Pediatric Genetics & Pathogenesis)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In **Acquired Pure Red Blood Cell Anemia**, what clinical phenotype or molecular mechanism is associated with **`TPO`**?</summary>

**Answer:**
Like the situation observed in iron-deficiency anemia and other red blood cell (RBC) hypoplasias, thrombocyto sis is presumably caused by increased erythropoietin (EPO), which has homology to thrombopoietin (TPO).
