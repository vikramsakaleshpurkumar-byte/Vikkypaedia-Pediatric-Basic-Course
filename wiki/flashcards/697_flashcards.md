### Card 8 (Core Pediatric Concept)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In the pediatric evaluation of **Photosensitivity** (*Photosensitivity*), how is **Sun protection** characterized?</summary>

**Answer:**
Sun protection is best achieved by sun avoidance, which includes minimizing time in the midday sun (10 am to 4 pm), staying in the shade, and wearing protective clothing including wide-brimmed hats.
