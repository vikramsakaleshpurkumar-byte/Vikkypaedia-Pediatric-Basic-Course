### Card 1 (Syndrome & Clinical Profile)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> What are the hallmark clinical features and diagnostic profile of **ARTERIOPATHIES (Pediatric Stroke)**?</summary>

**Answer:**
**Category / Inheritance:** The Nervous System **Clinical Synthesis:** Focal or transient cerebral arteriopathy; Craniocervical arterial dissection; Fibromuscular dysplasia; Moyamoya disease or syndrome; Sickle cell arteriopathy; Primary CNS angiitis; HANAC syndrome; Genetic variants (see text and Table 641.2)

</details>

### Card 2 (Syndrome & Clinical Profile)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> What are the hallmark clinical features and diagnostic profile of **Congenital (Pediatric Stroke)**?</summary>

**Answer:**
**Category / Inheritance:** The Nervous System **Clinical Synthesis:** Aortic stenosis; Mitral stenosis; Ventricular septal defects; Patent ductus arteriosus; Patent foramen ovale; PHACE syndrome; Cyanotic congenital heart disease

</details>

### Card 3 (Syndrome & Clinical Profile)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> What are the hallmark clinical features and diagnostic profile of **Acquired (Pediatric Stroke)**?</summary>

**Answer:**
**Category / Inheritance:** The Nervous System **Clinical Synthesis:** Endocarditis; Kawasaki disease; Cardiomyopathy; Atrial myxoma; Arrhythmia; Rheumatic heart disease; Prosthetic heart valve; Catheterization/surgery

</details>

### Card 4 (Pediatric Genetics & Pathogenesis)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In **Pediatric Stroke**, what clinical phenotype or molecular mechanism is associated with **`ADA2`**?</summary>

**Answer:**
ACTA2, COL4A1, and ADA2 gene variants may be associated with AIS, and new genetic arteriopathic conditions are steadily being added to this list.

</details>

### Card 5 (Pediatric Genetics & Pathogenesis)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In **Pediatric Stroke**, what clinical phenotype or molecular mechanism is associated with **`CCM3`**?</summary>

**Answer:**
Cerebral cavernous malformations may be sporadic or familial (autosomal dominant) and associated with gene variants in the CCM1, CCM2, or CCM3 genes.

</details>

### Card 6 (Pediatric Therapeutics & Management)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> What is the clinical management or therapeutic approach for **HEMATOLOGIC DISORDERS**?</summary>

**Answer:**
**Therapy / Protocol:** Genetic Associations with Thrombotic, Hemorrhagic, or Vascular Stroke **Clinical Application:** Antithrombin deficiency; Protein C and S deficiency; Thrombomodulin deficiency; Activated protein C resistance; Factor V Leiden variant; Prothrombin G20210A variant; Sickle cell disease; Factor V, VII, VIII, IX, X, XI, XII, XIII deficiency; Hemoglobinopathies

</details>

### Card 7 (Pediatric Therapeutics & Management)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> What is the clinical management or therapeutic approach for **Pediatric Stroke: DRUGS**?</summary>

**Answer:**
**Therapy / Protocol:** Causes of Cerebral Venous Thrombosis **Clinical Application:** Asparaginase; Estrogen and oral contraceptives; Androgen; ε-Aminocaproic acid; Cisplatin and etoposide; Medroxyprogesterone; Heparin (heparin-induced thrombocytopenia); Immunoglobulin G (intravenous immunoglobulin)

</details>
