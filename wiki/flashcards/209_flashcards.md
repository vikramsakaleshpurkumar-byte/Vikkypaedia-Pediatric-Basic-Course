### Card 1 (Syndrome & Clinical Profile)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> What are the hallmark clinical features and diagnostic profile of **LONG TERM THERAPY (Sarcoidosis)**?</summary>

**Answer:**
**Category / Inheritance:** Rheumatic Diseases of Childh **Clinical Synthesis:** Small aneurysms: Aspirin 3-5 mg/kg/d; Medium aneurysms: Aspirin +/- clopidogrel 1 mg/kg/d; (max 75 mg/d); Giant aneurysms: Aspirin + anticoagulation with warfarin, low molecular; weight heparin, or direct oral anticoagulants (e.g. apixaban)

### Card 6 (Pediatric Therapeutics & Management)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> What is the clinical management or therapeutic approach for **Sarcoidosis**?</summary>

**Answer:**
