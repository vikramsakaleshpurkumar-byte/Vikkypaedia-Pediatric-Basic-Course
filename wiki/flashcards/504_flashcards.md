### Card 8 (Core Pediatric Concept)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In the pediatric evaluation of **Iron-Deficiency Anemia** (*503.3 Other Rare Megaloblastic Anemias Courtney D. Thornburg*), how is **Orotic aciduria** characterized?</summary>

**Answer:**
Orotic aciduria is a rare autosomal recessive disorder that usually appears in the first year of life and is characterized by growth failure, developmen tal delay, megaloblastic anemia, and increased urinary excretion of orotic acid.

</details>
