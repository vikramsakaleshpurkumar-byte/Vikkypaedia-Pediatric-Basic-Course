### Card 3 (Pediatric Therapeutics & Management)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> What is the clinical management or therapeutic approach for **Bell Palsy**?</summary>

**Answer:**
**Therapy / Protocol:** Prednisone **Clinical Application:** TREATMENT In contrast to adults, the outcome of pediatric Bell palsy is so good that benefit from treatment with corticosteroids, with or without acy clovir, has not been established, although many centers recommend a brief course of oral prednisone.

</details>
