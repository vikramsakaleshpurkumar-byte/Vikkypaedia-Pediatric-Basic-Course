### Card 5 (Core Pediatric Concept)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In the pediatric evaluation of **Diseases of the Gallbladder** (*Diseases of the Gallbladder*), how is **Diseases of the gallbladder** characterized?</summary>

**Answer:**
Diseases of the gallbladder in infants, children, and adolescents include congenital anomalies (gallbladder agenesis, bilobed/ectopic gallbladder), gallbladder hydrops, cholelithiasis (gallstones), calculous and acalculous cholecystitis, and biliary dyskinesia.

</details>

### Card 7 (Core Pediatric Concept)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In the pediatric evaluation of **Diseases of the Gallbladder** (*Diseases of the Gallbladder*), how is **Acute acalculous cholecystitis** characterized?</summary>

**Answer:**
Acute acalculous cholecystitis accounts for 30–50% of acute cholecystitis in children (far higher than in adults!) and occurs during severe systemic illness, sepsis, trauma/burns, Kawasaki disease (gallbladder hydrops), typhoid fever (Salmonella Typhi), EBV, or streptococcal infection.

</details>
