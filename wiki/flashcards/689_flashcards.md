### Card 1 (Syndrome & Clinical Profile)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> What are the hallmark clinical features and diagnostic profile of **Cutaneous Defects**?</summary>

**Answer:**
**Associated Gene(s):** `AR, BMS1` · **Category / Inheritance:** The Skin **Clinical Synthesis:** The differential diagnosis in neonates includes transient neonatal pustular melanosis, erythema toxicum, milia, cutaneous candidiasis, and staphylococcal pustulosis.

### Card 3 (Pediatric Therapeutics & Management)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> What is the clinical management or therapeutic approach for **Cutaneous Defects**?</summary>

**Answer:**
**Therapy / Protocol:** Mg/kg **Clinical Application:** Dapsone (1-2 mg/kg/day by mouth, divided in one to two doses) is effective but has potentially serious side effects, notably, hemolytic anemia and met hemoglobinemia; its use should be limited to particularly severe cases.

</details>
