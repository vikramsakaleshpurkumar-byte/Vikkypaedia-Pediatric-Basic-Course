### Card 3 (Pediatric Genetics & Pathogenesis)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In **Disorders of Thyroxine-Binding Globulin**, what clinical phenotype or molecular mechanism is associated with **`PAX8`**?</summary>

**Answer:**
A small minority (2–5%) of thyroid dysgenesis is caused by genetic defects in one of several transcription factors essential for thyroid morphogenesis and differentiation, including NKX2.1 (formerly TTF1), FOXE1 (formerly TTF2), and PAX8.

</details>

### Card 4 (Pediatric Genetics & Pathogenesis)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In **Disorders of Thyroxine-Binding Globulin**, what clinical phenotype or molecular mechanism is associated with **`NKX2-1`**?</summary>

**Answer:**
NKX2.1 is expressed in the thyroid, lung, and central nervous system, and recessive pathogenic variants in NKX2-1 cause thyroid dysgenesis, respiratory distress, and neurologic problems (chorea and ataxia) (brain-lung-thyroid syn- drome).

</details>

### Card 8 (Core Pediatric Concept)

**Answer:**
