### Card 3 (Syndrome & Clinical Profile)

**Answer:**
**Category / Inheritance:** The Digestive System **Clinical Synthesis:** 1. Two or more periods of intense unremitting nausea and; paroxysmal vomiting, lasting hours to days within a 6-mo period; 2.
