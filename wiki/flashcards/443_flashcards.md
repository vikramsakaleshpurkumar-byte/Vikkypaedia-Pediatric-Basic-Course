### Card 6 (Pediatric Therapeutics & Management)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> What is the clinical management or therapeutic approach for **Other Distal Airway Diseases (443.1 Bronchiolitis Obliterans)**?</summary>

**Answer:**
**Therapy / Protocol:** Methylprednisolone **Clinical Application:** Corticosteroids should be given early, pulse steroid therapy with methylprednisolone is preferred, and a prolonged course of oral steroids should be avoided to minimize severe side effects and complications or mortality from infections.

</details>

### Card 7 (Core Pediatric Concept)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In the pediatric evaluation of **Other Distal Airway Diseases** (*443.1 Bronchiolitis Obliterans*), how is **Bronchiolitis obliterans syndrome (BOS)** characterized?</summary>

**Answer:**
Bronchiolitis obliterans syndrome (BOS) is a clinical diagnosis related to graft deterioration after transplantation, defined as a pro gressive decline in lung function based on forced expiratory volume in 1 second (FEV1).

</details>
