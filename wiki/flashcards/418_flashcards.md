### Card 1 (Syndrome & Clinical Profile)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> What are the hallmark clinical features and diagnostic profile of **SAAG ≥ 1.1 g/dL (Portal Hypertension)**?</summary>

**Answer:**
**Category / Inheritance:** The Digestive System **Clinical Synthesis:** Cirrhosis, biliary atresia, Budd-Chiari syndrome, post-Fontan / right heart failure; treat with Na restriction + Spironolactone ± Furosemide

</details>

### Card 2 (Syndrome & Clinical Profile)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> What are the hallmark clinical features and diagnostic profile of **SAAG < 1.1 g/dL (Non-Portal Hypertension)**?</summary>

**Answer:**
**Category / Inheritance:** The Digestive System **Clinical Synthesis:** Pancreatic ascites (high ascitic amylase), tuberculous peritonitis, peritoneal malignancy, lupus serositis

</details>

### Card 3 (Syndrome & Clinical Profile)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> What are the hallmark clinical features and diagnostic profile of **Chylous Ascites (Subchapter 418.1)**?</summary>

**Answer:**
**Category / Inheritance:** The Digestive System **Clinical Synthesis:** Milky fluid, triglycerides >200 mg/dL, lymphocyte predominance; congenital lymphangiectasia (#1 in infants) or surgical/abusive trauma; treat with MCT-based diet ± Octreotide/TPN

</details>

### Card 6 (Core Pediatric Concept)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In the pediatric evaluation of **Ascites** (*Ascites*), how is **antidiuretic hormone (ADH)** characterized?</summary>

**Answer:**
antidiuretic hormone (ADH) to cause avid renal sodium and water retention, compounded by hypoalbuminemia (reduced plasma oncotic pressure).

</details>

### Card 7 (Core Pediatric Concept)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In the pediatric evaluation of **Ascites** (*Ascites*), how is **congenital lymphatic malformations** characterized?</summary>

**Answer:**
congenital lymphatic malformations (the #1 cause in neonates and infants, including primary intestinal lymphangiectasia / Waldmann disease), thoracic duct / cisterna chyli trauma (retroperitoneal surgery, Fontan surgery, or non-accidental trauma [child abuse]), malrotation with midgut volvulus, or lymphoma.

</details>

### Card 8 (Core Pediatric Concept)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In the pediatric evaluation of **Ascites** (*Ascites*), how is **Abdominal ultrasound** characterized?</summary>

**Answer:**
Abdominal ultrasound detects as little as 50–100 mL of free peritoneal fluid.

</details>
