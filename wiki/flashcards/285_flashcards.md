### Card 2 (Pediatric Genetics & Pathogenesis)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In **Blastomycosis**, what clinical phenotype or molecular mechanism is associated with **`BAD1`**?</summary>

**Answer:**
In the yeast form, the essential virulence factor BAD1 (Blastomyces adhesin-1; for merly WI-1) is secreted into the extracellular milieu and binds back to chitin on the fungal cell wall.
