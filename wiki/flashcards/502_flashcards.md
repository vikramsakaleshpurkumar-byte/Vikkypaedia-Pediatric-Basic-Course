### Card 1 (Syndrome & Clinical Profile)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> What are the hallmark clinical features and diagnostic profile of **Physiologic Anemia of Infancy**?</summary>

**Answer:**
**Associated Gene(s):** `GATA1, KLF1` · **Category / Inheritance:** X-Linked **Clinical Synthesis:** XLTDA is an X-linked condition due to pathogenic variants in GATA1, a DNA-binding protein with two zinc fingers and a transacti vation domain that plays a key role in development and maintenance of red cell and platelet lineages.
