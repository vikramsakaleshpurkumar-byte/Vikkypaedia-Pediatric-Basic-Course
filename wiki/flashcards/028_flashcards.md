### Card 8 (High-Yield Board Pearl)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> What is a high-yield clinical or diagnostic principle regarding **Developmental and Behavioral Surveillance and Screening** (*Developmental and Behavioral Surveillance and Screening*)?</summary>

**Answer:**
Among the many types of developmental or behavioral conditions, the most common include language problems, affecting at least 10% of children; behavior or emotional disorders, affecting up to 25% of children, with 6% considered serious; attention-deficit/hyper activity disorder, affecting 10% of children; and learning disabilities, affecting up to 10%.
