### Card 1 (Syndrome & Clinical Profile)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> What are the hallmark clinical features and diagnostic profile of **Carcinoma of the Thyroid**?</summary>

**Answer:**
**Associated Gene(s):** `APC, BRAF, DICER1, PTEN, RET` · **Category / Inheritance:** The Endocrine System **Clinical Synthesis:** PATHOGENESIS The majority of differentiated thyroid cancers are of follicular cell origin; papillary carcinoma (85–90%) is the most common subtype.

### Card 2 (Syndrome & Clinical Profile)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> What are the hallmark clinical features and diagnostic profile of **Thyroid Nodules (Carcinoma of the Thyroid)**?</summary>

**Answer:**
**Associated Gene(s):** `APC, BRAF, DICER1, PTEN` · **Category / Inheritance:** The Endocrine System **Clinical Synthesis:** About 70% of adults with papillary thyroid cancer exhibit pathogenic somatic variants in BRAF or RAS; pediatric papillary thyroid cancers are more commonly caused by somatic gene fusion events involving the oncogenic tyrosine kinases RET or NTRK1/3.

</details>
