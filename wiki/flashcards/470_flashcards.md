### Card 8 (Pathophysiology & Diagnosis)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> What core clinical principle governs **470.3 The Neonatal Circulation Daniel Bernstein** in **The Fetal to Neonatal Circulatory Transition**?</summary>

**Answer:**
The newborn cardiac output, indexed to body weight (200 mL/kg/min), falls in the first 2 months of life to approximately 150 mL/kg/min and then more gradually to the normal adult cardiac output of 75 mL/kg/ min.
