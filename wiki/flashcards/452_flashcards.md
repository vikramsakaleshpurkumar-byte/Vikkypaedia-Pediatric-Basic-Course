### Card 2 (Syndrome & Clinical Profile)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> What are the hallmark clinical features and diagnostic profile of **AIRWAY INJURY (Bronchiectasis)**?</summary>

**Answer:**
**Category / Inheritance:** The Respiratory System **Clinical Synthesis:** Bronchiolitis obliterans (e.g., postviral, after lung transplantation); Recurrent pneumonitis or pneumonia (e.g., pneumococcal pneumonia,; aspiration pneumonia, tuberculosis, B. pertussis)

</details>

### Card 4 (Pediatric Therapeutics & Management)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> What is the clinical management or therapeutic approach for **Bronchiectasis**?</summary>

**Answer:**
**Therapy / Protocol:** Amoxicillin **Clinical Application:** A 14-day course of amoxicillin/clavulanic acid (22.5 mg/kg/dose twice daily) has been particularly successful at treating most pulmonary exacerbations.

</details>
