### Card 1 (Syndrome & Clinical Profile)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> What are the hallmark clinical features and diagnostic profile of **Transient Tachypnea of the Newborn**?</summary>

**Answer:**
**Category / Inheritance:** The Fetus and the Neonatal I **Clinical Synthesis:** CLINICAL MANIFESTATIONS TTN is characterized by the early onset of tachypnea (>60 breaths/min), sometimes with retractions or expiratory grunting and occasionally with cyanosis that is relieved by minimal O2 supplementation (<40%).

### Card 2 (Pediatric Therapeutics & Management)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> What is the clinical management or therapeutic approach for **Transient Tachypnea of the Newborn**?</summary>
