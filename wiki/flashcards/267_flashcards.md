### Card 2 (Pediatric Therapeutics & Management)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> What is the clinical management or therapeutic approach for **Relapsing Fever (Borrelia)**?</summary>

**Answer:**
**Therapy / Protocol:** Immunoglobulin **Clinical Application:** During febrile episodes, spirochetes enter the bloodstream, induce the development of specific immunoglobulin M and G antibodies, and undergo agglutination, immobilization, lysis, and phagocytosis.

</details>

### Card 4 (Core Pediatric Concept)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In the pediatric evaluation of **Relapsing Fever (Borrelia)** (*Relapsing Fever (Borrelia)*), how is **Louse-borne (epidemic) relapsing fever** characterized?</summary>

**Answer:**
Louse-borne (epidemic) relapsing fever is caused by Borrelia recur rentis and is transmitted from person to person by Pediculus humanus, the human body louse.

</details>

### Card 5 (Core Pediatric Concept)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In the pediatric evaluation of **Relapsing Fever (Borrelia)** (*Relapsing Fever (Borrelia)*), how is **Tick-borne (endemic) relapsing fever** characterized?</summary>

**Answer:**
Tick-borne (endemic) relapsing fever is caused by several species of Borrelia and is transmitted to humans by Ornithodoros ticks.

</details>
