<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In the pediatric evaluation of **Other Congenital Heart and Vascular Malformations** (*481.1 Anomalies of the Aortic Arch*), how is **RIGHT AORTIC ARCH** characterized?</summary>

**Answer:**
RIGHT AORTIC ARCH In this abnormality, the aorta curves to the right and, if it descends on the right side of the vertebral column, is usually associated with other cardiac malformations.
