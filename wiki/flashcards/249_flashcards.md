### Card 4 (Pediatric Therapeutics & Management)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> What is the clinical management or therapeutic approach for **Yersinia (249.1 Yersinia enterocolitica Ericka V. Hayes)**?</summary>

**Answer:**
**Therapy / Protocol:** Trimethoprim **Clinical Application:** Y. enterocolitica organisms are typically susceptible to trimethoprim-sulfamethoxazole (TMP-SMX), amino glycosides, third-generation cephalosporins, and quinolones, although strains resistant to quinolones have been reported.

</details>
