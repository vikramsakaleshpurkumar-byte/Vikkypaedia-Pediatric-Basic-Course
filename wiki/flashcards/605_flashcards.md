### Card 5 (Core Pediatric Concept)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In the pediatric evaluation of **Goiter** (*605.3 Endemic Goiter and Cretinism Ari J. Wassner and Jessica R. Smith*), how is **ETIOLOGY** characterized?</summary>

**Answer:**
ETIOLOGY Goiter caused by iodine deficiency is termed endemic goiter, whereas cre- tinism refers to the clinical manifestations of severe hypothyroidism in early life.

</details>

### Card 6 (Core Pediatric Concept)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In the pediatric evaluation of **Goiter** (*605.3 Endemic Goiter and Cretinism Ari J. Wassner and Jessica R. Smith*), how is **CLINICAL MANIFESTATIONS** characterized?</summary>

**Answer:**
CLINICAL MANIFESTATIONS In mild iodine deficiency, thyroid enlargement generally is not noticeable except when demand for thyroid hormone synthesis is increased, such as during rapid growth in adolescence and pregnancy.

</details>

### Card 7 (Core Pediatric Concept)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In the pediatric evaluation of **Goiter** (*605.3 Endemic Goiter and Cretinism Ari J. Wassner and Jessica R. Smith*), how is **Endemic cretinism** characterized?</summary>

**Answer:**
Endemic cretinism is the most severe consequence of iodine deficiency and occurs only in association with endemic goiter.

</details>
