### Card 8 (Pathophysiology & Diagnosis)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> What core clinical principle governs **Acquired Inhibitors of Coagulation** in **Acquired Inhibitors of Coagulation**?</summary>

**Answer:**
Acquired circulating anticoagulants (inhibitors) are antibodies that react, or cross-react, with clotting factors or components used in coag ulation screening tests (phospholipids), thereby prolonging screening tests, such as prothrombin time (PT) and partial thromboplastin time (PTT), although not all of them result in a clinical bleeding state.

</details>
