### Card 4 (Pediatric Genetics & Pathogenesis)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In **Disorders of Purine and Pyrimidine Metabolism**, what clinical phenotype or molecular mechanism is associated with **`SLC22A12`**?</summary>

**Answer:**
The disorder is autosomal recessive and caused by pathogenic variants in the SLC22A12 or SLC2A9 genes, which code for transporters important for renal retention of uric acid.

</details>
