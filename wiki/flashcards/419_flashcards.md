### Card 1 (Syndrome & Clinical Profile)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> What are the hallmark clinical features and diagnostic profile of **Acute Primary Peritonitis / SBP (Subchapter 419.1)**?</summary>

**Answer:**
**Category / Inheritance:** The Digestive System **Clinical Synthesis:** Nephrotic syndrome or cirrhosis; monomicrobial (S. pneumoniae, E. coli, Klebsiella); ascitic PMN ≥250 cells/mm³; treat with IV Ceftriaxone/Cefotaxime (medical, non-surgical)

</details>

### Card 2 (Syndrome & Clinical Profile)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> What are the hallmark clinical features and diagnostic profile of **Acute Secondary Peritonitis (Subchapter 419.2)**?</summary>

**Answer:**
**Category / Inheritance:** The Digestive System **Clinical Synthesis:** Perforated appendicitis (#1 in children) or neonatal NEC; polymicrobial (E. coli + Bacteroides fragilis); free air on imaging; urgent surgical source control + Pip-Tazo or Ceftriaxone/Metronidazole

</details>

### Card 3 (Syndrome & Clinical Profile)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> What are the hallmark clinical features and diagnostic profile of **Localized Peritoneal Abscess (Subchapter 419.3)**?</summary>

**Answer:**
**Category / Inheritance:** The Digestive System **Clinical Synthesis:** Pelvic, interloop, or subphrenic abscess post-appendicitis; spiking fevers & tenesmus; US/CT-guided percutaneous drainage + IV antibiotics

</details>
