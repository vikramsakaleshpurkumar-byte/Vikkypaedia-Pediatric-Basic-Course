### Card 1 (Syndrome & Clinical Profile)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> What are the hallmark clinical features and diagnostic profile of **EATS (Neonatal Abstinence Syndrome)**?</summary>

**Answer:**
**Category / Inheritance:** The Fetus and the Neonatal I **Clinical Synthesis:** Takes > 10 min to coordinate feeding with hunger cues; < 10 min for breastfeeds; < 10 mL of a feed

</details>

### Card 3 (Syndrome & Clinical Profile)
