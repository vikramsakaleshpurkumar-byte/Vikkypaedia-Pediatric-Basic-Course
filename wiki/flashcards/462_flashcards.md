### Card 4 (Core Pediatric Concept)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In the pediatric evaluation of **Pneumomediastinum** (*Pneumomediastinum*), how is **PATHOGENESIS** characterized?</summary>

**Answer:**
PATHOGENESIS According to the Macklin effect, after an intrapulmonary alveolar rupture, air dissects along the pressure gradient through the perivascular sheaths and other soft tissue planes toward the hilum and enters the mediastinum.

</details>

**Answer:**
CLINICAL MANIFESTATIONS Dyspnea and transient stabbing chest pain that may radiate to the neck are the principal features of pneumomediastinum.
