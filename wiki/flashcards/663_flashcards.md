### Card 2 (Syndrome & Clinical Profile)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> What are the hallmark clinical features and diagnostic profile of **DIRECTION OF DEVIATION**?</summary>

**Answer:**
**Category / Inheritance:** Disorders of the Eye **Clinical Synthesis:** Eso-: inward, horizontal deviation (“crossing”); Exo-: outward, horizontal deviation (“wall eye”); Hyper-: upward, vertical deviation; Hypo-: downward, vertical deviation; Incyclo-: nasal torsional deviation of the superior pole of the cornea; Excyclo-: temporal torsional deviation of the superior pole of the; cornea

</details>

### Card 3 (Syndrome & Clinical Profile)

**Answer:**
**Category / Inheritance:** Disorders of the Eye **Clinical Synthesis:** Concomitant: misalignment is equal in all positions of gaze; Noncomitant: misalignment varies significantly in different positions; of gaze
