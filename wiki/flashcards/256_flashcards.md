### Card 1 (Syndrome & Clinical Profile)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> What are the hallmark clinical features and diagnostic profile of **NEUROMUSCULAR (Botulism (Clostridium botulinum))**?</summary>

**Answer:**
**Category / Inheritance:** Infectious Diseases **Clinical Synthesis:** Spinal muscular atrophy (type 1); Congenital myasthenia gravis; Guillain-Barré syndrome and its variants; Poliomyelitis; Acute flaccid paralysis; Transverse myelitis
