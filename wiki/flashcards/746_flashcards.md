### Card 5 (Pediatric Genetics & Pathogenesis)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In **Bone Structure, Growth, and Hormonal Regulation**, what clinical phenotype or molecular mechanism is associated with **`FBLN4`**?</summary>

**Answer:**
Subtype ARCL1b, caused by pathogenic variants in FBLN4, in addition to skin findings, typically develops elongation, tortuosity, and aneurysms of the large- and middle-sized arteries.
