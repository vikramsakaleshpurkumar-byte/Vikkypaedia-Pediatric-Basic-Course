### Card 5 (Core Pediatric Concept)

**Answer:**
CLINICAL MANIFESTATIONS Patients at risk for the subsequent development of tethered cord syn drome can often be identified at birth by the presence of an open myelomeningocele or by cutaneous manifestations of dysraphism.
