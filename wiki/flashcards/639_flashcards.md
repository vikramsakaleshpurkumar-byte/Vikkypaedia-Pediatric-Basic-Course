**Answer:**
Pseudomigraine syndrome with CSF pleocytosis (PMP) or head ache with neurologic deficits and CSF lymphocytosis (HaNDL) is an ill-defined entity that predominantly affects young male adults with a family history of migraine, although adolescents can be affected.
