### Card 4 (Pediatric Genetics & Pathogenesis)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In **Diabetes Insipidus**, what clinical phenotype or molecular mechanism is associated with **`AVPR2`**?</summary>

**Answer:**
Vasopressin exerts its principal effect on the kidney via V2 (AVPR2) receptors located primarily in the collecting tubule, the thick ascending limb of the loop of Henle, and the periglomerular tubules.

</details>

### Card 5 (Pediatric Genetics & Pathogenesis)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In **Diabetes Insipidus**, what clinical phenotype or molecular mechanism is associated with **`WFS2`**?</summary>

**Answer:**
Autosomal recessive pathogenic variants in the WFS1 (most common) or WFS2 (CISD2) genes, which give rise to endoplasmic reticulum proteins, are associated with this condition.

</details>

### Card 7 (Pediatric Therapeutics & Management)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> What is the clinical management or therapeutic approach for **Diabetes Insipidus**?</summary>

**Answer:**
**Therapy / Protocol:** Amphotericin **Clinical Application:** Acquired NDI can result from hypercalcemia or hypokalemia and is associated with lithium, demeclocycline, foscarnet, clozapine, amphotericin, methicillin, and rifampin.
