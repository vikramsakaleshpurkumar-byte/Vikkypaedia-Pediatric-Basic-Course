### Card 1 (Syndrome & Clinical Profile)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> What are the hallmark clinical features and diagnostic profile of **Biliary Atresia (Extrahepatic Fibro-Obliterative Cholangiopathy)**?</summary>

**Answer:**
**Category / Inheritance:** The Digestive System **Clinical Synthesis:** Acholic stools, hepatomegaly, high GGT & MMP-7; biopsy shows bile duct proliferation + bile plugs; IOC gold standard; Kasai portoenterostomy <60 days of life

</details>

### Card 3 (Syndrome & Clinical Profile)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> What are the hallmark clinical features and diagnostic profile of **PFIC1 (ATP8B1 / FIC1) & PFIC2 (ABCB11 / BSEP)**?</summary>

**Answer:**

</details>

### Card 8 (High-Yield Board Pearl)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> What is a high-yield clinical or diagnostic principle regarding **Cholestasis** (*Cholestasis*)?</summary>

**Answer:**
Percutaneous liver biopsy in BA demonstrates bile ductular proliferation, portal tract edema/fibrosis, and canalicular/ductular bile plugs; the gold standard confirmation is intraoperative cholangiography (IOC) showing failure of contrast to pass through the extrahepatic biliary tree.

</details>
