### Card 7 (Core Pediatric Concept)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In the pediatric evaluation of **Clinical Evaluation of the Child with Hematuria** (*Clinical Evaluation of the Child with Hematuria*), how is **Myoglobinuria** characterized?</summary>

**Answer:**
Myoglobinuria without hematuria occurs in the presence of rhabdomyolysis resulting from skeletal muscle injury and is generally associated with a fivefold increase in the plasma concentration of creatine kinase.

</details>
