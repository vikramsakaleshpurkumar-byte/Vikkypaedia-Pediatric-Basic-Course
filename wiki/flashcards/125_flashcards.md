### Card 1 (Syndrome & Clinical Profile)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> What are the hallmark clinical features and diagnostic profile of **Apnea**?</summary>

**Answer:**
**Category / Inheritance:** The Fetus and the Neonatal I **Clinical Synthesis:** Apnea of prematurity results from immature respiratory control, most frequently occurs in infants <34 weeks of GA, and occurs in the absence of identifiable predisposing diseases.

</details>

### Card 2 (Pediatric Therapeutics & Management)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> What is the clinical management or therapeutic approach for **Apnea**?</summary>

**Answer:**
**Therapy / Protocol:** Caffeine **Clinical Application:** Caffeine and theophyl line are similarly effective methylxanthines, but caffeine is preferred because of its longer half-life and lower potential for side effects (less tachycardia and feeding intolerance).

</details>
