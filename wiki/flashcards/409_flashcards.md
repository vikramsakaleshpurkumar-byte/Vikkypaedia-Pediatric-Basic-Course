### Card 1 (Syndrome & Clinical Profile)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> What are the hallmark clinical features and diagnostic profile of **Alpers-Huttenlocher Syndrome (POLG, OMIM #203700)** (#203700)?</summary>

**Answer:**

### Card 2 (Syndrome & Clinical Profile)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> What are the hallmark clinical features and diagnostic profile of **DGUOK Deficiency (Hepatocerebral MDS)**?</summary>

**Answer:**
**Associated Gene(s):** `DGUOK` · **Category / Inheritance:** mtDNA **Clinical Synthesis:** Most common neonatal mitochondrial liver failure; cholestasis, lactic acidosis (lactate:pyruvate >25), hypoglycemia, nystagmus, high ferritin

</details>

### Card 3 (Syndrome & Clinical Profile)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> What are the hallmark clinical features and diagnostic profile of **TRMU Deficiency (Reversible Infantile Liver Failure)**?</summary>

**Answer:**
**Associated Gene(s):** `TRMU` · **Category / Inheritance:** The Digestive System **Clinical Synthesis:** Acute infantile liver failure with severe lactic acidosis at 1–6 months; high rate of spontaneous complete recovery when supported with L-cysteine and N-acetylcysteine

</details>

### Card 4 (Pediatric Genetics & Pathogenesis)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In **Mitochondrial Hepatopathies**, what clinical phenotype or molecular mechanism is associated with **`MPV17`**?</summary>

**Answer:**
Definitive diagnosis is established by next-generation sequencing (whole-exome/genome or mitochondrial gene panel) for DGUOK, POLG, MPV17, TWNK, TRMU (transient infantile liver failure due to TRMU variants, which can recover spontaneously with L-cysteine + N-acetylcysteine supplementation!).

</details>

### Card 5 (Pediatric Genetics & Pathogenesis)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In **Mitochondrial Hepatopathies**, what clinical phenotype or molecular mechanism is associated with **`TRMU`**?</summary>

**Answer:**
Supportive care includes continuous dextrose infusion to prevent catabolism, correction of lactic acidosis, and cofactor supplementation (coenzyme Q10, riboflavin, thiamine, L-carnitine; L-cysteine + N-acetylcysteine for reversible TRMU deficiency).

</details>

### Card 8 (Core Pediatric Concept)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In the pediatric evaluation of **Mitochondrial Hepatopathies** (*Mitochondrial Hepatopathies*), how is **Alpers-Huttenlocher syndrome** characterized?</summary>

**Answer:**
Alpers-Huttenlocher syndrome [203700]; refractory focal/myoclonic seizures, psychomotor regression, cortical blindness, and fulminant micronodular liver failure often precipitated within weeks of starting valproic acid), MPV17 (Navajo neurohepatopathy), and TWNK (Twinkle helicase).

</details>
