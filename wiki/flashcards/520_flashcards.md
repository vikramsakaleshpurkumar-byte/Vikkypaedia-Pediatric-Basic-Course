### Card 6 (Core Pediatric Concept)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In the pediatric evaluation of **Platelet Transfusions** (*Platelet Transfusions*), how is **Preoperative autolo gous** characterized?</summary>

**Answer:**
Preoperative autolo gous blood collections from the patient occur up to 6 weeks before the surgery and require careful considerations for the volume to be drawn, vascular access, and use of EPO and iron to help restore the donated RBCs.

</details>

### Card 7 (Core Pediatric Concept)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In the pediatric evaluation of **Platelet Transfusions** (*Platelet Transfusions*), how is **Acute normovolemic hemodilution** characterized?</summary>

**Answer:**
Acute normovolemic hemodilution occurs in the preoperative period, in which blood is withdrawn from the patient and replaced with saline, a task often difficult in centers without experience in the process.

</details>

### Card 8 (Core Pediatric Concept)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In the pediatric evaluation of **Platelet Transfusions** (*Platelet Transfusions*), how is **Salvaged autologous blood** characterized?</summary>

**Answer:**
Salvaged autologous blood is collected from blood loss during the operation but is impractical unless the volume of blood salvaged is fairly large to permit washing and transfusion of a significant number of RBCs.

</details>
