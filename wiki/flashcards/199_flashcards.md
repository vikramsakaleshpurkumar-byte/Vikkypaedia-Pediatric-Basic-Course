### Card 7 (Core Pediatric Concept)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In the pediatric evaluation of **Systemic Lupus Erythematosus** (*Systemic Lupus Erythematosus*), how is **Environmental exposures** characterized?</summary>

**Answer:**
Environmental exposures that may trigger the development of SLE remain largely unknown; certain viral infections (including Epstein-Barr virus) may play a role in susceptible individuals, and ultraviolet light exposure is known to trigger SLE disease activity.

</details>
