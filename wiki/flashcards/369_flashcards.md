### Card 6 (Core Pediatric Concept)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In the pediatric evaluation of **Gastroesophageal Reflux Disease** (*Gastroesophageal Reflux Disease*), how is **Transient LES relaxation (TLESR)** characterized?</summary>

**Answer:**
Transient LES relaxation (TLESR) is the primary mechanism allowing reflux to occur and is defined as simultaneous relaxation of both LES and the surrounding crura.

### Card 7 (Core Pediatric Concept)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In the pediatric evaluation of **Gastroesophageal Reflux Disease** (*Gastroesophageal Reflux Disease*), how is **EPIDEMIOLOGY AND NATURAL HISTORY Infant reflux** characterized?</summary>

**Answer:**
EPIDEMIOLOGY AND NATURAL HISTORY Infant reflux becomes evident in the first few months of life, peaks at 4 months, and resolves in up to 88% by 12 months and in nearly all by 24 months.

</details>
