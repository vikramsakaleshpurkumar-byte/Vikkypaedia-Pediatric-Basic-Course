### [Chapter 648: Developmental Disorders of Muscle](../chapters/648.md)
- **Core Clinical Pearl:** Though clinical features, including phenotype, can raise a strong suspi cion of a congenital myopathy, the definitive diagnosis is determined by the histopathologic findings in the muscle biopsy specimen or by genetic te
- **Key Genes:** `ACTA1`, `AKT1`, `AR`, `BIN1`, `CACNA1S`, `CCDC78`, `CFL2`, `CHAT`, `CNTN1`, `DCX`, `DMD`, `DNAJB6`
### [Chapter 649: Muscular Dystrophies](../chapters/649.md)
- **Core Clinical Pearl:** MANAGEMENT Each affected individual with arthrogryposis is unique, and the natural course of the condition depends on the underlying etiology and man agement.
- **Key Genes:** `ANO5`, `AR`, `CAPN3`, `CAV3`, `COL6A1`, `COL6A2`, `COL6A3`, `DAG1`, `DMD`, `DYSF`, `FHL1`, `FSHD1`
### [Chapter 650: Endocrine and Toxic Myopathies](../chapters/650.md)
- **Core Clinical Pearl:** Although muscle wasting is most characteristic, one form of cretinism, Kocher- Debré-Sémélaigne syndrome, is characterized by generalized pseudo hypertrophy of weak muscles.
- **Key Genes:** `GIRK4`, `KCNJ18`, `KCNJ5`
### [Chapter 651: Metabolic Myopathies and Channelopathies*](../chapters/651.md)
- **Core Clinical Pearl:** All familial forms of periodic paralysis are caused by pathogenic variants in genes encoding voltage-gated ion channels in muscle: sodium, calcium, and potassium.
- **Key Genes:** `ACADS`, `ACADVL`, `CACNA1S`, `ETFA`, `ETFB`, `ETFDH`, `HADH`, `HADHA`, `HADHB`, `HSPG2`, `LPIN1`, `PNPLA2`
### [Chapter 652: Disorders of Neuromuscular Transmission and of Motor Neurons*](../chapters/652.md)
- **Core Clinical Pearl:** Some patients may also require pyridostigmine (acetylcholinesterase [AChE] inhibitor) management transiently.
- **Key Genes:** `ALG14`, `ALG2`, `AR`, `ASAH1`, `ASCC1`, `ATM`, `ATP7A`, `BICD2`, `CHAT`, `CHCHD10`, `CHD8`, `CHRNE`
- **OMIM IDs:** `#606407`
### [Chapter 653: Hereditary Motor-Sensory Neuropathies](../chapters/653.md)
- **Core Clinical Pearl:** CMT is the most common genetically determined neuropathy and the most common demyelinating variant, also described as CMT1A, and has an overall prevalence of 3.8/100,000 population and accounts for >50% of cases of HMSN 
- **Key Genes:** `ABC1`, `ABCD1`, `AIFM1`, `AR`, `ARHGEF10`, `ARSA`, `ASCC1`, `ATL1`, `ATL3`, `ATM`, `ATXN10`, `ATXN3`
### [Chapter 654: Toxic Neuropathies](../chapters/654.md)
- **Core Clinical Pearl:** DIAGNOSIS All HSANs are characterized by absence of the normal axon flare response to intradermal injection of histamine phosphate.
- **Key Genes:** `BRAF`, `TRPA1`
### [Chapter 655: Autonomic Neuropathies](../chapters/655.md)
- **Core Clinical Pearl:** The treatment of acquired auto nomic dysfunction includes both management of the primary disorder (Guillain-Barré syndrome, diabetes) and symptomatic management of organ-specific manifestations.
- **Key Genes:** `ALT1`, `AR`, `ATL3`, `DMNT1`, `FAM134B`, `KIF1A`, `SCN11A`, `SCN9A`, `SPTLC2`
### [Chapter 656: Guillain-Barré Syndrome](../chapters/656.md)
- **Core Clinical Pearl:** GBS may follow administration of vaccines against rabies, influenza, and conjugated meningococcal vaccine, particularly serogroup C.
- **Key Genes:** `GM1`
### [Chapter 657: Bell Palsy](../chapters/657.md)
- **Core Clinical Pearl:** PROGNOSIS Most children experience complete spontaneous recovery from Bell palsy within a few weeks from onset.
