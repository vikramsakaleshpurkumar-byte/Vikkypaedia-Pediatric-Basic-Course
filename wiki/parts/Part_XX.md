## Chapter Synthesis & Clinical Highlights
### [Chapter 540: Epidemiology of Childhood and Adolescent Cancer](../chapters/540.md)
- **Core Clinical Pearl:** Pediatric malignancies differ greatly from adult malignancies in both prognosis and distribution by histology and tumor site.
- **Key Genes:** `DICER1`, `GATA2`, `NF1`, `RB1`, `TP53`, `WT1`
### [Chapter 541: Molecular and Cellular Biology of Cancer](../chapters/541.md)
- **Core Clinical Pearl:** SYNDROMES PREDISPOSING TO CANCER Several syndromes are associated with an increased risk of develop ing malignancies, which can be characterized by different mechanisms.
- **Key Genes:** `ADA`, `ALK`, `APC`, `ATM`, `BLM`, `BMPR1A`, `BTK`, `DICER1`, `ELANE`, `EXT1`, `EXT2`, `G6PC`
### [Chapter 542: Principles of Cancer Diagnosis](../chapters/542.md)
- **Core Clinical Pearl:** Almost all cervical carcinomas are caused by human papillomaviruses (HPVs).
- **Key Genes:** `IGF2`, `TERT`, `TP53`
### [Chapter 543: Principles of Cancer Treatment](../chapters/543.md)
- **Core Clinical Pearl:** All patients with cancer should be referred to an appropriate specialized center as soon as pos sible when the diagnosis of cancer is suspected.
- **Key Genes:** `ALK`, `APC`, `CD19`, `CD20`, `CD30`, `GD2`, `NUDT15`
### [Chapter 544: The Leukemias](../chapters/544.md)
- **Core Clinical Pearl:** Most cases of ALL are thought to be caused by post-conception somatic pathogenic gene variants in lymphoid cells.
- **Key Genes:** `CREBBP`, `CRLF2`, `EP300`, `GATA1`, `GATA2`, `IKZF1`, `KMT2A`, `KRAS`, `LYL1`, `NF1`, `NPM1`, `NRAS`
### [Chapter 545: Lymphoma](../chapters/545.md)

- **Key Genes:** `ALK`, `ARID1A`, `BTK`, `CREBBP`, `EZH2`, `G6PD`, `NOTCH2`, `SMARCA4`, `TCF3`, `TP53`

### [Chapter 547: Neuroblastoma](../chapters/547.md)
- **Core Clinical Pearl:** Therapeutic approaches are based on the specific histologic diagnosis and may incorporate radiation therapy, intrathecal administration of chemotherapy, and/or systemic administration of chemotherapy.
- **Key Genes:** `ALK`, `BARD1`, `FANCA`, `FANCC`, `FANCG`, `HRAS`, `MYCN`, `NF1`, `PHOX2B`, `TERT`
### [Chapter 548: Neoplasms of the Kidney](../chapters/548.md)
- **Core Clinical Pearl:** Children with intermediate-risk neu roblastoma, including children with L2 disease and infants with M disease both with favorable characteristics, have an excellent prognosis and >90% survival with this moderate treatmen
- **Key Genes:** `BLM`, `CTNNB1`, `DD4A`, `DICER1`, `EE4A`, `IGF2`, `MYCN`, `NSD1`, `SIX2`, `SMARCA4`, `SMARCB1`, `TARBP2`
### [Chapter 549: Soft Tissue Sarcomas](../chapters/549.md)
- **Core Clinical Pearl:** RHABDOMYOSARCOMA Epidemiology The most common pediatric soft tissue sarcoma, rhabdomyosarcoma, accounts for approximately 3.5% of childhood cancers.
- **Key Genes:** `APC`, `BUB1B`, `COL1A1`, `CREBBP`, `DICER1`, `FOXO1`, `HMGA2`, `MYCN`, `NF1`, `PSM2`, `PTPN11`, `RB1`
### [Chapter 550: Neoplasms of Bone](../chapters/550.md)
- **Core Clinical Pearl:** Osteosarcoma is the most common primary malignant bone tumor in children and adoles cents, followed by Ewing sarcoma.
- **Key Genes:** `EXT1`, `EXT2`, `FLI1`
### [Chapter 551: Retinoblastoma](../chapters/551.md)
- **Core Clinical Pearl:** The median age at diagnosis is approximately 2 years, and >90% of cases are diagnosed in children <5 years old.
- **Key Genes:** `RB1`
### [Chapter 552: Gonadal and Germ Cell Neoplasms](../chapters/552.md)
- **Core Clinical Pearl:** Metastatic disease is rarely pres ent at diagnosis; evaluation of the cerebrospinal fluid and bone marrow for tumor metastasis and radionuclide bone scan are required only if indicated by other clinical, laboratory, or i
- **Key Genes:** `DICER1`, `RB1`
### [Chapter 553: Neoplasms of the Liver](../chapters/553.md)
- **Core Clinical Pearl:** PROGNOSIS The overall cure rate for children with GCTs is >80%.
- **Key Genes:** `MDR3`, `TJP2`
### [Chapter 554: Complex Vascular Anomalies](../chapters/554.md)
- **Core Clinical Pearl:** A gross total resec tion should be attempted at diagnosis when possible; if not, neoadju vant chemotherapy should be given to convert nonresectable tumors into resectable ones.
- **Key Genes:** `AKT1`, `BRAF`, `EPHB4`, `GLA`, `GNA11`, `IDH1`, `IDH2`, `KRAS`, `PICK3CA`, `PIK3CA`, `PTEN`, `RASA1`
### [Chapter 555: Rare Tumors](../chapters/555.md)
- **Core Clinical Pearl:** Risk of local invasion and metastasis is quite high, so rapid diagnosis, surgical resection, and initiation of sarcoma-based chemo therapy is important.
- **Key Genes:** `BRAF`, `EGLN1`, `EGLN2`, `EPAS1`, `HIF2A`, `KIF1B`, `MEN1`, `NF1`, `RET`, `SDHAF2`, `TMEM127`, `TP53`
### [Chapter 556: Histiocytosis Syndromes of Childhood](../chapters/556.md)
- **Core Clinical Pearl:** The initial treatment of ACT and PHEO/PGL is resection by a sur geon experienced in the management of these tumors.
- **Key Genes:** `ALK`, `AP3B1`, `APC`, `AR`, `BRAF`, `BRAFV600E`, `CD27`, `CD70`, `CDC42`, `COG6`, `CTPS1`, `FHL1`
