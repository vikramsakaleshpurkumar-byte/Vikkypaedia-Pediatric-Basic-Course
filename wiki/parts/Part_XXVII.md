## Chapter Synthesis & Clinical Highlights
### [Chapter 658: Growth and Development of the Eye](../chapters/658.md)
- **Core Clinical Pearl:** In an infant, the sclera is thin and translucent, with a bluish tinge.
### [Chapter 659: Examination of the Eye](../chapters/659.md)
- **Core Clinical Pearl:** The most common visual acuity test in infants is an assessment of their ability to fixate and follow a target.
### [Chapter 660: Abnormalities of Refraction and Accommodation](../chapters/660.md)
- **Core Clinical Pearl:** Globally the prevalence of myopia appears to be increasing, leading to heightened interest in myopia prevention treatment.
- **Key Genes:** `RASGRF1`, `SLITRK6`
### [Chapter 661: Disorders of Vision](../chapters/661.md)
- **Core Clinical Pearl:** Severe visual impairment (corrected vision poorer than 6/60) and blindness in children have many etiologies and may be caused by multi ple defects affecting any structure or function along the visual pathways.
- **Key Genes:** `AIPL1`, `AR`, `CEP290`, `COL4A1`, `CRB1`, `CREBBP`, `CYP1B1`, `FOXC1`, `GDF6`, `GLIS3`, `GUCY2D`, `IMPDH1`
### [Chapter 662: Abnormalities of Pupil and Iris](../chapters/662.md)
- **Core Clinical Pearl:** The usual manifestation is a report of reduced visual acuity in one or both eyes.
- **Key Genes:** `ADAMTSL4`, `PAX6`, `WT1`
### [Chapter 663: Disorders of Eye Movement and Alignment](../chapters/663.md)
- **Core Clinical Pearl:** Diagnosis Many techniques are used to assess ocular alignment and movement of the eyes to aid in diagnosing strabismic disorders.
- **Key Genes:** `PAX6`
### [Chapter 664: Abnormalities of the Lids](../chapters/664.md)
- **Core Clinical Pearl:** Nelson Pediatric Symptom-Based Diagnosis. 2nd ed.
### [Chapter 665: Disorders of the Lacrimal System](../chapters/665.md)
- **Core Clinical Pearl:** CHALAZION A chalazion is a granulomatous inflammation of a meibomian gland characterized by a firm, nontender nodule in the upper or lower lid.
### [Chapter 666: Disorders of the Conjunctiva](../chapters/666.md)
- **Core Clinical Pearl:** Ophthalmia Neonatorum This form of conjunctivitis, occurring in infants younger than 4 weeks of age, is the most common eye disease of newborns.
- **Key Genes:** `AR`
### [Chapter 667: Abnormalities of the Cornea](../chapters/667.md)
- **Core Clinical Pearl:** It is a serious complication of Stevens-Johnson syndrome.
- **Key Genes:** `AR`, `CYP1B1`, `FOXC1`, `PAX6`, `PITX2`, `SLC4A11`, `STS`, `TGFB1`
### [Chapter 668: Abnormalities of the Lens](../chapters/668.md)
- **Core Clinical Pearl:** Differential Diagnosis The differential diagnosis of cataracts in infants and children includes a wide range of developmental disorders, infectious and inflammatory processes, metabolic diseases, and toxic and trau matic
### [Chapter 669: Disorders of the Uveal Tract](../chapters/669.md)
- **Core Clinical Pearl:** Treatment and Prognosis Displacement of the lens often results only in optical problems.
### [Chapter 670: Disorders of the Retina and Vitreous](../chapters/670.md)
- **Core Clinical Pearl:** Stage 1 is characterized by a demarcation line that separates vas cularized from avascular retina.
- **Key Genes:** `ABCA4`, `CTC1`, `ELOVL4`, `FZD4`, `LRP5`, `RPE65`
### [Chapter 671: Abnormalities of the Optic Nerve](../chapters/671.md)
- **Core Clinical Pearl:** Because the macula is generally unaffected, the visual prognosis is good.
- **Key Genes:** `FXN`, `HESX1`, `MTND1`, `MTND4`, `MTND6`, `ND4`, `PROKR2`, `SOX2`, `SOX3`, `WFS1`
### [Chapter 672: Childhood Glaucoma](../chapters/672.md)
- **Core Clinical Pearl:** Glaucoma is a general term used to indicate damage to the optic nerve with visual field loss that is caused by or related to elevated pressure within the eye.
- **Key Genes:** `NF1`
### [Chapter 673: Orbital Abnormalities](../chapters/673.md)
- **Core Clinical Pearl:** It may be caused by shallowness of the orbits, as in many craniofacial malformations, or by increased tissue mass within the orbit, as with neoplastic, vascular, and inflam matory disorders.
### [Chapter 674: Orbital Infections](../chapters/674.md)
- **Core Clinical Pearl:** Dacryocystitis may occur in newborns as a complication of a con genital dacryocystocele.
- **Key Genes:** `STS`
### [Chapter 675: Injuries to the Eye](../chapters/675.md)
- **Core Clinical Pearl:** The diagnosis requires vitreous sampling because aqueous samples are often negative.
- **Key Genes:** `AR`
