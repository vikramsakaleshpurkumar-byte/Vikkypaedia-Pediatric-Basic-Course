## Chapter Synthesis & Clinical Highlights
### [Chapter 685: Morphology of the Skin](../chapters/685.md)
- **Core Clinical Pearl:** Langer hans cells are dendritic cells of the mononuclear phagocyte system and are uniquely characterized by a specific organelle, the Birbeck granule, which resembles a tennis racket on electron microscopy.
### [Chapter 686: Dermatologic Evaluation of the Patient](../chapters/686.md)
- **Core Clinical Pearl:** Lichenification is a thickening of skin with accentuation of normal skin lines that is caused by chronic irritation (rubbing, scratching) or inflammation.
- **Key Genes:** `SCN9A`
### [Chapter 687: Principles of Dermatologic Therapy](../chapters/687.md)
- **Core Clinical Pearl:** Differential diagnosis includes generalized pustular psoriasis, bul lous impetigo, IgA pemphigus, and subcorneal pustular dermatosis.
### [Chapter 688: Dermatologic Diseases of the Neonate](../chapters/688.md)
- **Core Clinical Pearl:** At times, splotchy erythema is the only manifestation.
### [Chapter 689: Cutaneous Defects](../chapters/689.md)
- **Core Clinical Pearl:** The differential diagnosis in neonates includes transient neonatal pustular melanosis, erythema toxicum, milia, cutaneous candidiasis, and staphylococcal pustulosis.
- **Key Genes:** `AR`, `BMS1`
### [Chapter 690: Ectodermal Dysplasias](../chapters/690.md)
- **Core Clinical Pearl:** FOCAL DERMAL HYPOPLASIA (GOLTZ-GORLIN SYNDROME) A rare congenital mesoectodermal and ectodermal disorder, focal dermal hypoplasia is characterized by dysplasia of connective tissue in the skin and skeleton.
- **Key Genes:** `AR`, `CDH3`, `CYP26C1`, `DLX3`, `EDA`, `EDA1`, `EDAR`, `EDARADD`, `GJA1`, `GJB1`, `GJB2`, `GJB6`
### [Chapter 691: Vascular Anomalies](../chapters/691.md)
- **Core Clinical Pearl:** When a CM is lateral and localized to the forehead and upper eyelid, the diagnosis of Sturge-Weber syndrome (glaucoma, leptomeningeal venous angioma, seizures, hemiparesis contralateral to the facial lesion, intracranial
- **Key Genes:** `ACVRL1`, `AR`, `ATM`, `EPHB4`, `IDH1`, `IDH2`, `PTEN`, `RASA1`, `SMAD4`, `TIE2`, `TSC1`, `TSC2`
### [Chapter 692: Cutaneous Nevi](../chapters/692.md)
- **Core Clinical Pearl:** Prognosis and Treatment Acquired pigmented nevi are benign, but a very small percentage undergo malignant transformation.
- **Key Genes:** `AKT1`, `BRAF`, `FGFR2`, `GJB2`, `HRAS`, `KRAS`, `KRT2`, `NRAS`, `PIK3CA`, `PTEN`
### [Chapter 693: Hyperpigmented Lesions](../chapters/693.md)
- **Core Clinical Pearl:** Carney complex is an autosomal dom inant syndrome characterized by multiple lentigines and neoplasms, including myxomas of the skin, heart (atrial), and breast; psammoma tous melanotic schwannomas; epithelioid blue nevi 
- **Key Genes:** `AR`, `ATM`, `BRAF`, `FANCA`, `GNAS`, `GNAS1`, `MAP2K1`, `NF1`, `NF2`, `PRKAR1A`, `PTEN`, `PTPN11`
### [Chapter 694: Hypopigmented Lesions](../chapters/694.md)
- **Core Clinical Pearl:** These disorders are autosomal recessive in inheri tance and result from pathogenic variants in genes related to mela nin synthesis or the transport and storage of melanin within cells.
- **Key Genes:** `AR`, `ATM`, `BRAF`, `EDN3`, `FANCA`, `GNAS`, `HMGA2`, `HPS1`, `HPS4`, `HPS5`, `HPS6`, `LYST`
### [Chapter 695: Vesiculobullous Disorders](../chapters/695.md)
- **Core Clinical Pearl:** ETIOLOGY Among the numerous factors implicated in the etiology of erythema multiforme (EM), infection with herpes simplex virus (HSV) is the most common.
- **Key Genes:** `COL17A1`, `COL7A1`, `FERMT1`, `ITGA6`, `ITGB4`, `KIND1`, `LAMA3`, `LAMB3`, `LAMC2`
### [Chapter 696: Eczematous Disorders](../chapters/696.md)
- **Core Clinical Pearl:** DIFFERENTIAL DIAGNOSIS The eruption can be distinguished by histopathologic and immuno fluorescence studies from PV, BP, DH, scabies, and EM.
### [Chapter 697: Photosensitivity](../chapters/697.md)
- **Core Clinical Pearl:** ACUTE SUNBURN REACTION The most common photosensitive reaction seen in children is acute sunburn, which is caused mainly by UVB radiation.
- **Key Genes:** `RECQL4`

### [Chapter 700: Diseases of the Dermis](../chapters/700.md)
- **Core Clinical Pearl:** Mal De Meleda (SLURP-1 Gene) A rare, progressive autosomal recessive condition, mal de Meleda is characterized by erythema and thick scales on the palms, fingers, soles, and flexor aspects of the wrists, knees, and elbow
- **Key Genes:** `ABCC6`, `DSG1`, `DSP`, `KRT1`
### [Chapter 701: Diseases of Subcutaneous Tissue](../chapters/701.md)
- **Core Clinical Pearl:** RAYNAUD PHENOMENON An exaggerated vascular response to cold temperatures or emotional stress, Raynaud phenomenon is characterized by sudden-onset, sharply demarcated, transient color changes of the skin of the digits.
- **Key Genes:** `AGL`
### [Chapter 702: Disorders of the Sweat Glands](../chapters/702.md)
- **Core Clinical Pearl:** ANHIDROSIS Neuropathic anhidrosis results from a disturbance in the neural path way from the control center in the brain to the peripheral efferent nerve fibers that activate sweating.
- **Key Genes:** `FGFR2`, `GJB2`, `MEFV`, `MVK`, `NLRP3`, `NOD2`, `PSTPIP1`
### [Chapter 703: Disorders of Hair](../chapters/703.md)
- **Core Clinical Pearl:** Acquired localized hair loss is the most common type of hair loss in childhood.
- **Key Genes:** `ATP7A`, `BCS1L`, `CREBBP`, `EP300`, `HDAC8`, `INSR`, `KRT81`, `KRT83`, `KRT86`, `NIPBL`, `PKRY5`, `RAD21`
### [Chapter 704: Disorders of the Nails](../chapters/704.md)
- **Core Clinical Pearl:** A girl with unruly locks: molecular genetics makes a diagnosis of uncomb able hair syndrome.
- **Key Genes:** `LMX1B`, `TGM3`
### [Chapter 705: Disorders of the Mucous Membranes](../chapters/705.md)
- **Core Clinical Pearl:** Periungual fibromas that appear in late childhood should suggest a diagnosis of tuberous sclerosis.
- **Key Genes:** `PTEN`
### [Chapter 706: Cutaneous Bacterial Infections](../chapters/706.md)
- **Core Clinical Pearl:** ETIOLOGY/PATHOGENESIS Impetigo is the most common skin infection in children throughout the world.
### [Chapter 707: Cutaneous Fungal Infections](../chapters/707.md)
- **Core Clinical Pearl:** Diagnosis is made by culturing the organism at 32–33°C (89.6–91.4°F).
- **Key Genes:** `ADA`, `AIRE`, `DOCK8`, `IL2RG`, `JAK3`, `NEMO`, `RAG1`, `RAG2`, `STAT3`
### [Chapter 708: Cutaneous Viral Infections](../chapters/708.md)
- **Core Clinical Pearl:** Plantar warts (verruca plantaris), although similar to the common wart, are caused by HPV type 1 and are usually flush with the surface of the sole because of the constant pressure from weight bearing.
- **Key Genes:** `ADA`, `ATM`, `DOCK8`, `GATA2`, `NEMO`
### [Chapter 709: Arthropod Bites and Infestations](../chapters/709.md)
- **Core Clinical Pearl:** Arthropod bites are a common affliction of children and occasionally pose a problem in diagnosis.
### [Chapter 710: Acne](../chapters/710.md)
- **Core Clinical Pearl:** The eruption appears to be caused by an allergic hypersensitivity reaction to venom from larvae of the thimble jellyfish (Linuche unguiculata).
### [Chapter 711: Tumors of the Skin](../chapters/711.md)
- **Core Clinical Pearl:** They are reten tion cysts caused by hyperproliferation of injured epithelium and are indistinguishable histopathologically from primary milia.
- **Key Genes:** `AKT1`, `F8`, `KRT17`, `PIK3CA`, `PTCH1`, `SUFU`
### [Chapter 712: Nutritional Dermatoses](../chapters/712.md)
- **Core Clinical Pearl:** The facies of patients with this syndrome are characterized by tem poroparietal bossing, prominent supraorbital ridges, a broad nasal root, ocular hypertelorism or dystopia canthorum, and prognathism.
- **Key Genes:** `RET`, `SLC39A4`
