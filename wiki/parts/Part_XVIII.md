### [Chapter 486: Infective Endocarditis*](../chapters/486.md)

- **Core Clinical Pearl:** The diagnosis of ARF requires the fulfillment of the Jones criteria, with carditis being a major criterion.
### [Chapter 488: Diseases of the Myocardium](../chapters/488.md)

- **Key Genes:** `AR`, `DMD`, `FBXL4`, `GTPBP3`, `MYBPC3`, `MYH7`, `SURF1`, `TAZ`

### [Chapter 490: Tumors of the Heart](../chapters/490.md)
- **Core Clinical Pearl:** Rhabdomyomas are the most common pediatric cardiac tumors and are associated with tuberous sclerosis in 70–95% of cases.
- **Key Genes:** `PRKAR1A`, `PTCH1`
### [Chapter 491: Heart Failure](../chapters/491.md)
- **Core Clinical Pearl:** In pediatric patients, Wilms tumor and lymphoma/leukemia are the most common causes of such secondary tumors.
### [Chapter 492: Pediatric Heart and Heart-Lung Transplantation](../chapters/492.md)
- **Core Clinical Pearl:** PERIOPERATIVE MANAGEMENT Heart transplantation in children can be performed either with a biatrial or bicaval anastomosis.
### [Chapter 493: Diseases of the Blood Vessels (aneurysms and Fistulas)](../chapters/493.md)
- **Core Clinical Pearl:** The gold standard for ACR is tissue diag nosis, generally performed with transbronchial biopsies.
- **Key Genes:** `ABCC6`, `ACTA2`, `ATP7A`, `ENPP1`, `FBN1`, `SCL2A10`, `SLC2A10`, `SMAD3`, `TGFBR1`, `TGFBR2`
### [Chapter 494: Systemic Hypertension](../chapters/494.md)
- **Core Clinical Pearl:** Severe and symptomatic hypertension in children is usually caused by secondary hypertension.
- **Key Genes:** `CUL3`, `CYP11B1`, `CYP17A1`, `NF1`, `RET`, `SCNN1A`, `SCNN1B`, `SCNN1G`, `VHL`
