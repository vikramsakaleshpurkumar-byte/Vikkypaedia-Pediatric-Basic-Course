## Chapter Synthesis & Clinical Highlights
### [Chapter 585: Gynecologic History and Physical Examination](../chapters/585.md)
- **Core Clinical Pearl:** Congenital adrenal hyperplasia is the most common cause of ambiguous genitalia (accounting for >90% of cases), and the salt-wasting forms can lead to rapid dehydration with subsequent fluid and electrolyte imbalance.
### [Chapter 586: Vulvovaginitis](../chapters/586.md)
- **Core Clinical Pearl:** After the examination, it is appropriate to review the exam find ings with the teen (and parents or caregivers as appropriate) and initiate a collaborative discussion of the management plan.
### [Chapter 587: Vaginal Bleeding in the Prepubertal Child](../chapters/587.md)
- **Core Clinical Pearl:** Lichen sclerosus is characterized by chronic inflammation, intense pruritus, loss of normal architecture, and thin ning and whitening of vulvar and perianal skin, often in a butterfly or keyhole distribution.
### [Chapter 588: Breast Health](../chapters/588.md)
- **Core Clinical Pearl:** A detailed history and thorough physical examination must be done to identify the source of bleeding and for a management plan to be established efficiently.
- **Key Genes:** `BRCA1`, `BRCA2`, `PTEN`
### [Chapter 589: Polycystic Ovary Syndrome and Hirsutism](../chapters/589.md)
- **Core Clinical Pearl:** Laboratory Findings, Diagnosis, and Differential Diagnosis The diagnosis of PCOS requires exclusion of disorders that would otherwise account for hyperandrogenism and anovulation.
### [Chapter 590: Gynecologic Neoplasms and Prevention Methods for Human Papillomavirus Infections in Adolescents](../chapters/590.md)
- **Core Clinical Pearl:** Patients can be primarily distinguished by evidence of an ovulatory disorder by menstrual history, and for those with absent or irregular menses, a diagnosis of PCOS can be made.
- **Key Genes:** `BRCA1`, `BRCA2`, `DICER1`, `OTC`
### [Chapter 591: Vulvovaginal and Müllerian Anomalies](../chapters/591.md)
- **Core Clinical Pearl:** Overall, data shows that prevention strate gies such as widespread implementation of HPV vaccine translates into reduced incidence of cervical cancer in the future.
- **Key Genes:** `AMH`, `AMHR2`, `EMX2`, `HNF1B`, `HOXA10`, `HOXA11`, `HOXA13`, `HOXA9`, `LIM1`, `PAX8`, `SRY`, `WNT4`
### [Chapter 592: Gynecologic Care for Adolescents with Special Needs](../chapters/592.md)
- **Core Clinical Pearl:** Education regarding HIV and other sexually transmitted infections, disease prevention, and contraception, includ ing emergency contraception, should be offered at a developmentally appropriate level.
### [Chapter 593: Female Genital Mutilation](../chapters/593.md)
- **Core Clinical Pearl:** CLINICAL MANAGEMENT OF FGM Most pediatricians will not see a child who is acutely unwell due to FGM.
