## Chapter Synthesis & Clinical Highlights
### [Chapter 750: Rehabilitation for Traumatic Brain Injury](../chapters/750.md)
- **Core Clinical Pearl:** Falls (35.1%) and being struck by or against an object (34.9%) are most common in those 5-14 years of age.
- **Key Genes:** `PTS`
### [Chapter 751: Spinal Cord Injury and Autonomic Dysreflexia Management](../chapters/751.md)
- **Core Clinical Pearl:** The most common manifestations include bradycardia, hypo tension, temperature dysregulation, and, once spinal shock has resolved, autonomic dysreflexia (AD).
### [Chapter 752: Spasticity](../chapters/752.md)
- **Core Clinical Pearl:** Spasticity is a component of the upper motor neuron syndrome characterized by velocity-dependent resistance to passive range of motion, resulting in tonic stretch reflexes and accompanied by exag gerated tendon jerks.
### [Chapter 753: Birth Brachial Plexus Palsy](../chapters/753.md)
- **Core Clinical Pearl:** It is by far the most common injury seen in BBPP.
### [Chapter 754: Meningomyelocele (Spina Bifida)](../chapters/754.md)
- **Core Clinical Pearl:** Fractures of the lower extremities are most common in the femur, followed by the tibia.
### [Chapter 755: Upper and Lower Extremity Assistive Devices](../chapters/755.md)
- **Core Clinical Pearl:** The type of lower-extremity orthosis prescribed is based on the child’s diagnosis, functional status, prognosis, and the goals of treat ment, with the prescription frequently modified over time as the child changes.
### [Chapter 756: Health and Wellness for Children with Disabilities](../chapters/756.md)
- **Core Clinical Pearl:** Health and wellness programs also include traditional preventive management strategies, such as anticipatory guidance.
