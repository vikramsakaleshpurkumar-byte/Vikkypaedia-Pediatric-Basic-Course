## Chapter Synthesis & Clinical Highlights
### [Chapter 104: An Approach to Inborn Errors of Metabolism](../chapters/104.md)
- **Core Clinical Pearl:** With some exceptions, the pre senting symptoms of most metabolic conditions lack the specificity to enable a definitive diagnosis without further evaluation.
- **Key Genes:** `ASL`, `CPS1`, `DGUOK`, `GLUD1`, `HADH`, `MMUT`, `MPV17`, `NAGS`, `OTC`, `PMM2`, `PNPO`, `POLG`
### [Chapter 105: Defects in Metabolism of Amino Acids](../chapters/105.md)
- **Core Clinical Pearl:** Deficiency of the enzyme phenylalanine hydroxylase (PAH) or one of its cofactors tetrahydrobiopterin (BH4) causes accumulation of phenylalanine and its metabolites in body fluids and in the brain.
- **Key Genes:** `ABCD4`, `ACAT1`, `ACAT2`, `ACSF3`, `AGXT`, `ALDH4A1`, `ALDH7A1`, `AR`, `ARG1`, `ASL`, `ASPA`, `ASS1`
- **OMIM IDs:** `#107930`, `#126455`, `#182125`, `#191290`, `#193001`, `#309850`, `#600225`, `#609312`, `#610045`
### [Chapter 106: Defects in Metabolism of Lipids](../chapters/106.md)
- **Core Clinical Pearl:** The most common early presentation is an acute epi sode of life-threatening coma, hepatic encephalopathy, and hypoglyce mia with or without hyperammonemia induced by a period of fasting or illness in the first 2-4 years 
- **Key Genes:** `ABCD1`, `ABCG5`, `ABCG8`, `ACADM`, `ACADS`, `ACADVL`, `ACOX1`, `AGPS`, `APOA1`, `APOC3`, `AR`, `ARSA`
### [Chapter 107: Defects in Metabolism of Carbohydrates](../chapters/107.md)
- **Core Clinical Pearl:** Myophosphorylase deficiency (type V, McArdle disease) is the most common in adolescents and adults.
- **Key Genes:** `AGA`, `AGL`, `ALDOB`, `ALG6`, `AR`, `ATP6AP2`, `ATP6V0A2`, `ATP6V1A`, `ATP6V1E1`, `BTD`, `CCDC115`, `CTSA`
- **OMIM IDs:** `#614921`
### [Chapter 108: Mitochondrial Disease Diagnosis](../chapters/108.md)
- **Core Clinical Pearl:** Primary mitochondrial disease results from defi cient RC function, which can be caused by pathogenic variants in genes that encode RC subunits, assembly factors or cofactors, components of mitochondrial DNA (mtDNA) metab
- **Key Genes:** `BTD`, `DGUOK`, `DLD`, `EMC1`, `ETFA`, `ETFB`, `ETFDH`, `FXN`, `HLCS`, `KCNQ2`, `MFN2`, `MPV17`
### [Chapter 109: Mucopolysaccharidoses](../chapters/109.md)
- **Core Clinical Pearl:** Mucopolysaccharidoses are autosomal recessive disorders, with the exception of Hunter disease (Hunter syndrome), which is X-linked recessive.
- **Key Genes:** `AR`, `ARSB`, `GALNS`, `GLB1`, `GNS`, `GUSB`, `HGSNAT`, `HYAL1`, `IDS`, `IDUA`, `NAGLU`, `SGSH`
### [Chapter 110: Disorders of Purine and Pyrimidine Metabolism](../chapters/110.md)
- **Core Clinical Pearl:** The disorder is autosomal recessive and caused by pathogenic variants in the SLC22A12 or SLC2A9 genes, which code for transporters important for renal retention of uric acid.
- **Key Genes:** `ABCG2`, `ADA`, `ADSSL1`, `AGL`, `AK1`, `AK2`, `ALDOB`, `AMPD1`, `AMPD2`, `AMPD3`, `APRT`, `CANT1`
### [Chapter 111: Hutchinson-Gilford Progeria Syndrome (Progeria)](../chapters/111.md)
- **Core Clinical Pearl:** The most common clinical features are shown in the top row.
- **Key Genes:** `LMNA`, `POLR3A`, `RECQL4`, `WRN`
### [Chapter 112: The Porphyrias](../chapters/112.md)
- **Core Clinical Pearl:** An erythroid-specific form, ALAS2, is deficient in X-linked sideroblastic anemia as a result of pathogenic variants of the ALAS2 gene on chromosome Xp11.2.
- **Key Genes:** `ALAS2`, `CYP1A1`, `CYP1A2`, `GATA1`, `HFE`
