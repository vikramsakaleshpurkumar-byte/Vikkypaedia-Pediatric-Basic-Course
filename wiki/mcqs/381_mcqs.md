### Question 1 (Clinical Pediatrics Vignette)
A pediatric patient is evaluated for the following clinical and diagnostic profile: Proton pump inhibitor; 1 mg/kg/dose twice a day; 1 mo. Which of the following conditions or syndromes is the most likely diagnosis?

- **A.** PROTON PUMP INHIBITORS
- **B.** H2-RECEPTOR ANTAGONISTS
- **C.** DURATION OF TREATMENT
- **D.** CYTOPROTECTIVE AGENTS

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: C** — DURATION OF TREATMENT

**Rationale:** **DURATION OF TREATMENT** is characterized by: Proton pump inhibitor; 1 mg/kg/dose twice a day; 1 mo.

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **PROTON PUMP INHIBITORS** is incorrect (characterized by Omeprazole; 1.0-3.3 mg/kg/day; weigh 20 kg: 20 mg/day; Approved for use in those older than 1 month;). **H2-RECEPTOR ANTAGONISTS** is incorrect (characterized by Ranitidine; 4-10 mg/kg/day divided 2 or 3 × a day; Pulled from market; Famotidine; 1-2 mg/kg/day div). **CYTOPROTECTIVE AGENTS** is incorrect (characterized by Sucralfate; 40-80 mg/kg/day; Suspension: 1,000 mg/5 mL; Tablet: 1,000 mg).
