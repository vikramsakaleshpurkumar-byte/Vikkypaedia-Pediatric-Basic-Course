### Question 1 (Clinical Pediatrics Vignette)
A pediatric patient is evaluated for the following clinical and diagnostic profile: Acinetobacter; Bacillus; Burkholderia cepacia; Citrobacter; Corynebacterium; Enterobacter spp.; Enterococcus faecalis; Enterococcus faecium; Escherichia coli; Klebsiella spp.; Listeria monocytogenes; Mycobacterium spp.; Neisseria meningitidis; Nocard. Which of the following conditions or syndromes is the most likely diagnosis?

- **A.** BACTERIA, ANAEROBIC
- **B.** VIRUSES (Infections in Immunocompromised Persons)
- **C.** BACTERIA, AEROBIC (Infections in Immunocompromised Persons)
- **D.** FUNGI (Infections in Immunocompromised Persons)

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: C** — BACTERIA, AEROBIC (Infections in Immunocompromised Persons)

**Rationale:** **BACTERIA, AEROBIC (Infections in Immunocompromised Persons)** is characterized by: Acinetobacter; Bacillus; Burkholderia cepacia; Citrobacter; Corynebacterium; Enterobacter spp.; Enterococcus faecalis; Enterococcus faecium; Escherichia coli; Klebsiella spp.; Listeria monocytogenes; Mycobacterium spp.; Neisseria meningitidis; Nocardia spp.; Pseudomonas aeruginosa; Staphylococcus aureus; Staphylococcus.

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **BACTERIA, ANAEROBIC** is incorrect (characterized by Bacillus; Clostridium; Fusobacterium; Peptococcus; Peptostreptococcus; Propionibacterium; Veillonell). **VIRUSES (Infections in Immunocompromised Persons)** is incorrect (characterized by Adenoviruses; Cytomegalovirus; Epstein-Barr virus; Herpes simplex virus; Human herpesvirus 6; Polyom). **FUNGI (Infections in Immunocompromised Persons)** is incorrect (characterized by Aspergillus; Candida albicans; Other Candida spp.; Cryptococcus neoformans; Fusarium spp.; Pneumocys).
