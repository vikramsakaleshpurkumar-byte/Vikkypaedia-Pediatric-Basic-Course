### Question 1 (Clinical Pediatrics Vignette)

Which of the following conditions or syndromes is the most likely diagnosis?

- **A.** GJB2 (Cutaneous Nevi)
- **B.** CONVENTIONAL MELANOMA
- **C.** AKT1 (Cutaneous Nevi)
- **D.** PTEN (Cutaneous Nevi)

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: B** — CONVENTIONAL MELANOMA

**Rationale:** **CONVENTIONAL MELANOMA** is characterized by: Clinical; Papule or nodule; frequently; amelanotic, but can be any color; (e.g., pink-red to blue-black);; distribution is not limited to sun-; exposed skin; New, rapidly growing nodule arising; in the deep dermis or subcutaneous; tissues of a CMN; commonly solitary; and ulcerated; by comparison,; proliferative nodules.

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **GJB2 (Cutaneous Nevi)** is incorrect (characterized by Inflammatory linear verrucous epidermal; nevus; Not identified to date). **AKT1 (Cutaneous Nevi)** is incorrect (characterized by PIK3CA-related overgrowth spectrum/). **PTEN (Cutaneous Nevi)** is incorrect (characterized by Proteus syndrome and epidermal nevi).
