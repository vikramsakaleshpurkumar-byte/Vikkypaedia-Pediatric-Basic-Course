### Question 1 (Clinical Pediatrics Vignette)
A pediatric patient is evaluated for the following clinical and diagnostic profile: Adolescent females; somatic the causative gene (nuclear β-catenin+, CD10+); mixed solid-cystic encapsulated mass; >95% cure with surgical resection. Which of the following conditions or syndromes is the most likely diagnosis?

- **A.** Pancreatoblastoma (Pancreatic Tumors)
- **B.** Insulinoma (PanNET)
- **C.** Solid Pseudopapillary Neoplasm (SPN / Frantz Tumor)
- **D.** Gastrinoma (Zollinger-Ellison Syndrome)

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: C** — Solid Pseudopapillary Neoplasm (SPN / Frantz Tumor)

**Rationale:** **Solid Pseudopapillary Neoplasm (SPN / Frantz Tumor)** (associated with **`CTNNB1`**) is characterized by: Adolescent females; somatic CTNNB1 (nuclear β-catenin+, CD10+); mixed solid-cystic encapsulated mass; >95% cure with surgical resection.

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **Pancreatoblastoma (Pancreatic Tumors)** is incorrect (characterized by Children 1–8 yr; Beckwith-Wiedemann & APC associations; elevated serum AFP; histologic squamoid corp). **Insulinoma (PanNET)** is incorrect (characterized by MEN1 (MEN1 gene, 11q13) association; Whipple triad with inappropriate insulin & C-peptide during hyp). **Gastrinoma (Zollinger-Ellison Syndrome)** is incorrect (characterized by MEN1 association; fasting hypergastrinemia (>1,000 pg/mL), secretin stimulation test+, refractory je).

### Question 3 (Pediatric Genetics & Molecular Etiology)
A child is diagnosed with **Solid Pseudopapillary Neoplasm (SPN / Frantz Tumor)** after presenting with Adolescent females; somatic the causative gene (nuclear β-catenin+, CD10+); mixed solid-cystic encapsulated mass; >95% cure with surgical resection. Pathogenic variants in which of the following genes are most classically implicated?

- **A.** TSC2
- **B.** PLEC
- **C.** CTNNB1
- **D.** LYST

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: C** — CTNNB1

**Rationale:** Pathogenic variants in **`CTNNB1`** (The Digestive System) are associated with **Solid Pseudopapillary Neoplasm (SPN / Frantz Tumor)** (Adolescent females; somatic CTNNB1 (nuclear β-catenin+, CD10+); mixed solid-cystic encapsulated mass; >95% cure with surgical resection).

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **TSC2** is incorrect (associated with TSC2 (Central Nervous System Tumors in Childhood)). **LYST** is incorrect (associated with (CHS) (Disorders of Phagocyte Function)).
