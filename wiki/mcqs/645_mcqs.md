### Question 1 (Clinical Pediatrics Vignette)
A pediatric patient is evaluated for the following clinical and diagnostic profile: Wiskott-Aldrich syndrome; Iron-deficiency anemia; Aplastic anemia; Sickle cell disease; Polycythemia; Bone marrow transplantation and associated treatments; Prothrombotic states; Fanconi anemia; Hemolytic anemia. Which of the following conditions or syndromes is the most likely diagnosis?

- **A.** CONNECTIVE TISSUE DISORDERS
- **B.** INFECTIONS (Idiopathic Intracranial Hypertension (Pseudotumor Cerebri))
- **C.** ENDOCRINE DISORDERS
- **D.** HEMATOLOGIC DISORDERS

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: D** — HEMATOLOGIC DISORDERS

**Rationale:** **HEMATOLOGIC DISORDERS** is characterized by: Wiskott-Aldrich syndrome; Iron-deficiency anemia; Aplastic anemia; Sickle cell disease; Polycythemia; Bone marrow transplantation and associated treatments; Prothrombotic states; Fanconi anemia; Hemolytic anemia.

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **CONNECTIVE TISSUE DISORDERS** is incorrect (characterized by Ehlers-Danlos syndrome; Marfan syndrome). **INFECTIONS (Idiopathic Intracranial Hypertension (Pseudotumor Cerebri))** is incorrect (characterized by Acute sinusitis; Otitis media (lateral sinus thrombosis); Mastoiditis; Tonsillitis; Measles; Roseola). **ENDOCRINE DISORDERS** is incorrect (characterized by Primary congenital hypothyroidism; Congenital adrenal hyperplasia).

</details>
