### Question 1 (Clinical Pediatrics Vignette)
A pediatric patient is evaluated for the following clinical and diagnostic profile: Intestinal malabsorption of fats may accompany cystic fibrosis, biliary atresia, or other liver diseases and results in a deficiency of fatsoluble dietary vitamins including vitamin K.. Which of the following conditions or syndromes is the most likely diagnosis?

- **A.** COVID-19 (Thrombotic Disorders in Children)
- **B.** Postneonatal Vitamin K Deficiency
- **C.** VII (Mucopolysaccharidoses)
- **D.** THERAPEUTIC MEASURES

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: B** — Postneonatal Vitamin K Deficiency

**Rationale:** **Postneonatal Vitamin K Deficiency** is characterized by: Intestinal malabsorption of fats may accompany cystic fibrosis, biliary atresia, or other liver diseases and results in a deficiency of fatsoluble dietary vitamins including vitamin K..

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **COVID-19 (Thrombotic Disorders in Children)** is incorrect (characterized by Antiphospholipid antibody syndrome). **VII (Mucopolysaccharidoses)** is incorrect (characterized by Intellectual disability; +). **THERAPEUTIC MEASURES** is incorrect (characterized by 21-Hydroxylase; deficiency, classic form).
