### Question 1 (Clinical Pediatrics Vignette)
A pediatric patient is evaluated for the following clinical and diagnostic profile: Propionic acidemia; Methylmalonic acidemia (methylmalonyl-CoA mutase); Methylmalonic acidemia (cobalamin disorders); Isovaleric acidemia; 3-Methylcrotonyl-CoA carboxylase deficiency; 3-Hydroxy-3-methyglutaric aciduria; Holocarboxylase synthase defici. Which of the following conditions or syndromes is the most likely diagnosis?

- **A.** ORGANIC ACID METABOLISM DISORDERS
- **B.** HEMOGLOBINOPATHIES
- **C.** DISORDERS OF FATTY ACID METABOLISM
- **D.** DISORDERS OF ORGANIC ACID METABOLISM

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: D** — DISORDERS OF ORGANIC ACID METABOLISM

**Rationale:** **DISORDERS OF ORGANIC ACID METABOLISM** is characterized by: Propionic acidemia; Methylmalonic acidemia (methylmalonyl-CoA mutase); Methylmalonic acidemia (cobalamin disorders); Isovaleric acidemia; 3-Methylcrotonyl-CoA carboxylase deficiency; 3-Hydroxy-3-methyglutaric aciduria; Holocarboxylase synthase deficiency; β-Ketothiolase deficiency; Glutaric acidemia type I.

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **ORGANIC ACID METABOLISM DISORDERS** is incorrect (characterized by Methylmalonic acidemia with homocystinuria; Malonic acidemia; 2-Methyl-3-hydroxybutyric aciduria; Is). **HEMOGLOBINOPATHIES** is incorrect (characterized by Sickle cell anemia (hemoglobin SS disease); Hemoglobin S/β-thalassemia; Hemoglobin S/C disease). **DISORDERS OF FATTY ACID METABOLISM** is incorrect (characterized by Medium-chain acyl-CoA dehydrogenase deficiency; Very-long-chain acyl-CoA dehydrogenase deficiency; L).
