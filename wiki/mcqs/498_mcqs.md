### Question 1 (Clinical Pediatrics Vignette)
A pediatric patient is evaluated for the following clinical and diagnostic profile: PROGNOSIS DBA has been identified as a cancer predisposition syndrome because of the higher risk of myelodysplastic syndrome, acute myeloid leukemia, colon carcinoma, osteogenic sarcoma, and female genital cancers.. Which of the following conditions or syndromes is the most likely diagnosis?

- **A.** CONSIDER (Hyperpigmented Lesions)
- **B.** TP53 (Soft Tissue Sarcomas)
- **C.** Pearson Syndrome
- **D.** CLCNKB (Inherited Tubular Transport Abnormalities)

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: C** — Pearson Syndrome

**Rationale:** **Pearson Syndrome** (associated with **`ADA`**) is characterized by: PROGNOSIS DBA has been identified as a cancer predisposition syndrome because of the higher risk of myelodysplastic syndrome, acute myeloid leukemia, colon carcinoma, osteogenic sarcoma, and female genital cancers..

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **CONSIDER (Hyperpigmented Lesions)** is incorrect (characterized by Ataxia telangiectasia). **TP53 (Soft Tissue Sarcomas)** is incorrect (characterized by Mosaic variegated aneuploidy; syndrome). **CLCNKB (Inherited Tubular Transport Abnormalities)** is incorrect (characterized by ClC-Kb; AR; many; sporadic; Variable age at presentation with severity corresponding; to type of gen).
