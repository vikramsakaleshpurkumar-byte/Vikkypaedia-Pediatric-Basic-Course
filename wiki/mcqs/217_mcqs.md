### Question 1 (Clinical Pediatrics Vignette)
A pediatric patient is evaluated for the following clinical and diagnostic profile: Otitis media; Yes; Sinusitis; Probably; Pharyngitis; Probably; Pneumonia; Yes. Which of the following conditions or syndromes is the most likely diagnosis?

- **A.** HERPESVIRUS INFECTIONS
- **B.** GASTROINTESTINAL TRACT INFECTIONS
- **C.** ASEPTIC MENINGITIS
- **D.** RESPIRATORY TRACT INFECTIONS

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: D** — RESPIRATORY TRACT INFECTIONS

**Rationale:** **RESPIRATORY TRACT INFECTIONS** is characterized by: Otitis media; Yes; Sinusitis; Probably; Pharyngitis; Probably; Pneumonia; Yes.

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **HERPESVIRUS INFECTIONS** is incorrect (characterized by Cytomegalovirus; Yes; Varicella-zoster virus; Yes; Herpes simplex virus; Probably). **GASTROINTESTINAL TRACT INFECTIONS** is incorrect (characterized by Diarrhea (rotavirus, norovirus,; calicivirus, astrovirus, enteric; adenovirus, Giardia lamblia,; Cry). **ASEPTIC MENINGITIS** is incorrect (characterized by Enteroviruses; Probably).
