### Question 1 (Clinical Pediatrics Vignette)
A pediatric patient is evaluated for the following clinical and diagnostic profile: Immune thrombocytopenias; Acute and chronic ITP; Autoimmune diseases with chronic ITP as a manifestation; • Cyclic thrombocytopenia; • Autoimmune lymphoproliferative syndrome and its variants; • Systemic lupus erythematosus; • Evans syndrome; • Antip. Which of the following conditions or syndromes is the most likely diagnosis?

- **A.** Primary Platelet Consumption Syndromes
- **B.** DEFECTS IN PLATELET ADHESION
- **C.** ASSOCIATION WITH OTHER DISEASES
- **D.** SEQUESTRATION (Platelet and Blood Vessel Disorders)

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: A** — Primary Platelet Consumption Syndromes

**Rationale:** **Primary Platelet Consumption Syndromes** is characterized by: Immune thrombocytopenias; Acute and chronic ITP; Autoimmune diseases with chronic ITP as a manifestation; • Cyclic thrombocytopenia; • Autoimmune lymphoproliferative syndrome and its variants; • Systemic lupus erythematosus; • Evans syndrome; • Antiphospholipid antibody syndrome; • Neoplasia-associated immune thrombocy.

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **DEFECTS IN PLATELET ADHESION** is incorrect (characterized by Bernard-Soulier syndrome). **ASSOCIATION WITH OTHER DISEASES** is incorrect (characterized by Fanconi anemia; Congenital amegakaryocytic thrombocytopenia; Shwachman-Diamond syndrome; Hemophagocy). **SEQUESTRATION (Platelet and Blood Vessel Disorders)** is incorrect (characterized by Hypersplenism; Hypothermia; Burns).

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **SEQUESTRATION (Platelet and Blood Vessel Disorders)** is incorrect (characterized by Hypersplenism; Hypothermia; Burns). **IMPAIRED PLATELET PRODUCTION** is incorrect (characterized by Hereditary disorders; Acquired disorders; • Aplastic anemia; • Myelodysplastic syndrome; • Marrow in). **DEFECTS IN PLATELET ADHESION** is incorrect (characterized by Bernard-Soulier syndrome).
