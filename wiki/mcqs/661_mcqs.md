### Question 1 (Clinical Pediatrics Vignette)

Which of the following conditions or syndromes is the most likely diagnosis?

- **A.** CONGENITAL (Disorders of Vision)
- **B.** NEURODEGENERATIVE DISEASES
- **C.** PHAKOMATOSES (Disorders of Vision)
- **D.** TUMORS (Disorders of Vision)

**Correct Answer: A** — CONGENITAL (Disorders of Vision)

**Rationale:** **CONGENITAL (Disorders of Vision)** is characterized by: Optic nerve hypoplasia or aplasia; Holoprosencephaly; Septo-optic dysplasia; Optic coloboma; Congenital hydrocephalus; Hydranencephaly; Porencephaly; Microcephaly; Encephalocele, particularly occipital; Morning glory disc; Aniridia; Microphthalmia/anophthalmia syndromes; Persistent pupillary membrane; Cataracts; Persis.

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **NEURODEGENERATIVE DISEASES** is incorrect (characterized by Cerebral storage disease; Gangliosidoses, particularly Tay-Sachs disease, Sandhoff variant,; general). **PHAKOMATOSES (Disorders of Vision)** is incorrect (characterized by Tuberous sclerosis; Neurofibromatosis (special association with optic glioma); Sturge-Weber syndrome). **TUMORS (Disorders of Vision)** is incorrect (characterized by Retinoblastoma; Optic glioma; Perioptic meningioma; Craniopharyngioma; Cerebral glioma; Astrocytoma;).
