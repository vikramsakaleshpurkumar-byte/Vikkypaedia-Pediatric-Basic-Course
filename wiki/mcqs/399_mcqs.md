### Question 1 (Clinical Pediatrics Vignette)
A pediatric patient is evaluated for the following clinical and diagnostic profile: Acetaminophen overdose; Alcohol; Anabolic androgenic steroids; l-Asparaginase; Azathioprine; Cannabis; Carbamazepine; Cimetidine; Cisplatin; Corticosteroids; Cytosine arabinoside; Dapsone; Didanosine; Enalapril; Erythromycin; Estrogen; Furosemide; Gl. Which of the following conditions or syndromes is the most likely diagnosis?

- **A.** COVID-19 (Pancreatitis)
- **B.** OBSTRUCTIVE (Pancreatitis)
- **C.** DRUGS AND TOXINS (Pancreatitis)
- **D.** TRAUMATIC (Pancreatitis)

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: C** — DRUGS AND TOXINS (Pancreatitis)

**Rationale:** **DRUGS AND TOXINS (Pancreatitis)** (associated with **`DDC`**) is characterized by: Acetaminophen overdose; Alcohol; Anabolic androgenic steroids; l-Asparaginase; Azathioprine; Cannabis; Carbamazepine; Cimetidine; Cisplatin; Corticosteroids; Cytosine arabinoside; Dapsone; Didanosine; Enalapril; Erythromycin; Estrogen; Furosemide; Glucagon-like peptide-1 agents; Interferon-α; Isoniazid; Lamivudine; Lis.

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **COVID-19 (Pancreatitis)** is incorrect (characterized by Coxsackie B virus; Echovirus; Enterovirus; Epstein-Barr virus; Hepatitides A, B; Herpes viruses; Inf). **OBSTRUCTIVE (Pancreatitis)** is incorrect (characterized by Ampullary disease; Ascariasis; Biliary tract malformations; Choledochal cyst; Choledochocele; Cholel). **TRAUMATIC (Pancreatitis)** is incorrect (characterized by Blunt injury; Burns; Child abuse; Hypothermia; Surgical trauma; Total-body cast).

### Question 2 (Clinical Pediatrics Vignette)

Which of the following conditions or syndromes is the most likely diagnosis?

- **A.** COVID-19 (Pancreatitis)
- **B.** GENETIC (Pancreatitis)
- **C.** SYSTEMIC DISEASE (Pancreatitis)
- **D.** TRAUMATIC (Pancreatitis)

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: B** — GENETIC (Pancreatitis)

**Rationale:** **GENETIC (Pancreatitis)** (associated with **`CFTR, CTRC, PRSS1, SPINK1, CPA1`**) is characterized by: Cationic trypsinogen gene (PRSS1); Carboxypeptidase A1 (CPA1); Chymotrypsin C gene (CTRC); Cystic fibrosis gene (CFTR); Trypsin inhibitor gene (SPINK1).

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **COVID-19 (Pancreatitis)** is incorrect (characterized by Coxsackie B virus; Echovirus; Enterovirus; Epstein-Barr virus; Hepatitides A, B; Herpes viruses; Inf). **SYSTEMIC DISEASE (Pancreatitis)** is incorrect (characterized by Autoimmune pancreatitis (IgG4-related systemic disease); Brain tumor; Collagen vascular diseases; Co). **TRAUMATIC (Pancreatitis)** is incorrect (characterized by Blunt injury; Burns; Child abuse; Hypothermia; Surgical trauma; Total-body cast).

### Question 3 (Pediatric Genetics & Molecular Etiology)
A child is diagnosed with **DRUGS AND TOXINS (Pancreatitis)** after presenting with Acetaminophen overdose; Alcohol; Anabolic androgenic steroids; l-Asparaginase; Azathioprine; Cannabis; Carbamazepine; Cimetidine; Cisplatin; Corticosteroids; Cytosine arabinoside; Dapsone; Didanosine; Enalapril. Pathogenic variants in which of the following genes are most classically implicated?

- **A.** WT1
- **B.** TPP1
- **C.** COL4A3
- **D.** DDC

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: D** — DDC

**Rationale:** Pathogenic variants in **`DDC`** (The Digestive System) are associated with **DRUGS AND TOXINS (Pancreatitis)** (Acetaminophen overdose; Alcohol; Anabolic androgenic steroids; l-Asparaginase; Azathioprine; Cannabis; Carbamazepine; Cimetidine; Cisplatin; Corticosteroids; Cytosine arabinoside; Dapsone; Didanosine; Enalapril; Erythromycin; Estrogen; Furosemide; Glucagon-like peptide-1 agents; Interferon-α; Isoniazid; Lamivudine; Lis).

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **WT1** is incorrect (associated with WT1-ASSOCIATED WILMS TUMOR PREDISPOSITION SYNDROMES).

</details>

### Question 5 (Pediatric Board Mastery (Pancreatitis))
When formulating an evidence-based diagnostic and management plan for **Pancreatitis** (*Part XVI: The Digestive System*), which of the following pediatric clinical statements is **ACCURATE**? (Item 1)

- **A.** The condition is an entirely benign physiologic variant that never warrants laboratory or clinical evaluation.
- **B.** Systemic tetracyclines and fluoroquinolones are universally required as routine prophylaxis in all healthy newborns.
- **C.** First-line management relies on immediate high-dose aspirin therapy in febrile infants.
- **D.** Acute pancreatitis (AP) is the most common pancreatic disorder in children; 50 or more cases are usually seen in major pediatric centers per year.

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: D** — Acute pancreatitis (AP) is the most common pancreatic disorder in children; 50 or more cases are usually seen in major pediatric centers per year.

**Rationale:** **Pediatric Clinical Principle (Pancreatitis):** Acute pancreatitis (AP) is the most common pancreatic disorder in children; 50 or more cases are usually seen in major pediatric centers per year.

</details>
