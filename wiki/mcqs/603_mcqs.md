### Question 1 (Clinical Pediatrics Vignette)

Which of the following conditions or syndromes is the most likely diagnosis?

- **A.** FGF8, FGFR1, GLI2, LEPR
- **B.** PRIMARY HYPOTHYROIDISM
- **C.** FOXE1 (Hypothyroidism)
- **D.** INCREASED TBG (Hypothyroidism)

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: D** — INCREASED TBG (Hypothyroidism)

**Rationale:** **INCREASED TBG (Hypothyroidism)** is characterized by: Androgens; Anabolic steroids; Glucocorticoids; Hepatocellular disease; Severe illness; Nephrotic syndrome; Protein-losing enteropathy; Nicotinic acid; l-Asparaginase; Estrogens; Selective estrogen receptor; modulators; Pregnancy; Hepatitis; Porphyria; Heroin, methadone; Mitotane; 5-Fluorouracil; Perphenazine.

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **FGF8, FGFR1, GLI2, LEPR** is incorrect (characterized by ACTH, Adrenocorticotropic hormone; FSH, follicle-stimulating hormone; GH, growth; hormone; LH, lutei). **PRIMARY HYPOTHYROIDISM** is incorrect (characterized by Defect of thyroid development (dysgenesis); • Agenesis; • Hypoplasia; • Ectopia; Defects in thyrotro). **FOXE1 (Hypothyroidism)** is incorrect (characterized by Athyreosis; Cleft palate, choanal atresia, kinky; hair, bifid epiglottis).

Which of the following conditions or syndromes is the most likely diagnosis?

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **FGF8, FGFR1, GLI2, LEPR** is incorrect (characterized by ACTH, Adrenocorticotropic hormone; FSH, follicle-stimulating hormone; GH, growth; hormone; LH, lutei). **CENTRAL (SECONDARY) HYPOTHYROIDISM** is incorrect (characterized by Isolated TSH deficiency; • Pathogenic variant in TSH β-subunit (TSHβ)—depending on; variant measured). **INCREASED TBG (Hypothyroidism)** is incorrect (characterized by Androgens; Anabolic steroids; Glucocorticoids; Hepatocellular disease; Severe illness; Nephrotic syn).

### Question 5 (Pediatric Board Mastery (Hypothyroidism))
When formulating an evidence-based diagnostic and management plan for **Hypothyroidism** (*Part XXIV: The Endocrine System*), which of the following pediatric clinical statements is **ACCURATE**? (Item 1)

- **A.** Congenital TBG deficiency is an X-linked recessive trait that occurs in 1 in 1,700 male newborns.

- **D.** Inheritance is exclusively holandric (Y-linked) with 100% penetrance restricted to male neonates.
