### Question 1 (Clinical Pediatrics Vignette)
A pediatric patient is evaluated for the following clinical and diagnostic profile: Emesis; Reflux; Volvulus; Pyloric stenosis; Hirschsprung disease; Imperforate anus. Which of the following conditions or syndromes is the most likely diagnosis?

- **A.** PFIC (Digestive System Disorders)
- **B.** NEC (Digestive System Disorders)
- **C.** HSV (Digestive System Disorders)
- **D.** SYSTEMIC DISORDERS

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: D** — SYSTEMIC DISORDERS

**Rationale:** **SYSTEMIC DISORDERS** is characterized by: Emesis; Reflux; Volvulus; Pyloric stenosis; Hirschsprung disease; Imperforate anus.

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **PFIC (Digestive System Disorders)** is incorrect (characterized by Alagille syndrome; Physiologic; Hemolysis). **NEC (Digestive System Disorders)** is incorrect (characterized by Meconium plug; Sepsis; Inborn error of metabolism; Congenital adrenal; hyperplasia; Increased intrac). **HSV (Digestive System Disorders)** is incorrect (characterized by Inborn error of metabolism; Abdominal; distention; Feeding intolerance; GI obstruction; Meconium plu).
