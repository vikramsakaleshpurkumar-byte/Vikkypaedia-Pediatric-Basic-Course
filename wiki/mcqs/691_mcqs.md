### Question 1 (Clinical Pediatrics Vignette)
A pediatric patient is evaluated for the following clinical and diagnostic profile: Congenital absence of sweat glands without ectodermal dysplasia; Incontinentia pigmenti; Burns. Which of the following conditions or syndromes is the most likely diagnosis?

- **A.** MULTISYSTEM DISORDERS
- **B.** MEDICATIONS (Vascular Anomalies)
- **C.** NEUROLOGIC DISORDERS
- **D.** CUTANEOUS LESIONS (Vascular Anomalies)

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: D** — CUTANEOUS LESIONS (Vascular Anomalies)

**Rationale:** **CUTANEOUS LESIONS (Vascular Anomalies)** is characterized by: Congenital absence of sweat glands without ectodermal dysplasia; Incontinentia pigmenti; Burns.

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **MULTISYSTEM DISORDERS** is incorrect (characterized by Fabry disease; Crisponi syndrome; Chronic graft-versus-host disease; Sjögren syndrome). **MEDICATIONS (Vascular Anomalies)** is incorrect (characterized by Anticholinergic drugs; Opioids; Botulism toxin; Clonidine; Barbiturate overdose; α2-Receptor antagon). **NEUROLOGIC DISORDERS** is incorrect (characterized by Spinal cord injury; Guillain-Barré syndrome; Hereditary sensory autonomic neuropathy type I, II, IV;).
