### Question 1 (Clinical Pediatrics Vignette)
A pediatric patient is evaluated for the following clinical and diagnostic profile: It is prevalent in regions with temperate climates and is the most common helminth infection in the United States.. Which of the following conditions or syndromes is the most likely diagnosis?

- **A.** Acquired Disorders of the Nose
- **B.** Chromatin Regulatory Disorders
- **C.** BACH2 (Immune Dysregulation)
- **D.** Enterobiasis (Enterobius vermicularis)

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: D** — Enterobiasis (Enterobius vermicularis)

**Rationale:** **Enterobiasis (Enterobius vermicularis)** is characterized by: It is prevalent in regions with temperate climates and is the most common helminth infection in the United States..

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **Acquired Disorders of the Nose** is incorrect (characterized by DIAGNOSIS Unilateral nasal discharge and obstruction should suggest the presence of a foreign body, ). **Chromatin Regulatory Disorders** is incorrect (characterized by KABUKI SYNDROME Kabuki syndrome (MIM: 147920 and 300867) is a genetic disorder characterized by faci). **BACH2 (Immune Dysregulation)** is incorrect (characterized by BACH2 deficiency).

### Question 5 (Pediatric Board Mastery (Enterobiasis (Enterobius vermicularis)))

**Rationale:** **Pediatric Clinical Principle (Enterobiasis (Enterobius vermicularis)):** It is prevalent in regions with temperate climates and is the most common helminth infection in the United States.
