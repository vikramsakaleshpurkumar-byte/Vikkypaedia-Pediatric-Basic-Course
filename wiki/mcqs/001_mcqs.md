### Question 5 (Pediatric Board Mastery (Overview of Pediatrics))
When formulating an evidence-based diagnostic and management plan for **Overview of Pediatrics** (*Part I: The Field of Pediatrics*), which of the following pediatric clinical statements is **ACCURATE**? (Item 1)

- **A.** Diagnostic confirmation relies solely on plain skull radiographs without biochemical, microbiologic, or clinical correlation.
- **B.** The interconnectedness of health issues across the world has achieved widespread recognition in the wake of new and emerging ill nesses, such as COVID-19, Zika, Ebola, and severe acute respiratory syndrome
- **C.** The condition is an entirely benign physiologic variant that never warrants laboratory or clinical evaluation.
- **D.** Systemic tetracyclines and fluoroquinolones are universally required as routine prophylaxis in all healthy newborns.

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: B** — The interconnectedness of health issues across the world has achieved widespread recognition in the wake of new and emerging ill nesses, such as COVID-19, Zika, Ebola, and severe acute respiratory syndrome

**Rationale:** **Pediatric Clinical Principle (Overview of Pediatrics):** The interconnectedness of health issues across the world has achieved widespread recognition in the wake of new and emerging ill nesses, such as COVID-19, Zika, Ebola, and severe acute respiratory syndrome (SARS), as well as familiar and persistent illnesses, such as malaria, tuberculosis, HIV/AIDS, and vaccine-preventable illness.

</details>
