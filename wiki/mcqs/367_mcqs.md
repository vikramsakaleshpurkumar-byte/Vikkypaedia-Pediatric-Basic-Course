### Question 1 (Clinical Pediatrics Vignette)
A pediatric patient is evaluated for the following clinical and diagnostic profile: The most common presentation is respira tory distress caused by compression of the adjacent airways.. Which of the following conditions or syndromes is the most likely diagnosis?

- **A.** Dysmotility
- **B.** (ABCC9, KCNJ8) (Disorders of Hair)
- **C.** +++ (Psychopharmacology)
- **D.** CONGENITAL (Neuropathic Bladder)

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: A** — Dysmotility

**Rationale:** **Dysmotility** is characterized by: The most common presentation is respira tory distress caused by compression of the adjacent airways..

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **(ABCC9, KCNJ8) (Disorders of Hair)** is incorrect (characterized by Coffin-Siris syndrome (BAF complex genes); Cornelia de Lange (Brachmann-de Lange) syndrome (NIPBL, S). **+++ (Psychopharmacology)** is incorrect (characterized by Tardive; dyskinesia). **CONGENITAL (Neuropathic Bladder)** is incorrect (characterized by Neural tube defect; Occult forms of neural tube defect (lipomeningocele and other; spinal dysraphism).
