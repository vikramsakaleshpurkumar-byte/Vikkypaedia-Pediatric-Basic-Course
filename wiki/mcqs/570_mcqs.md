**Correct Answer: B** — GENERALIZED DISTAL DYSFUNCTION

**Rationale:** **GENERALIZED DISTAL DYSFUNCTION** is characterized by: Plasma [K+]; Low; Low; High; Urine pH with acidosis; 5.5; 5.5; Urine net charge; Negative; Positive; Positive; Fractional bicarbonate excretion; >10–15% during alkali therapy; 2–5%; 5–10%; U−BPco2; Normal; Low; Low; Response to therapy; Least responsive; Responsive; Less responsive; Associated features; Fanconi syndrom.

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **LABYRINTH (Congenital Malformations of the Ear)** is incorrect (characterized by Complete labyrinthine aplasia (Michel); Cochlear anomalies; Cochlear aplasia; Cochlear hypoplasia; I). **Nephrogenic Diabetes Insipidus** is incorrect (characterized by Acquired; X-linked (OMIM 304800); Autosomal recessive (OMIM 125800); Autosomal dominant (OMIM 125800). **Trichomoniasis (Trichomonas vaginalis)** is incorrect (characterized by The Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC) offer a molecular identification of Enterocytoz).

### Question 5 (Pediatric Board Mastery (Nephrogenic Diabetes Insipidus))
When formulating an evidence-based diagnostic and management plan for **Nephrogenic Diabetes Insipidus** (*Part XXI: Nephrology*), which of the following pediatric clinical statements is **ACCURATE**? (Item 1)

- **A.** Systemic tetracyclines and fluoroquinolones are universally required as routine prophylaxis in all healthy newborns.
- **B.** The prognosis of RTA depends to a large extent on the nature of any existing underlying disease.
- **C.** Inheritance is exclusively holandric (Y-linked) with 100% penetrance restricted to male neonates.
- **D.** Diagnostic confirmation relies solely on plain skull radiographs without biochemical, microbiologic, or clinical correlation.

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: B** — The prognosis of RTA depends to a large extent on the nature of any existing underlying disease.

**Rationale:** **Pediatric Clinical Principle (Nephrogenic Diabetes Insipidus):** The prognosis of RTA depends to a large extent on the nature of any existing underlying disease.

</details>
