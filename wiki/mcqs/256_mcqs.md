### Question 1 (Clinical Pediatrics Vignette)
A pediatric patient is evaluated for the following clinical and diagnostic profile: Spinal muscular atrophy (type 1); Congenital myasthenia gravis; Guillain-Barré syndrome and its variants; Poliomyelitis; Acute flaccid paralysis; Transverse myelitis. Which of the following conditions or syndromes is the most likely diagnosis?

- **A.** METABOLIC (Botulism (Clostridium botulinum))
- **B.** NEUROMUSCULAR (Botulism (Clostridium botulinum))
- **C.** INFECTIOUS (Botulism (Clostridium botulinum))
- **D.** ADEM (Botulism (Clostridium botulinum))

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: B** — NEUROMUSCULAR (Botulism (Clostridium botulinum))

**Rationale:** **NEUROMUSCULAR (Botulism (Clostridium botulinum))** is characterized by: Spinal muscular atrophy (type 1); Congenital myasthenia gravis; Guillain-Barré syndrome and its variants; Poliomyelitis; Acute flaccid paralysis; Transverse myelitis.

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **METABOLIC (Botulism (Clostridium botulinum))** is incorrect (characterized by Medium chain acetyl-coenzyme A deficiency; Carnitine deficiency; Congenital disorders of glycosylati). **INFECTIOUS (Botulism (Clostridium botulinum))** is incorrect (characterized by Enteroviral and parechovirus encephalitis; Sepsis). **ADEM (Botulism (Clostridium botulinum))** is incorrect (characterized by Congenital myopathy; Encephalitis (viral, autoimmune); Global developmental delay; Leukodystrophy).
