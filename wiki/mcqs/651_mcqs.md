### Question 1 (Clinical Pediatrics Vignette)
A pediatric patient is evaluated for the following clinical and diagnostic profile: Cimetidine; d-Penicillamine; Procainamide; l-Tryptophan; L-dopa. Which of the following conditions or syndromes is the most likely diagnosis?

- **A.** NONINFLAMMATORY NECROTIZING OR VACUOLAR CAUSES
- **B.** MYOSIN LOSS (Metabolic Myopathies and Channelopathies*)
- **C.** INFLAMMATORY CAUSES
- **D.** RHABDOMYOLYSIS AND MYOGLOBINURIA

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: C** — INFLAMMATORY CAUSES

**Rationale:** **INFLAMMATORY CAUSES** is characterized by: Cimetidine; d-Penicillamine; Procainamide; l-Tryptophan; L-dopa.

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **NONINFLAMMATORY NECROTIZING OR VACUOLAR CAUSES** is incorrect (characterized by Cholesterol-lowering agents (statins); Chloroquine; Colchicine; Emetine; ε-Aminocaproic acid; Labeta). **MYOSIN LOSS (Metabolic Myopathies and Channelopathies*)** is incorrect (characterized by Nondepolarizing neuromuscular blocking agents; Intravenous glucocorticoids; Modified from Goldman L,). **RHABDOMYOLYSIS AND MYOGLOBINURIA** is incorrect (characterized by Cholesterol-lowering drugs (especially statins); Alcohol; Heroin; Amphetamine; Toluene; Cocaine; ε-A).

</details>
