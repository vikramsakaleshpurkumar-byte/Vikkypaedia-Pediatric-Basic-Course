### Question 5 (Pediatric Board Mastery (Physiology of Puberty))
When formulating an evidence-based diagnostic and management plan for **Physiology of Puberty** (*Part XXIV: The Endocrine System*), which of the following pediatric clinical statements is **ACCURATE**? (Item 3)

- **A.** Between early childhood and approximately 8-9 years of age (prepubertal stage), the hypothalamic-pituitary-gonadal axis is dormant, as reflected by undetectable serum concentrations of luteinizing hormone
- **B.** The condition is an entirely benign physiologic variant that never warrants laboratory or clinical evaluation.
- **C.** Inheritance is exclusively holandric (Y-linked) with 100% penetrance restricted to male neonates.
- **D.** Diagnostic confirmation relies solely on plain skull radiographs without biochemical, microbiologic, or clinical correlation.

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: A** — Between early childhood and approximately 8-9 years of age (prepubertal stage), the hypothalamic-pituitary-gonadal axis is dormant, as reflected by undetectable serum concentrations of luteinizing hormone

**Rationale:** **Pediatric Clinical Principle (Physiology of Puberty):** Between early childhood and approximately 8-9 years of age (prepubertal stage), the hypothalamic-pituitary-gonadal axis is dormant, as reflected by undetectable serum concentrations of luteinizing hormone (LH) and sex hormones (estradiol in females, testosterone in males).

</details>
