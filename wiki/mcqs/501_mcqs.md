### Question 1 (Clinical Pediatrics Vignette)
A pediatric patient is evaluated for the following clinical and diagnostic profile: These rare conditions are characterized by variable degrees of anemia, ineffective erythropoiesis, hyperbilirubinemia, gallstones, splenomegaly, and secondary hemochromatosis (iron overload).. Which of the following conditions or syndromes is the most likely diagnosis?

- **A.** ANK1 (Hereditary Spherocytosis)
- **B.** Congenital Dyserythropoietic Anemias
- **C.** INCREASED TBG (Disorders of Thyroxine-Binding Globulin)
- **D.** Neonatal Resuscitation and Delivery Room Emergencies

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: B** — Congenital Dyserythropoietic Anemias

**Rationale:** **Congenital Dyserythropoietic Anemias** (associated with **`CDAN1, CDIN1, GATA1, KIF23, KLF1`**) is characterized by: These rare conditions are characterized by variable degrees of anemia, ineffective erythropoiesis, hyperbilirubinemia, gallstones, splenomegaly, and secondary hemochromatosis (iron overload)..

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **ANK1 (Hereditary Spherocytosis)** is incorrect (characterized by Frameshift; Nonsense; Splicing; Missense; Insertion/deletion; Promoter region; 50–67%; 5–10% in Japa). **INCREASED TBG (Disorders of Thyroxine-Binding Globulin)** is incorrect (characterized by Androgens; Anabolic steroids; Glucocorticoids; Hepatocellular disease; Severe illness; Nephrotic syn). **Neonatal Resuscitation and Delivery Room Emergencies** is incorrect (characterized by The umbilical cord management plan should be established before delivery.).
