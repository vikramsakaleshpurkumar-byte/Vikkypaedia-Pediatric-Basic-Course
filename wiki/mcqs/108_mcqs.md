### Question 1 (Clinical Pediatrics Vignette)
A pediatric patient is evaluated for the following clinical and diagnostic profile: Complex I subunits and assembly factors. Which of the following conditions or syndromes is the most likely diagnosis?

- **A.** MT-CO1, MT-CO2, MT-CO3
- **B.** MT-ND1, MT-ND2, MT-ND3, MT-ND4, MT-ND4L, MT-ND5, MT-ND6
- **C.** OXIDATIVE PHOSPHORYLATION DEFICIENCY
- **D.** DISORDERS OF MITOCHONDRIAL DNA MAINTENANCE

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: C** — OXIDATIVE PHOSPHORYLATION DEFICIENCY

**Rationale:** **OXIDATIVE PHOSPHORYLATION DEFICIENCY** is characterized by: Complex I subunits and assembly factors.

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **MT-CO1, MT-CO2, MT-CO3** is incorrect (characterized by Complex V subunits and assembly factors). **MT-ND1, MT-ND2, MT-ND3, MT-ND4, MT-ND4L, MT-ND5, MT-ND6** is incorrect (characterized by Complex II subunits and assembly factors). **DISORDERS OF MITOCHONDRIAL DNA MAINTENANCE** is incorrect (characterized by Nucleotide pool maintenance).

</details>

### Question 5 (Pediatric Board Mastery (Mitochondrial Disease Diagnosis))
When formulating an evidence-based diagnostic and management plan for **Mitochondrial Disease Diagnosis** (*Part IX: Metabolic Disorders*), which of the following pediatric clinical statements is **ACCURATE**? (Item 1)

- **A.** The condition is an entirely benign physiologic variant that never warrants laboratory or clinical evaluation.
- **B.** Primary mitochondrial disease results from defi cient RC function, which can be caused by pathogenic variants in genes that encode RC subunits, assembly factors or cofactors, components of mitochondrial DN
- **C.** Systemic tetracyclines and fluoroquinolones are universally required as routine prophylaxis in all healthy newborns.
- **D.** First-line management relies on immediate high-dose aspirin therapy in febrile infants.

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: B** — Primary mitochondrial disease results from defi cient RC function, which can be caused by pathogenic variants in genes that encode RC subunits, assembly factors or cofactors, components of mitochondrial DN

**Rationale:** **Pediatric Clinical Principle (Mitochondrial Disease Diagnosis):** Primary mitochondrial disease results from defi cient RC function, which can be caused by pathogenic variants in genes that encode RC subunits, assembly factors or cofactors, components of mitochondrial DNA (mtDNA) metabolism and maintenance, or a host of other basic metabolic processes ongoing within mitochondria.

</details>
