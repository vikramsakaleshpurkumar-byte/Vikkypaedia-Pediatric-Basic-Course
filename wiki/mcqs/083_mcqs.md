**Correct Answer: C** — The most common etiologies of brain injury leading to brain death are hypoxic-ischemic injury from cardiac arrest, shock and/or respiratory failure without cardiac arrest, traumatic brain injury, intracran

**Rationale:** **Pediatric Clinical Principle (Brain Death: Death by Neurologic Criteria):** The most common etiologies of brain injury leading to brain death are hypoxic-ischemic injury from cardiac arrest, shock and/or respiratory failure without cardiac arrest, traumatic brain injury, intracranial infections, toxic ingestions, and causes of malignant cerebral edema.
