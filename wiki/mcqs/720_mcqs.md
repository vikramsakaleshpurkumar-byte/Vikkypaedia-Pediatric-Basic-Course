### Question 1 (Clinical Pediatrics Vignette)
A pediatric patient is evaluated for the following clinical and diagnostic profile: Infantile; Juvenile; Adolescent. Which of the following conditions or syndromes is the most likely diagnosis?

- **A.** Idiopathic (The Spine)
- **B.** Congenital (The Spine)
- **C.** Syndromes (The Spine)
- **D.** Myopathies (The Spine)

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: A** — Idiopathic (The Spine)

**Rationale:** **Idiopathic (The Spine)** is characterized by: Infantile; Juvenile; Adolescent.

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **Congenital (The Spine)** is incorrect (characterized by Failure of formation; Wedge vertebrae; Hemivertebrae; Failure of segmentation; Unilateral bar; Block). **Syndromes (The Spine)** is incorrect (characterized by Neurofibromatosis; Marfan syndrome). **Myopathies (The Spine)** is incorrect (characterized by Duchenne muscular dystrophy; Arthrogryposis; Other muscular dystrophies).

### Question 2 (Clinical Pediatrics Vignette)
A pediatric patient is evaluated for the following clinical and diagnostic profile: Failure of formation; Wedge vertebrae; Hemivertebrae; Failure of segmentation; Unilateral bar; Block vertebra; Mixed. Which of the following conditions or syndromes is the most likely diagnosis?

- **A.** Compensatory (The Spine)
- **B.** Neuromuscular (The Spine)
- **C.** Congenital (The Spine)
- **D.** Myopathies (The Spine)

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: C** — Congenital (The Spine)

**Rationale:** **Congenital (The Spine)** is characterized by: Failure of formation; Wedge vertebrae; Hemivertebrae; Failure of segmentation; Unilateral bar; Block vertebra; Mixed.

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **Compensatory (The Spine)** is incorrect (characterized by Leg-length discrepancy). **Neuromuscular (The Spine)** is incorrect (characterized by Neuropathic diseases; Upper motor neuron; Cerebral palsy; Spinocerebellar degeneration (Friedreich a). **Myopathies (The Spine)** is incorrect (characterized by Duchenne muscular dystrophy; Arthrogryposis; Other muscular dystrophies).

</details>
