### Question 5 (Pediatric Board Mastery (Developmental and Behavioral Surveillance and Screening))

- **B.** Systemic tetracyclines and fluoroquinolones are universally required as routine prophylaxis in all healthy newborns.

- **D.** First-line management relies on immediate high-dose aspirin therapy in febrile infants.

**Correct Answer: A** — Among the many types of developmental or behavioral conditions, the most common include language problems, affecting at least 10% of children; behavior or emotional disorders, affecting up to 25% of childr

**Rationale:** **Pediatric Clinical Principle (Developmental and Behavioral Surveillance and Screening):** Among the many types of developmental or behavioral conditions, the most common include language problems, affecting at least 10% of children; behavior or emotional disorders, affecting up to 25% of children, with 6% considered serious; attention-deficit/hyper activity disorder, affecting 10% of children; and learning disabilities, affecting up to 10%.
