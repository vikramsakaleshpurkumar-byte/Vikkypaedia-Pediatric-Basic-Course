### Question 1 (Clinical Pediatrics Vignette)
A pediatric patient is evaluated for the following clinical and diagnostic profile: The most common football injuries include joint sprains, muscle strains, and contusions, with the lower extremities injured most frequently.. Which of the following conditions or syndromes is the most likely diagnosis?

- **A.** IDIOPATHIC HYPERTROPHIC PYLORIC STENOSIS
- **B.** NCF4 (Eosinophilic Gastroenteritis)
- **C.** Specific Sports and Associated Injuries
- **D.** ADNFLE (Conditions That Mimic Seizures)

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: C** — Specific Sports and Associated Injuries

**Rationale:** **Specific Sports and Associated Injuries** is characterized by: The most common football injuries include joint sprains, muscle strains, and contusions, with the lower extremities injured most frequently..

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **IDIOPATHIC HYPERTROPHIC PYLORIC STENOSIS** is incorrect (characterized by Infantile-onset pyloric stenosis; Late onset; Associated with medications: erythromycin, prostagland). **NCF4 (Eosinophilic Gastroenteritis)** is incorrect (characterized by Neutrophil oxidative burst study). **ADNFLE (Conditions That Mimic Seizures)** is incorrect (characterized by Inheritance; AD–16q; AD–2q35; AD–2q13).
