- **A.** Tense Ascites Paracentesis Protocol

### Question 3 (Differential Diagnosis (Ascites))
During a pediatric consultation regarding **Ascites**, which clinical entity or concept is described by the following profile? "to cause avid renal sodium and water retention, compounded by hypoalbuminemia (reduced plasma oncotic pressure)."

- **A.** congenital lymphatic malformations
- **B.** Viral (Splenomegaly)
- **C.** Chylous ascites
- **D.** antidiuretic hormone (ADH)

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: D** — antidiuretic hormone (ADH)

**Rationale:** antidiuretic hormone (ADH) to cause avid renal sodium and water retention, compounded by hypoalbuminemia (reduced plasma oncotic pressure).
