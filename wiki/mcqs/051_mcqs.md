**Correct Answer: B** — A neural signature for dyslexia. Image on left shows left hemisphere brain systems in typical (nonimpaired) readers. The three systems for reading are an anterior system in the region of the infe

**Rationale:** **Fig. 51.2**: A neural signature for dyslexia. Image on left shows left hemisphere brain systems in typical (nonimpaired) readers. The three systems for reading are an anterior system in the region of the inferior frontal gyrus (Broca’s area), serving articulation and word analysis, and two posterior systems: one in the occipitotemporal region serving word analysis and a second in the occipitotemporal region (the word-form area) serving the rapid, automatic, fluent identification of words.
