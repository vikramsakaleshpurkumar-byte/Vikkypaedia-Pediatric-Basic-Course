### Question 1 (Clinical Pediatrics Vignette)
A pediatric patient is evaluated for the following clinical and diagnostic profile: • Volvulus; • Gastroschisis; • Necrotizing enterocolitis; • Intestinal atresia; • Trauma. Which of the following conditions or syndromes is the most likely diagnosis?

- **A.** SHORT BOWEL (Intestinal Transplantation in Children with Intestinal Failure)
- **B.** MMIHS (Intestinal Transplantation in Children with Intestinal Failure)
- **C.** INTESTINAL DYSMOTILITY
- **D.** VASCULAR (Intestinal Transplantation in Children with Intestinal Failure)

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: A** — SHORT BOWEL (Intestinal Transplantation in Children with Intestinal Failure)

**Rationale:** **SHORT BOWEL (Intestinal Transplantation in Children with Intestinal Failure)** is characterized by: • Volvulus; • Gastroschisis; • Necrotizing enterocolitis; • Intestinal atresia; • Trauma.

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **MMIHS (Intestinal Transplantation in Children with Intestinal Failure)** is incorrect (characterized by • Intestinal aganglionosis (Hirschsprung disease)). **INTESTINAL DYSMOTILITY** is incorrect (characterized by • Intestinal pseudoobstruction). **VASCULAR (Intestinal Transplantation in Children with Intestinal Failure)** is incorrect (characterized by • Acute diffuse intestinal infarction with hepatic failure; MMIHS, megacystis microcolon intestinal ).
