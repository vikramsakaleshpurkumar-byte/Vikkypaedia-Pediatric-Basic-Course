### Question 1 (Clinical Pediatrics Vignette)
A pediatric patient is evaluated for the following clinical and diagnostic profile: EPIDEMIOLOGY Before the introduction of the varicella vaccine in 1995, varicella was an almost universal communicable infection of childhood in the United States.. Which of the following conditions or syndromes is the most likely diagnosis?

- **A.** Wilson disease
- **B.** POSTTRANSPLANTATION
- **C.** Varicella-Zoster Virus*
- **D.** EDAR (Ectodermal Dysplasias)

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: C** — Varicella-Zoster Virus*

**Rationale:** **Varicella-Zoster Virus*** is characterized by: EPIDEMIOLOGY Before the introduction of the varicella vaccine in 1995, varicella was an almost universal communicable infection of childhood in the United States..

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **POSTTRANSPLANTATION** is incorrect (characterized by Chronic rejection of lung or heart/lung transplantation; Graft versus host disease associated with b). **EDAR (Ectodermal Dysplasias)** is incorrect (characterized by EDA receptor (EDAR); Receptor of EDA).
