### Question 1 (Clinical Pediatrics Vignette)

Which of the following conditions or syndromes is the most likely diagnosis?

- **A.** Lymphoproliferative Disorders
- **B.** Rheumatologic Conditions
- **C.** REACTIVE AUTOANTIBODIES
- **D.** Immune Deficiencies

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: C** — REACTIVE AUTOANTIBODIES

**Rationale:** **REACTIVE AUTOANTIBODIES** is characterized by: Primary (idiopathic); Secondary; Lymphoproliferative disorders; Connective tissue disorders (especially systemic lupus; erythematosus); Nonlymphoid neoplasms (e.g., ovarian tumors); Chronic inflammatory diseases (e.g., ulcerative colitis); Immunodeficiency disorders.

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **Lymphoproliferative Disorders** is incorrect (characterized by Autoimmune lymphoproliferative syndrome (ALPS)*; Rosai-Dorfman disease; Castleman disease; Ras-assoc). **Rheumatologic Conditions** is incorrect (characterized by Systemic lupus erythematosus (SLE); Antiphospholipid antibody syndrome; Juvenile idiopathic arthriti). **Immune Deficiencies** is incorrect (characterized by Common variable immune deficiency (CVID)*; Selective IgA deficiency; Chromosome 22q11.2 deletion (Di).
