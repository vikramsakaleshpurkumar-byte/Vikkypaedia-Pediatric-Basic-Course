### Question 1 (Clinical Pediatrics Vignette)
A pediatric patient is evaluated for the following clinical and diagnostic profile: Chorea-; acanthocytosis; Autosomal recessive. Which of the following conditions or syndromes is the most likely diagnosis?

- **A.** JPH3 (Hemoglobinopathies)
- **B.** MTTP (Hemoglobinopathies)
- **C.** GENES (Hemoglobinopathies)
- **D.** PCSK9 (Hemoglobinopathies)

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: C** — GENES (Hemoglobinopathies)

**Rationale:** **GENES (Hemoglobinopathies)** is characterized by: Chorea-; acanthocytosis; Autosomal recessive.

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **JPH3 (Hemoglobinopathies)** is incorrect (characterized by Pantothenate kinase-; associated neurode; generation; Autosomal recessive). **MTTP (Hemoglobinopathies)** is incorrect (characterized by Hereditary; hypobetalipo; proteinemia; Autosomal recessive). **PCSK9 (Hemoglobinopathies)** is incorrect (characterized by Aceruloplasminemia; Autosomal recessive).
