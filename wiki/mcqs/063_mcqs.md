### Question 1 (Clinical Pediatrics Vignette)
A pediatric patient is evaluated for the following clinical and diagnostic profile: Potassium chloride: KCl; 224.0; 24 mmol; Tripotassium citrate; 81.0; 2 mmol; Magnesium chloride: MgCl2•6H2O; 76.0; 3 mmol; Zinc acetate: Zn acetate•2H2O; 8.2; 300 μmol; Copper (cupric) sulfate: CuSO4•5H2O; 1.4; 45 μmol; Water: make up to; 2500 mL; Ad. Which of the following conditions or syndromes is the most likely diagnosis?

- **A.** mol/20 mL (Refeeding Syndrome)
- **B.** Cardiac (Refeeding Syndrome)
- **C.** Gastrointestinal (Refeeding Syndrome)
- **D.** Neurologic (Refeeding Syndrome)

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: A** — mol/20 mL (Refeeding Syndrome)

**Rationale:** **mol/20 mL (Refeeding Syndrome)** is characterized by: Potassium chloride: KCl; 224.0; 24 mmol; Tripotassium citrate; 81.0; 2 mmol; Magnesium chloride: MgCl2•6H2O; 76.0; 3 mmol; Zinc acetate: Zn acetate•2H2O; 8.2; 300 μmol; Copper (cupric) sulfate: CuSO4•5H2O; 1.4; 45 μmol; Water: make up to; 2500 mL; Add 20 mL when preparing 1 L of feed or ReSoMal.; *Make fresh each month.

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **Cardiac (Refeeding Syndrome)** is incorrect (characterized by Hypotension; Decreased stroke volume). **Gastrointestinal (Refeeding Syndrome)** is incorrect (characterized by Nausea; Vomiting; Constipation). **Neurologic (Refeeding Syndrome)** is incorrect (characterized by Paresthesia; Weakness; Confusion; Disorientation; Lethargy; Areflexic paralysis; Seizures; Coma; Hem).
