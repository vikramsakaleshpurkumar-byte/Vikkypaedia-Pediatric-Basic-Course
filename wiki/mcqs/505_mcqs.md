### Question 1 (Clinical Pediatrics Vignette)
A pediatric patient is evaluated for the following clinical and diagnostic profile: 100s; Infancy to; adulthood; Mild to; severe; female; Iron overload in the; absence of; transfusions. Which of the following conditions or syndromes is the most likely diagnosis?

- **A.** GLRX5 (Other Microcytic Anemias)
- **B.** SLC25A38 (Other Microcytic Anemias)
- **C.** FECH (Other Microcytic Anemias)
- **D.** ALAS2 (Other Microcytic Anemias)

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: D** — ALAS2 (Other Microcytic Anemias)

**Rationale:** **ALAS2 (Other Microcytic Anemias)** (associated with **`ALAS2`**) is characterized by: 100s; Infancy to; adulthood; Mild to; severe; female; Iron overload in the; absence of; transfusions.

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **GLRX5 (Other Microcytic Anemias)** is incorrect (characterized by 2; Adulthood; Mild to; severe). **SLC25A38 (Other Microcytic Anemias)** is incorrect (characterized by 40; Infancy; Severe; ↓; Transfusional iron; overload). **FECH (Other Microcytic Anemias)** is incorrect (characterized by 100s; Childhood; Mild; ↓; Acute; photosensitivity; Fe-S; biogenesis; defects).
