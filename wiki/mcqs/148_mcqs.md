### Question 1 (Clinical Pediatrics Vignette)
A pediatric patient is evaluated for the following clinical and diagnostic profile: Cytomegalovirus (CMV); Herpes simplex virus (HSV); Mycobacterium tuberculosis; Rubella virus; Treponema pallidum; Varicella-zoster virus (VZV); Listeria monocytogenes. Which of the following conditions or syndromes is the most likely diagnosis?

- **A.** HSV (Epidemiology of Infections)
- **B.** CMV (Epidemiology of Infections)
- **C.** POSTNATAL (Epidemiology of Infections)
- **D.** TRANSPLACENTAL (Epidemiology of Infections)

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: D** — TRANSPLACENTAL (Epidemiology of Infections)

**Rationale:** **TRANSPLACENTAL (Epidemiology of Infections)** is characterized by: Cytomegalovirus (CMV); Herpes simplex virus (HSV); Mycobacterium tuberculosis; Rubella virus; Treponema pallidum; Varicella-zoster virus (VZV); Listeria monocytogenes.

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **HSV (Epidemiology of Infections)** is incorrect (characterized by Listeria monocytogenes; Mycoplasma). **CMV (Epidemiology of Infections)** is incorrect (characterized by Enteric bacteria; Group B Streptococci; Haemophilus influenzae). **POSTNATAL (Epidemiology of Infections)** is incorrect (characterized by Adenovirus; Candida spp.*; Coagulase-negative; staphylococci).
