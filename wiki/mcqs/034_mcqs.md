### Question 1 (Clinical Pediatrics Vignette)

Which of the following conditions or syndromes is the most likely diagnosis?

- **A.** ABA + DSP (Psychotherapy)
- **B.** DBT (Psychotherapy)
- **C.** PROBABLY EFFICACIOUS
- **D.** EMDR (Psychotherapy)

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: C** — PROBABLY EFFICACIOUS

**Rationale:** **PROBABLY EFFICACIOUS** is characterized by: Anorexia; Family therapy: behavioral; Family therapy: systemic; Individual psychodynamic psychotherapy; Anxiety, children under 8; Family-based CBT; Group parent CBT ± group child CBT; Anxiety; CBT ± parent component ± medication; Family psychoeducation; Parent/child CBT; Relaxation, assertiveness training; Attention-d.

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **ABA + DSP (Psychotherapy)** is incorrect (characterized by Individual, focused (communication) ABA ± DSP; Focused DSP parent training; Behavior, child; Group B). **DBT (Psychotherapy)** is incorrect (characterized by Depression, child; None; None; Depression, adolescent; Individual/group CBT; Individual IPT; Group I). **EMDR (Psychotherapy)** is incorrect (characterized by Substance use; Individual/group/family CBT ± MET; Family-based treatment, ecologic ±).
