### Question 1 (Clinical Pediatrics Vignette)
A pediatric patient is evaluated for the following clinical and diagnostic profile: PROGNOSIS Children with bronchiectasis often suffer from recurrent pulmonary illnesses, resulting in missed school days, stunted growth, osteope nia, and osteoporosis.. Which of the following conditions or syndromes is the most likely diagnosis?

- **A.** Pulmonary Abscess
- **B.** 4. Internal bronchial obstruction
- **C.** ETV6-RUNX1 (The Leukemias)
- **D.** BLOOD LOSS (Anemia in the Newborn Infant)

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: A** — Pulmonary Abscess

**Rationale:** **Pulmonary Abscess** is characterized by: PROGNOSIS Children with bronchiectasis often suffer from recurrent pulmonary illnesses, resulting in missed school days, stunted growth, osteope nia, and osteoporosis..

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **4. Internal bronchial obstruction** is incorrect (characterized by • Bronchial stenosis; • Bronchomalacia; • Meconium aspiration; • Hypertrophic mucosa membranes; • Mo). **ETV6-RUNX1 (The Leukemias)** is incorrect (characterized by Favorable; TCF3-PBX1 translocation, t(1;19); (q23;p13); 5% children, AYAs and adults). **BLOOD LOSS (Anemia in the Newborn Infant)** is incorrect (characterized by • Iatrogenic blood loss (phlebotomy); • Placental hemorrhage; • Placental previa; • Injury of umbili).
