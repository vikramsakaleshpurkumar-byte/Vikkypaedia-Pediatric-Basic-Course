### Question 1 (Clinical Pediatrics Vignette)
A pediatric patient is evaluated for the following clinical and diagnostic profile: Familial lymphedema; No known gene; Chromosomal aneuploidy; Turner syndrome. Which of the following conditions or syndromes is the most likely diagnosis?

- **A.** VEGFR3 AR, AD (Abnormalities of Lymphatic Vessels)
- **B.** 47,XX+18; 47,XY+18
- **C.** PTPN11, SOS1, RAF1, RIT1, KRAS, AD
- **D.** LSC1, AR (Abnormalities of Lymphatic Vessels)

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: A** — VEGFR3 AR, AD (Abnormalities of Lymphatic Vessels)

**Rationale:** **VEGFR3 AR, AD (Abnormalities of Lymphatic Vessels)** (associated with **`AR, VEGFR3`**) is characterized by: Familial lymphedema; No known gene; Chromosomal aneuploidy; Turner syndrome.

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **47,XX+18; 47,XY+18** is incorrect (characterized by Other genetic and; syndromic disorders; Emberger syndrome (primary lymphedema with myelodysplasia); ). **PTPN11, SOS1, RAF1, RIT1, KRAS, AD** is incorrect (characterized by Cholestasis-lymphedema syndrome (Aagenaes syndrome)). **LSC1, AR (Abnormalities of Lymphatic Vessels)** is incorrect (characterized by Ectodermal dysplasia, hypo/anhidrotic, lymphedema and; immunodeficiency; immunodeficiency 33; IKBKG,).

</details>

### Question 2 (Pediatric Genetics & Molecular Etiology)
A child is diagnosed with **VEGFR3 AR, AD (Abnormalities of Lymphatic Vessels)** after presenting with Familial lymphedema; No known gene; Chromosomal aneuploidy; Turner syndrome. Pathogenic variants in which of the following genes are most classically implicated?

- **A.** ATM
- **B.** AR
- **C.** FLNA
- **D.** WDR62

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: B** — AR

**Rationale:** Pathogenic variants in **`AR`** (Diseases of the Blood) are associated with **VEGFR3 AR, AD (Abnormalities of Lymphatic Vessels)** (Familial lymphedema; No known gene; Chromosomal aneuploidy; Turner syndrome).

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **ATM** is incorrect (associated with DIT (Demyelinating Disorders of the Central Nervous System)). **WDR62** is incorrect (associated with GENETIC/METABOLIC ETIOLOGY).
