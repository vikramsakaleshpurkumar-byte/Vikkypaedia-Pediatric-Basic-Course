### Question 1 (Clinical Pediatrics Vignette)
A pediatric patient is evaluated for the following clinical and diagnostic profile: In mitral insufficiency caused by a congenitally malformed valve, the mitral valve annulus may be dilated, the chordae tendineae short and may insert anomalously, and the valve leaflets are deformed.. Which of the following conditions or syndromes is the most likely diagnosis?

- **A.** PROTEINS (Acquired Inhibitors of Coagulation)
- **B.** Membrane Disorders
- **C.** Musculoskeletal: bone/muscle pain, degenerative disc disease
- **D.** Acyanotic Congenital Heart Disease: Regurgitant Lesions

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: D** — Acyanotic Congenital Heart Disease: Regurgitant Lesions

**Rationale:** **Acyanotic Congenital Heart Disease: Regurgitant Lesions** is characterized by: In mitral insufficiency caused by a congenitally malformed valve, the mitral valve annulus may be dilated, the chordae tendineae short and may insert anomalously, and the valve leaflets are deformed..

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **PROTEINS (Acquired Inhibitors of Coagulation)** is incorrect (characterized by Decreased fibrinogen and factors FII, FV, FVII,; FIX, FX, and FXI; Decreased protein C; Decreased α2). **Membrane Disorders** is incorrect (characterized by Hereditary spherocytosis; Spherocytes; EMA binding, osmotic fragility; Hereditary elliptocytosis; El). **Musculoskeletal: bone/muscle pain, degenerative disc disease** is incorrect (characterized by osteoporosis/osteopenia).
