### Question 1 (Clinical Pediatrics Vignette)
A pediatric patient is evaluated for the following clinical and diagnostic profile: Agammaglobulinemia; Absence of antibody production, lack of B cells. Which of the following conditions or syndromes is the most likely diagnosis?

- **A.** CD79B (B-Cell and Antibody Deficiencies)
- **B.** CD79A (B-Cell and Antibody Deficiencies)
- **C.** IGHM (B-Cell and Antibody Deficiencies)
- **D.** BLNK (B-Cell and Antibody Deficiencies)

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: D** — BLNK (B-Cell and Antibody Deficiencies)

**Rationale:** **BLNK (B-Cell and Antibody Deficiencies)** is characterized by: Agammaglobulinemia; Absence of antibody production, lack of B cells.

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **CD79B (B-Cell and Antibody Deficiencies)** is incorrect (characterized by Agammaglobulinemia; Loss of the Igβ required for signal transduction, absence of antibody production). **CD79A (B-Cell and Antibody Deficiencies)** is incorrect (characterized by Agammaglobulinemia; Loss of the Igα required for signal transduction, absence of antibody production). **IGHM (B-Cell and Antibody Deficiencies)** is incorrect (characterized by Agammaglobulinemia; Loss of the Ig heavy chain, absence of antibody production, lack of B cells).

</details>
