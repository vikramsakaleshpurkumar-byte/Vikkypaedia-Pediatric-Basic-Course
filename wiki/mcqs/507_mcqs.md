### Question 3 (Pediatric Genetics & Molecular Etiology)
A child is diagnosed with **ANK1 (Hereditary Spherocytosis)** after presenting with Frameshift; Nonsense; Splicing; Missense; Insertion/deletion; Promoter region; 50–67%; 5–10% in Japan; Mostly dominant,; rare recessive; Mild to moderate; Band 3. Pathogenic variants in which of the following genes are most classically implicated?

- **A.** CLCN1
- **B.** ANK1
- **C.** TH
- **D.** ATM

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: B** — ANK1

**Rationale:** Pathogenic variants in **`ANK1`** (Diseases of the Blood) are associated with **ANK1 (Hereditary Spherocytosis)** (Frameshift; Nonsense; Splicing; Missense; Insertion/deletion; Promoter region; 50–67%; 5–10% in Japan; Mostly dominant,; rare recessive; Mild to moderate; Band 3).

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **TH** is incorrect (associated with Ascariasis (Ascaris lumbricoides)). **ATM** is incorrect (associated with DIT (Demyelinating Disorders of the Central Nervous System)).

### Question 5 (Pediatric Board Mastery (Hereditary Spherocytosis))

- **A.** Inheritance is exclusively holandric (Y-linked) with 100% penetrance restricted to male neonates.
- **B.** Systemic tetracyclines and fluoroquinolones are universally required as routine prophylaxis in all healthy newborns.

- **D.** Described in patients of all ethnic groups, it is the most common inherited abnormality of the erythrocyte associated with inherited hemolytic anemia in persons of Northern European origin.
