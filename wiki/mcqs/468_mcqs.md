### Question 1 (Clinical Pediatrics Vignette)
A pediatric patient is evaluated for the following clinical and diagnostic profile: Bronchopulmonary dysplasia (BPD); Severe acquired lung disease, such as after pediatric acute; respiratory distress syndrome (PARDS); Pulmonary fibrosis syndromes. Which of the following conditions or syndromes is the most likely diagnosis?

- **A.** AIRWAY (Chronic Respiratory Insufficiency)
- **B.** CLINICAL MANIFESTATIONS
- **C.** CHEST (Chronic Respiratory Insufficiency)
- **D.** PULMONARY/ALVEOLAR

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: D** — PULMONARY/ALVEOLAR

**Rationale:** **PULMONARY/ALVEOLAR** is characterized by: Bronchopulmonary dysplasia (BPD); Severe acquired lung disease, such as after pediatric acute; respiratory distress syndrome (PARDS); Pulmonary fibrosis syndromes.

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **AIRWAY (Chronic Respiratory Insufficiency)** is incorrect (characterized by Severe tracheomalacia; Severe bronchomalacia; Obstructive sleep apnea (OSA); Storage diseases). **CLINICAL MANIFESTATIONS** is incorrect (characterized by Ophthalmologic; Decreased/absent pupillary light response; Anisocoria; Strabismus; Lack of convergen). **CHEST (Chronic Respiratory Insufficiency)** is incorrect (characterized by Kyphoscoliosis; Skeletal dysplasias; Obesity).

### Question 2 (Clinical Pediatrics Vignette)
A pediatric patient is evaluated for the following clinical and diagnostic profile: Severe tracheomalacia; Severe bronchomalacia; Obstructive sleep apnea (OSA); Storage diseases. Which of the following conditions or syndromes is the most likely diagnosis?

- **A.** CHEST (Chronic Respiratory Insufficiency)
- **B.** NEUROMUSCULAR (Chronic Respiratory Insufficiency)
- **C.** CENTRAL NERVOUS SYSTEM (CNS)
- **D.** AIRWAY (Chronic Respiratory Insufficiency)

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: D** — AIRWAY (Chronic Respiratory Insufficiency)

**Rationale:** **AIRWAY (Chronic Respiratory Insufficiency)** is characterized by: Severe tracheomalacia; Severe bronchomalacia; Obstructive sleep apnea (OSA); Storage diseases.

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **CHEST (Chronic Respiratory Insufficiency)** is incorrect (characterized by Kyphoscoliosis; Skeletal dysplasias; Obesity). **NEUROMUSCULAR (Chronic Respiratory Insufficiency)** is incorrect (characterized by Spinal muscular atrophy; Spinal cord injury; Diaphragmatic dysfunction; Mitochondrial diseases). **CENTRAL NERVOUS SYSTEM (CNS)** is incorrect (characterized by Congenital central hypoventilation syndrome (CCHS); Rapid-onset obesity with hypothalamic dysregulat).
