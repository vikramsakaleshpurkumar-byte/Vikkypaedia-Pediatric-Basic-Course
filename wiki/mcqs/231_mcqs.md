### Question 1 (Clinical Pediatrics Vignette)
A pediatric patient is evaluated for the following clinical and diagnostic profile: Hemolysis; β; β; β; α; Lancefield group. Which of the following conditions or syndromes is the most likely diagnosis?

- **A.** EXTREMITIES (Vitamin D Deficiency (Rickets) and Excess)
- **B.** Coarctation of the aorta
- **C.** VIRIDANS STREPTOCOCCI
- **D.** CMV (Congenital and Perinatal Infections)

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: C** — VIRIDANS STREPTOCOCCI

**Rationale:** **VIRIDANS STREPTOCOCCI** is characterized by: Hemolysis; β; β; β; α; Lancefield group.

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **EXTREMITIES (Vitamin D Deficiency (Rickets) and Excess)** is incorrect (characterized by Enlargement of wrists and ankles; Valgus or varus deformities; Windswept deformity (valgus deformity). **CMV (Congenital and Perinatal Infections)** is incorrect (characterized by Hygienic education to avoid direct contact with toddler; urine/saliva, avoid new sexual partners).
