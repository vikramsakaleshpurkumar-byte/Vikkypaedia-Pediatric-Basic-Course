### Question 1 (Clinical Pediatrics Vignette)
A pediatric patient is evaluated for the following clinical and diagnostic profile: Neurocardiogenic (vasovagal); Emotion (seeing blood); Pain (needle phobia); Reflex asystolic syncope (pallid breath-holding). Which of the following conditions or syndromes is the most likely diagnosis?

- **A.** SYSTEMIC ILLNESS (Syncope)
- **B.** REFLEX VASODEPRESSOR SYNCOPE
- **C.** SEIZURES (Syncope)
- **D.** CENTRAL NERVOUS SYSTEM

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: B** — REFLEX VASODEPRESSOR SYNCOPE

**Rationale:** **REFLEX VASODEPRESSOR SYNCOPE** is characterized by: Neurocardiogenic (vasovagal); Emotion (seeing blood); Pain (needle phobia); Reflex asystolic syncope (pallid breath-holding).

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **SYSTEMIC ILLNESS (Syncope)** is incorrect (characterized by Hypoglycemia; Anemia; Infection; Hypovolemia, dehydration; Adrenal insufficiency; Narcolepsy, catapl). **SEIZURES (Syncope)** is incorrect (characterized by Relation to posture; Common; No; Time of day; Diurnal; Diurnal or nocturnal; Precipitating factors; ). **CENTRAL NERVOUS SYSTEM** is incorrect (characterized by Seizure (atonic, absence, myoclonic-astatic); Hyperekplexia; Stroke, transient ischemic attack; Frie).

</details>
