- **D.** MYOPATHIES PRESENTING IN CHILDHOOD

**Correct Answer: A** — Parainfluenza Viruses

**Rationale:** **Parainfluenza Viruses** is characterized by: CLINICAL MANIFESTATIONS The most common type of illness caused by HPIV infection consists of some combination of low-grade fever, rhinorrhea, cough, pharyn gitis, and hoarseness and may be associated with vomiting or diar rhea..

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **ORAL OSELTAMIVIR (Influenza Viruses)** is incorrect (characterized by Adults; 75 mg twice daily; 75 mg once daily; Children ≥12 mo; ≤15 kg (≤33 lb); 30 mg twice daily; 30). **IL10RB (Inflammatory Bowel Disease)** is incorrect (characterized by IL-10 receptor B; Crohn disease; Antiinflammatory cytokine receptor, NFκB; inhibition, JAK-STAT regu). **MYOPATHIES PRESENTING IN CHILDHOOD** is incorrect (characterized by Congenital myopathies: nemaline myopathy, centronuclear; myopathy, central core; Endocrine-metabolic).

**Correct Answer: D** — HPIV-4 causes a similar range of illness as the other types, and with advancements in molecular diagnosis, there is evidence that HPIV-4 may be comparable to HPIV-3 and frequently associated with acute low

**Rationale:** **Pediatric Clinical Principle (Parainfluenza Viruses):** HPIV-4 causes a similar range of illness as the other types, and with advancements in molecular diagnosis, there is evidence that HPIV-4 may be comparable to HPIV-3 and frequently associated with acute lower respiratory infection (ALRI).
