### Question 1 (Clinical Pediatrics Vignette)
A pediatric patient is evaluated for the following clinical and diagnostic profile: Sex chromosome [condition]; 47,XXY (Klinefelter syndrome and variants); 45,X/46,XY (mixed gonadal dysgenesis); 46,XX/46,XY (chimerism). Which of the following conditions or syndromes is the most likely diagnosis?

- **A.** Disorders of Sex Development (DSD)
- **B.** 46,XY DSD (Disorders and Anomalies of the Scrotal Contents)
- **C.** Syndromes Associated with Both Primary and Secondary Hypogonadism
- **D.** Congenital Hypogonadotropic Hypogonadism in 46,XY

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: A** — Disorders of Sex Development (DSD)

**Rationale:** **Disorders of Sex Development (DSD)** is characterized by: Sex chromosome DSD; 47,XXY (Klinefelter syndrome and variants); 45,X/46,XY (mixed gonadal dysgenesis); 46,XX/46,XY (chimerism).

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **46,XY DSD (Disorders and Anomalies of the Scrotal Contents)** is incorrect (characterized by Disorders of testicular development; Complete or partial gonadal dysgenesis; Variations in ARX, ATRX). **Syndromes Associated with Both Primary and Secondary Hypogonadism** is incorrect (characterized by Prader-Willi syndrome; Bardet-Biedl syndrome). **Congenital Hypogonadotropic Hypogonadism in 46,XY** is incorrect (characterized by Isolated hypogonadotropic hypogonadism with anosmia (Kallmann syndrome); KAL1, FGFR1/FGF8, PROK2/PRO).
