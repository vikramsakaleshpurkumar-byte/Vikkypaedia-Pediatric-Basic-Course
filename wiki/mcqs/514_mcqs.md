### Question 1 (Clinical Pediatrics Vignette)
A pediatric patient is evaluated for the following clinical and diagnostic profile: Acquired: Plasma exchange (PEX); Glucocorticoids; +/- Rituximab; Congenital: Scheduled plasma; infusions, recombinant. Which of the following conditions or syndromes is the most likely diagnosis?

- **A.** ADULT ROME IV (Cyclic Vomiting Syndrome)
- **B.** ADAMTS13 >10% (Hemolytic Anemias Secondary to Other Extracellular Factors)
- **C.** ADAMTS13 (Hemolytic Anemias Secondary to Other Extracellular Factors)
- **D.** ADAMTS13 <10% (Hemolytic Anemias Secondary to Other Extracellular Factors)

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: D** — ADAMTS13 <10% (Hemolytic Anemias Secondary to Other Extracellular Factors)

**Rationale:** **ADAMTS13 <10% (Hemolytic Anemias Secondary to Other Extracellular Factors)** (associated with **`ADAMTS13`**) is characterized by: Acquired: Plasma exchange (PEX); Glucocorticoids; +/- Rituximab; Congenital: Scheduled plasma; infusions, recombinant.

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **ADULT ROME IV (Cyclic Vomiting Syndrome)** is incorrect (characterized by Stereotypical episodes of vomiting regarding onset (acute) and; duration (<1 wk); 1. ≥3 discrete epi). **ADAMTS13 >10% (Hemolytic Anemias Secondary to Other Extracellular Factors)** is incorrect (characterized by Decreased factors H and I; (inhibitors of complement)†; Eculizumab (Ab to C5); Plasmapheresis not in). **ADAMTS13 (Hemolytic Anemias Secondary to Other Extracellular Factors)** is incorrect (characterized by Hemolytic-uremic syndrome (HUS); Escherichia coli 0157, Shiga toxin; E. coli 0157, Shiga toxin; Supp).

**Correct Answer: C** — ADAMTS13

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **F8** is incorrect (associated with Tumors of the Skin). **NF2** is incorrect (associated with NF2 (Central Nervous System Tumors in Childhood)). **DSP** is incorrect (associated with ABA + DSP (Psychotherapy)).
