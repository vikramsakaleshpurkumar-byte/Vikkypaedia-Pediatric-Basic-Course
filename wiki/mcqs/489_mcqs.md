- **A.** NCF1
- **B.** GNAS
- **C.** SMAD4
- **D.** DNAI1

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: C** — SMAD4

**Rationale:** Pathogenic variants in **`SMAD4`** (The Cardiovascular System) are associated with **CONGENITAL (Diseases of the Pericardium)** (Absence (partial, complete); Cysts; Mulibrey nanism (TRIM 37 gene variant); Camptodactyly-arthropathy–coxa vara–pericarditis syndrome (PRG4; gene variant); Myhre syndrome (SMAD4 gene variant)).

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **NCF1** is incorrect (associated with NCF1 (Eosinophilic Gastroenteritis)). **GNAS** is incorrect (associated with PRIMARY HYPOTHYROIDISM).
