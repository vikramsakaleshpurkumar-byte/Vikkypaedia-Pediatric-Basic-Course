### Question 1 (Clinical Pediatrics Vignette)
A pediatric patient is evaluated for the following clinical and diagnostic profile: CHOANAL ATRESIA This is the most common congenital anomaly of the nose and has a frequency of approximately 1 in 7,000 live births.. Which of the following conditions or syndromes is the most likely diagnosis?

- **A.** Congenital Disorders of the Nose
- **B.** AUTOIMMUNE (Sarcoidosis)
- **C.** SYNDROMIC (Diseases of the Myocardium)
- **D.** SURGERY ON THE PLACENTA, CORD, OR MEMBRANES

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: A** — Congenital Disorders of the Nose

**Rationale:** **Congenital Disorders of the Nose** (associated with **`CHD7`**) is characterized by: CHOANAL ATRESIA This is the most common congenital anomaly of the nose and has a frequency of approximately 1 in 7,000 live births..

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **AUTOIMMUNE (Sarcoidosis)** is incorrect (characterized by GPA (Wegener granulomatosis); Churg-Strauss syndrome; Behçet disease). **SYNDROMIC (Diseases of the Myocardium)** is incorrect (characterized by Dilated (DCM); Dilation of LV and RV; with minimal or no; wall thickening; Reduced contractility; is). **SURGERY ON THE PLACENTA, CORD, OR MEMBRANES** is incorrect (characterized by 7. Chorioangioma; High-output cardiac failure due to AV shunting; Effects of polyhydramnios; Reversa).
