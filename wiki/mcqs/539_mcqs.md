### Question 1 (Clinical Pediatrics Vignette)

- **A.** SLE, JIA (Lymphadenopathy)
- **B.** RARE CAUSES (Lymphadenopathy)
- **C.** HIV (Lymphadenopathy)
- **D.** ANTERIOR AURICULAR

**Rationale:** **HIV (Lymphadenopathy)** is characterized by: Syphilis; Toxoplasmosis; Nonspecific viral; infections.

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **SLE, JIA (Lymphadenopathy)** is incorrect (characterized by Leukemia/lymphoma; Tuberculosis (miliary); Sarcoidosis). **RARE CAUSES (Lymphadenopathy)** is incorrect (characterized by Chagas disease; (congenital); Congenital; leukemia; Congenital; tuberculosis; Reticuloendothe; liosi). **ANTERIOR AURICULAR** is incorrect (characterized by Conjunctivitis or other eye infections; Oculoglandular tularemia, cat-scratch disease, EBV, adenovir).
