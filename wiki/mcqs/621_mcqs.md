### Question 1 (Clinical Pediatrics Vignette)
A pediatric patient is evaluated for the following clinical and diagnostic profile: An autosomal dominant pattern of inheritance should raise suspicion for the latter disorder.. Which of the following conditions or syndromes is the most likely diagnosis?

- **A.** Dermatologic: angioedema, dermatographism, flushing, pruritus
- **B.** Pheochromocytoma
- **C.** Hormones and Peptides of Calcium Homeostasis and Bone Metabolism
- **D.** >_32 WEEKS (Bronchopulmonary Dysplasia)

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: B** — Pheochromocytoma

**Rationale:** **Pheochromocytoma** (associated with **`NF1, RET, VHL`**) is characterized by: An autosomal dominant pattern of inheritance should raise suspicion for the latter disorder..

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **Dermatologic: angioedema, dermatographism, flushing, pruritus** is incorrect (characterized by urticaria pigmentosa). **Hormones and Peptides of Calcium Homeostasis and Bone Metabolism** is incorrect (characterized by Inactivating variants of the receptor for PTH/PTHrP results in a lethal bone disorder characterized ). **>_32 WEEKS (Bronchopulmonary Dysplasia)** is incorrect (characterized by Time point of assessment; 36 weeks’ PMA or discharge to home, whichever comes first; >28 days but <5).

### Question 2 (Pediatric Genetics & Molecular Etiology)
A child is diagnosed with **Pheochromocytoma** after presenting with An autosomal dominant pattern of inheritance should raise suspicion for the latter disorder.. Pathogenic variants in which of the following genes are most classically implicated?

- **A.** NF1
- **B.** NKX2-1
- **C.** RET
- **D.** APOL1

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: A** — NF1

**Rationale:** Pathogenic variants in **`NF1`** (AD) are associated with **Pheochromocytoma** (An autosomal dominant pattern of inheritance should raise suspicion for the latter disorder.).

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **NKX2-1** is incorrect (associated with Respiratory Distress Syndrome (Hyaline Membrane Disease)). **RET** is incorrect (associated with POLG1 (DNA- (Motility Disorders and Hirschsprung Disease)). **APOL1** is incorrect (associated with African Trypanosomiasis (Sleeping Sickness; Trypanosoma brucei Complex)).
