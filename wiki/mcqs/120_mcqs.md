### Question 1 (Clinical Pediatrics Vignette)
A pediatric patient is evaluated for the following clinical and diagnostic profile: LEVELS OF NEONATAL CARE A Level I facility must be able to provide basic neonatal care.. Which of the following conditions or syndromes is the most likely diagnosis?

- **A.** 7-12 MO (Language Development and Communication Disorders)
- **B.** Transport of the Critically Ill Newborn
- **C.** MAJOR CAUSES (Encephalopathies)
- **D.** CAD (Disorders of Purine and Pyrimidine Metabolism)

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: B** — Transport of the Critically Ill Newborn

**Rationale:** **Transport of the Critically Ill Newborn** is characterized by: LEVELS OF NEONATAL CARE A Level I facility must be able to provide basic neonatal care..

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **7-12 MO (Language Development and Communication Disorders)** is incorrect (characterized by • Listens when spoken to; • Turns and looks in direction of sounds; • Enjoys social games, such as p). **MAJOR CAUSES (Encephalopathies)** is incorrect (characterized by Spastic diplegia (35%); Periventricular leukomalacia; Periventricular cysts or scars in white matter). **CAD (Disorders of Purine and Pyrimidine Metabolism)** is incorrect (characterized by Uridine; Suppresses seizures, reverses neurobehavioral and; hematologic abnormalities; Familial juve).
