<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: B** — Though clinical features, including phenotype, can raise a strong suspi cion of a congenital myopathy, the definitive diagnosis is determined by the histopathologic findings in the muscle biopsy specimen o

**Rationale:** **Pediatric Clinical Principle (Developmental Disorders of Muscle):** Though clinical features, including phenotype, can raise a strong suspi cion of a congenital myopathy, the definitive diagnosis is determined by the histopathologic findings in the muscle biopsy specimen or by genetic testing in lymphocytes, most commonly by testing for panels of genes with next-generation sequencing (NGS).
