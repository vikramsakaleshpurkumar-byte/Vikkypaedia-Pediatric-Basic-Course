### Question 1 (Clinical Pediatrics Vignette)
A pediatric patient is evaluated for the following clinical and diagnostic profile: Intestinal malabsorption of fats may accompany cystic fibrosis, biliary atresia, or other liver diseases and results in a deficiency of fatsoluble dietary vitamins including vitamin K.. Which of the following conditions or syndromes is the most likely diagnosis?

- **A.** LRBA (Immune Dysregulation)
- **B.** ILLNESS (Transmissible Spongiform Encephalopathies)
- **C.** INFECTIOUS (Botulism (Clostridium botulinum))
- **D.** Coagulopathy in Liver Disease

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: D** — Coagulopathy in Liver Disease

**Rationale:** **Coagulopathy in Liver Disease** is characterized by: Intestinal malabsorption of fats may accompany cystic fibrosis, biliary atresia, or other liver diseases and results in a deficiency of fatsoluble dietary vitamins including vitamin K..

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **LRBA (Immune Dysregulation)** is incorrect (characterized by LRBA deficiency). **ILLNESS (Transmissible Spongiform Encephalopathies)** is incorrect (characterized by sCJD; Dementia, myoclonus,; ataxia; Unknown; Worldwide; ∼1-2/1 million/; yr; 85–95% of all CJD; case). **INFECTIOUS (Botulism (Clostridium botulinum))** is incorrect (characterized by Enteroviral and parechovirus encephalitis; Sepsis).
