### Question 1 (Clinical Pediatrics Vignette)
A pediatric patient is evaluated for the following clinical and diagnostic profile: Petechial hemorrhages, lungs, and pleura; Pulmonary edema. Which of the following conditions or syndromes is the most likely diagnosis?

- **A.** (VEGF) (Sudden Infant Death Syndrome)
- **B.** INFANT RISK FACTORS
- **C.** COMMON OBSERVATIONS
- **D.** MATERNAL AND ANTENATAL RISK FACTORS

**Correct Answer: C** — COMMON OBSERVATIONS

**Rationale:** **COMMON OBSERVATIONS** is characterized by: Petechial hemorrhages, lungs, and pleura; Pulmonary edema.

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **(VEGF) (Sudden Infant Death Syndrome)** is incorrect (characterized by • Secondary to preexisting asphyxia; • VEGF polymorphisms). **INFANT RISK FACTORS** is incorrect (characterized by Age (peak 1-4 mo); Male gender; Ethnicity (Black, American Indian, Alaska Native); Growth failure; N). **MATERNAL AND ANTENATAL RISK FACTORS** is incorrect (characterized by Elevated second-trimester serum α-fetoprotein; Smoking; Alcohol use; Drug use (cocaine, heroin); Nut).
