### Question 1 (Clinical Pediatrics Vignette)
A pediatric patient is evaluated for the following clinical and diagnostic profile: Disseminated NTM, BCG,; Salmonella; Mucocutaneous candidiasis; Variable penetration; Antimycobacterials; consider; IFN-γ with careful monitoring; IL-12p40 deficiency. Which of the following conditions or syndromes is the most likely diagnosis?

- **A.** SPPL2A/AR (Defects of Innate Immunity)
- **B.** IL12RB2/AR (Defects of Innate Immunity)
- **C.** STAT1/AD (Defects of Innate Immunity)
- **D.** IL12RB1/AR (Defects of Innate Immunity)

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: D** — IL12RB1/AR (Defects of Innate Immunity)

**Rationale:** **IL12RB1/AR (Defects of Innate Immunity)** (associated with **`AR`**) is characterized by: Disseminated NTM, BCG,; Salmonella; Mucocutaneous candidiasis; Variable penetration; Antimycobacterials; consider; IFN-γ with careful monitoring; IL-12p40 deficiency.

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **SPPL2A/AR (Defects of Innate Immunity)** is incorrect (characterized by Disseminated BCG; Antimycobacterials; TYK2 deficiency). **IL12RB2/AR (Defects of Innate Immunity)** is incorrect (characterized by NTM, BCG, and tuberculosis; Antimycobacterials; IL-23R deficiency). **STAT1/AD (Defects of Innate Immunity)** is incorrect (characterized by Disseminated BCG/NTM, often; osteomyelitis; Antimycobacterials; consider; IFN-γ; SPPL2a deficiency).

### Question 3 (Pediatric Genetics & Molecular Etiology)

- **C.** FMR1
- **D.** AR

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: D** — AR
