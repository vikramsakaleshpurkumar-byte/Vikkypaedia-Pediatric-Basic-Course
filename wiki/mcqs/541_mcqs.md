### Question 1 (Clinical Pediatrics Vignette)
A pediatric patient is evaluated for the following clinical and diagnostic profile: Chromosomal; translocation; t(9;22). Which of the following conditions or syndromes is the most likely diagnosis?

- **A.** APML (Molecular and Cellular Biology of Cancer)
- **B.** TCF3 (E2A)::PBX1 (Molecular and Cellular Biology of Cancer)
- **C.** PML::RARA (Molecular and Cellular Biology of Cancer)
- **D.** TUMOR (Molecular and Cellular Biology of Cancer)

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: D** — TUMOR (Molecular and Cellular Biology of Cancer)

**Rationale:** **TUMOR (Molecular and Cellular Biology of Cancer)** is characterized by: Chromosomal; translocation; t(9;22).

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **APML (Molecular and Cellular Biology of Cancer)** is incorrect (characterized by 11q23 and others (over; 50 fusions partners)). **TCF3 (E2A)::PBX1 (Molecular and Cellular Biology of Cancer)** is incorrect (characterized by Chimeric transcription factor). **PML::RARA (Molecular and Cellular Biology of Cancer)** is incorrect (characterized by Chimeric transcription factor).
