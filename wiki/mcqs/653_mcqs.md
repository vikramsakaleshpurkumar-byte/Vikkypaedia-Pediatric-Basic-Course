### Question 1 (Clinical Pediatrics Vignette)
A pediatric patient is evaluated for the following clinical and diagnostic profile: Demyelinating; Forearm NCV usually <38 m/sec. Which of the following conditions or syndromes is the most likely diagnosis?

- **A.** HEREDITARY MOTOR AND SENSORY NEUROPATHIES (HMSNs)
- **B.** MPZ (P0 ) (Hereditary Motor-Sensory Neuropathies)
- **C.** NEFL (Hereditary Motor-Sensory Neuropathies)
- **D.** FBLN5 (Hereditary Motor-Sensory Neuropathies)

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: A** — HEREDITARY MOTOR AND SENSORY NEUROPATHIES (HMSNs)

**Rationale:** **HEREDITARY MOTOR AND SENSORY NEUROPATHIES (HMSNs)** is characterized by: Demyelinating; Forearm NCV usually <38 m/sec.

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **MPZ (P0 ) (Hereditary Motor-Sensory Neuropathies)** is incorrect (characterized by Pathogenic variants also cause CMT2 and CMT3). **NEFL (Hereditary Motor-Sensory Neuropathies)** is incorrect (characterized by DSS phenotype common; CMT1 plus). **FBLN5 (Hereditary Motor-Sensory Neuropathies)** is incorrect (characterized by Macular degeneration; cutis laxa).

### Question 2 (Clinical Pediatrics Vignette)
