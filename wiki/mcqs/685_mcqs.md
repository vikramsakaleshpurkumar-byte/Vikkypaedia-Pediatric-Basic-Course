### Question 1 (Clinical Pediatrics Vignette)
A pediatric patient is evaluated for the following clinical and diagnostic profile: Langer hans cells are dendritic cells of the mononuclear phagocyte system and are uniquely characterized by a specific organelle, the Birbeck granule, which resembles a tennis racket on electron microscopy.. Which of the following conditions or syndromes is the most likely diagnosis?

- **A.** Morphology of the Skin
- **B.** CHARACTERISTICS (Common Fractures)
- **C.** FRUITS (Adverse and Allergic Reactions to Drugs)
- **D.** INCREASED TBG (Disorders of Thyroxine-Binding Globulin)

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: A** — Morphology of the Skin

**Rationale:** **Morphology of the Skin** is characterized by: Langer hans cells are dendritic cells of the mononuclear phagocyte system and are uniquely characterized by a specific organelle, the Birbeck granule, which resembles a tennis racket on electron microscopy..

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **CHARACTERISTICS (Common Fractures)** is incorrect (characterized by the zones of hypertrophic and degenerating; cartilage cell columns; extending through the metaphyses). **FRUITS (Adverse and Allergic Reactions to Drugs)** is incorrect (characterized by Blueberries, strawberries, plum,; watermelon, peach; Apple, pear, orange; Avocado, banana). **INCREASED TBG (Disorders of Thyroxine-Binding Globulin)** is incorrect (characterized by Androgens; Anabolic steroids; Glucocorticoids; Hepatocellular disease; Severe illness; Nephrotic syn).
