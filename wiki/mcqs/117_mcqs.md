### Question 1 (Clinical Pediatrics Vignette)

- **A.** MATERNAL SERUM α-FETOPROTEIN CONCENTRATION
- **B.** MRI (The Fetus)
- **C.** ANTEPARTUM BIOPHYSICAL MONITORING
- **D.** MATERNAL CERVIX (The Fetus)

**Correct Answer: B** — MRI (The Fetus)

**Rationale:** **MRI (The Fetus)** is characterized by: Defining of lesions before fetal surgery; Better delineation of fetal CNS anatomy.

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **MATERNAL SERUM α-FETOPROTEIN CONCENTRATION** is incorrect (characterized by Elevated; Twins, neural tube defects (anencephaly, spina bifida), intestinal atresia, hepatitis, nep). **ANTEPARTUM BIOPHYSICAL MONITORING** is incorrect (characterized by Nonstress test; Fetal distress; hypoxia; Biophysical profile and modified biophysical; profile; Feta). **MATERNAL CERVIX (The Fetus)** is incorrect (characterized by Fetal fibronectin; Indicates possible risk of preterm birth; Transvaginal cervical length; Short len).
