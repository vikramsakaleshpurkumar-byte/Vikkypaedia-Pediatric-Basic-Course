### Question 1 (Clinical Pediatrics Vignette)
A pediatric patient is evaluated for the following clinical and diagnostic profile: ETIOLOGY this pediatric condition is an arthropod (lice or tick)-transmitted infection caused by spirochetes of the genus [condition].. Which of the following conditions or syndromes is the most likely diagnosis?

- **A.** CONVENTIONAL MELANOMA
- **B.** Relapsing Fever (Borrelia)
- **C.** CUTANEOUS SIGNS (Poisoning)
- **D.** HEAT EXHAUSTION: REMOVE FROM PLAY

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: B** — Relapsing Fever (Borrelia)

**Rationale:** **Relapsing Fever (Borrelia)** is characterized by: ETIOLOGY Relapsing fever is an arthropod (lice or tick)-transmitted infection caused by spirochetes of the genus Borrelia..

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **CONVENTIONAL MELANOMA** is incorrect (characterized by Clinical; Papule or nodule; frequently; amelanotic, but can be any color; (e.g., pink-red to blue-bl). **CUTANEOUS SIGNS (Poisoning)** is incorrect (characterized by Diaphoresis; Cholinergics (organophosphates), sympathomimetics, salicylates, phencyclidine (PCP), wi). **HEAT EXHAUSTION: REMOVE FROM PLAY** is incorrect (characterized by Dizziness; Rapid pulse; Headaches; Nausea; Vomiting; Loss of coordination; Profuse sweating; Core te).
