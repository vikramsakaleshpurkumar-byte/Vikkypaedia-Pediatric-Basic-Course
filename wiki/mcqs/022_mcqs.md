### Question 1 (Clinical Pediatrics Vignette)
A pediatric patient is evaluated for the following clinical and diagnostic profile: Fetal adaptations or responses to an adverse situation in utero, termed fetal programming or developmental plasticity, have life long implications.. Which of the following conditions or syndromes is the most likely diagnosis?

- **A.** The Newborn
- **B.** 7-12 MO (Language Development and Communication Disorders)
- **C.** OCD (Psychopharmacology)
- **D.** MAIN CLINICAL FEATURES

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: A** — The Newborn

**Rationale:** **The Newborn** is characterized by: Fetal adaptations or responses to an adverse situation in utero, termed fetal programming or developmental plasticity, have life long implications..

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **7-12 MO (Language Development and Communication Disorders)** is incorrect (characterized by • Listens when spoken to; • Turns and looks in direction of sounds; • Enjoys social games, such as p). **OCD (Psychopharmacology)** is incorrect (characterized by (7-17); Depression; Anxiety; Obsessions; Compulsions; Age 6-12: 10; Age 13-17: 20; Depression: 10-20). **MAIN CLINICAL FEATURES** is incorrect (characterized by Hutchinson-Gilford; progeria syndrome).
