**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **XOR (Disorders of Purine and Pyrimidine Metabolism)** is incorrect (characterized by XOR inhibitor; Prevents renal stones and renal failure; Type 2). **OUTCOME (Disorders of Purine and Pyrimidine Metabolism)** is incorrect (characterized by APRT deficiency). **CAD (Disorders of Purine and Pyrimidine Metabolism)** is incorrect (characterized by Uridine; Suppresses seizures, reverses neurobehavioral and; hematologic abnormalities; Familial juve).

### Question 5 (Pediatric Board Mastery (Disorders of Purine and Pyrimidine Metabolism))
