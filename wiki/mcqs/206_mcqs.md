### Question 1 (Clinical Pediatrics Vignette)
A pediatric patient is evaluated for the following clinical and diagnostic profile: The hereditary amyloidoses include diseases caused by pathogenic variants in the genes for transthyretin and apolipoprotein A and usually do not present in childhood.. Which of the following conditions or syndromes is the most likely diagnosis?

- **A.** Pancreatoblastoma (Pancreatic Tumors)
- **B.** Primary (Without Associated Systemic Disease)
- **C.** Amyloidosis
- **D.** (DEBONEL) (Spotted Fever Group Rickettsioses)

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: C** — Amyloidosis

**Rationale:** **Amyloidosis** (associated with **`MEFV, MVK, NLRP3, TNFRSF1A`**) is characterized by: The hereditary amyloidoses include diseases caused by pathogenic variants in the genes for transthyretin and apolipoprotein A and usually do not present in childhood..

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **Pancreatoblastoma (Pancreatic Tumors)** is incorrect (characterized by Children 1–8 yr; Beckwith-Wiedemann & APC associations; elevated serum AFP; histologic squamoid corp). **Primary (Without Associated Systemic Disease)** is incorrect (characterized by Genetic; Sporadic). **(DEBONEL) (Spotted Fever Group Rickettsioses)** is incorrect (characterized by Rickettsia; slovaca,; Rickettsia; raoultii,; Rickettsia; sibirica; mongolotimonae; Tick bite:; Derma).

### Question 5 (Pediatric Board Mastery (Amyloidosis))
