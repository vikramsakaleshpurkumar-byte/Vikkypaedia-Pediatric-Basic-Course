### Question 1 (Clinical Pediatrics Vignette)
A pediatric patient is evaluated for the following clinical and diagnostic profile: Fever persisting at least 5 days†; Presence of at least four principal features:; Changes in extremities; • Acute: erythema of palms, soles; edema of hands, feet; • Subacute: periungual peeling of fingers, toes in wk 2 and 3; Polymorphous exanthem; B. Which of the following conditions or syndromes is the most likely diagnosis?

- **A.** Musculoskeletal System
- **B.** Respiratory System
- **C.** Central Nervous System
- **D.** EPIDEMIOLOGIC CASE DEFINITION (CLASSIC CLINICAL CRITERIA)

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: D** — EPIDEMIOLOGIC CASE DEFINITION (CLASSIC CLINICAL CRITERIA)

**Rationale:** **EPIDEMIOLOGIC CASE DEFINITION (CLASSIC CLINICAL CRITERIA)** is characterized by: Fever persisting at least 5 days†; Presence of at least four principal features:; Changes in extremities; • Acute: erythema of palms, soles; edema of hands, feet; • Subacute: periungual peeling of fingers, toes in wk 2 and 3; Polymorphous exanthem; Bilateral bulbar conjunctival injection without exudate; Erythema and c.

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **Musculoskeletal System** is incorrect (characterized by Arthritis, arthralgias (pleocytosis of synovial fluid)). **Respiratory System** is incorrect (characterized by Peribronchial and interstitial infiltrates on chest; radiograph; Pulmonary nodules). **Central Nervous System** is incorrect (characterized by Extreme irritability; Aseptic meningitis (pleocytosis of cerebrospinal; fluid); Facial nerve palsy; ).

### Question 2 (Clinical Pediatrics Vignette)

Which of the following conditions or syndromes is the most likely diagnosis?

- **A.** Respiratory System
- **B.** Cardiovascular System
- **C.** Gastrointestinal Tract
- **D.** Central Nervous System

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: B** — Cardiovascular System

**Rationale:** **Cardiovascular System** is characterized by: Myocarditis, pericarditis, valvular regurgitation, cardiogenic; shock; Coronary artery abnormalities; Aneurysms of medium-sized noncoronary arteries; Peripheral gangrene; Aortic root enlargement.

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **Respiratory System** is incorrect (characterized by Peribronchial and interstitial infiltrates on chest; radiograph; Pulmonary nodules). **Gastrointestinal Tract** is incorrect (characterized by Diarrhea, vomiting, abdominal pain; Hepatitis, jaundice; Hydrops of gallbladder; Pancreatitis; Parot). **Central Nervous System** is incorrect (characterized by Extreme irritability; Aseptic meningitis (pleocytosis of cerebrospinal; fluid); Facial nerve palsy; ).
