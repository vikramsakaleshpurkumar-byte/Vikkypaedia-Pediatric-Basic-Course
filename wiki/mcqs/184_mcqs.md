### Question 1 (Clinical Pediatrics Vignette)
A pediatric patient is evaluated for the following clinical and diagnostic profile: Seasonal; Perennial; Perennial with seasonal exacerbations. Which of the following conditions or syndromes is the most likely diagnosis?

- **A.** Physiologic (Allergic Rhinitis)
- **B.** Structural/Mechanical Factors
- **C.** Infectious (Allergic Rhinitis)
- **D.** ALLERGIC RHINITIS

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: D** — ALLERGIC RHINITIS

**Rationale:** **ALLERGIC RHINITIS** is characterized by: Seasonal; Perennial; Perennial with seasonal exacerbations.

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **Physiologic (Allergic Rhinitis)** is incorrect (characterized by Primary ciliary dyskinesia; Atrophic rhinitis; Hormonally induced; Hypothyroidism; Pregnancy; Oral c). **Structural/Mechanical Factors** is incorrect (characterized by Deviated septum/septal wall anomalies; Hypertrophic turbinates; Adenoidal hypertrophy; Foreign bodie). **Infectious (Allergic Rhinitis)** is incorrect (characterized by Acute infections; Chronic infections; Congenital syphilis).

### Question 5 (Pediatric Board Mastery (Allergic Rhinitis))
When formulating an evidence-based diagnostic and management plan for **Allergic Rhinitis** (*Part XIII: Allergic Disorders*), which of the following pediatric clinical statements is **ACCURATE**? (Item 1)

- **A.** Clinical manifestations are permanently restricted to late adulthood and never present during infancy, childhood, or adolescence.
- **B.** Bridging of the IgE molecules on the surface of mast cells by allergen initiates the early-phase allergic response, characterized by degranulation of mast cells and release of preformed and newly generated
- **C.** The condition is an entirely benign physiologic variant that never warrants laboratory or clinical evaluation.
- **D.** First-line management relies on immediate high-dose aspirin therapy in febrile infants.

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: B** — Bridging of the IgE molecules on the surface of mast cells by allergen initiates the early-phase allergic response, characterized by degranulation of mast cells and release of preformed and newly generated

**Rationale:** **Pediatric Clinical Principle (Allergic Rhinitis):** Bridging of the IgE molecules on the surface of mast cells by allergen initiates the early-phase allergic response, characterized by degranulation of mast cells and release of preformed and newly generated inflammatory mediators, including histamine, prostaglandin 2, and the cysteinyl leukotrienes.

</details>
