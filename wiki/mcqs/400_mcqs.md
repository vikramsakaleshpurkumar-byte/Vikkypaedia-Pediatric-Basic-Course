### Question 1 (Clinical Pediatrics Vignette)
A pediatric patient is evaluated for the following clinical and diagnostic profile: 80% resolve spontaneously with supportive care. Which of the following conditions or syndromes is the most likely diagnosis?

- **A.** Acute Necrotic Collection (ANC)
- **B.** Acute Peripancreatic Fluid Collection (APFC)
- **C.** Walled-Off Necrosis (WON)
- **D.** Pancreatic Pseudocyst

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: B** — Acute Peripancreatic Fluid Collection (APFC)

**Rationale:** **Acute Peripancreatic Fluid Collection (APFC)** is characterized by: 80% resolve spontaneously with supportive care.

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **Acute Necrotic Collection (ANC)** is incorrect (characterized by <4 weeks after necrotizing pancreatitis; heterogeneous fluid and necrotic parenchyma; avoid early in). **Walled-Off Necrosis (WON)** is incorrect (characterized by ≥4 weeks encapsulated pancreatic/peripancreatic necrosis; managed via step-up endoscopic translumina). **Pancreatic Pseudocyst** is incorrect (characterized by ≥4 weeks; well-circumscribed encapsulated fluid collection rich in amylase/lipase without solid necr).

</details>
