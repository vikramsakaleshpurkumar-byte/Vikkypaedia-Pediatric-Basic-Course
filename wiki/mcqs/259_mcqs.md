### Question 1 (Clinical Pediatrics Vignette)
A pediatric patient is evaluated for the following clinical and diagnostic profile: A clue to the diagnosis of gas gangrene is pain out of pro portion to the clinical appearance of the wound.. Which of the following conditions or syndromes is the most likely diagnosis?

- **A.** Other Anaerobic Infections
- **B.** ABDOMINAL DISTENTION OR MASS
- **C.** Secondary Glomerular Disease
- **D.** RIFAMPIN (Neisseria meningitidis (Meningococcus))

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: A** — Other Anaerobic Infections

**Rationale:** **Other Anaerobic Infections** is characterized by: A clue to the diagnosis of gas gangrene is pain out of pro portion to the clinical appearance of the wound..

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **ABDOMINAL DISTENTION OR MASS** is incorrect (characterized by Ascites: nephrotic syndrome, neoplasm, heart failure; Discrete mass: Wilms tumor, hydronephrosis, ne). **Secondary Glomerular Disease** is incorrect (characterized by Systemic lupus erythematosus (SLE nephritis); Hemolytic uremic syndrome; Polyarteritis and other vas). **RIFAMPIN (Neisseria meningitidis (Meningococcus))** is incorrect (characterized by Infants <1 mo¶; 5 mg/kg PO every 12 hr; 2 days (4 doses); Children ≥1 mo and adults; 15-20 mg/kg PO ).
