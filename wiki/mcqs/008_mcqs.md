### Question 5 (Pediatric Board Mastery (Pediatric Palliative Care))
When formulating an evidence-based diagnostic and management plan for **Pediatric Palliative Care** (*Part I: The Field of Pediatrics*), which of the following pediatric clinical statements is **ACCURATE**? (Item 1)

- **A.** The philosophy of palliative care can be successfully integrated into any hospital setting, including the ICU, when the focus of care also includes the prevention or amelioration of suffering and improving
- **B.** Clinical manifestations are permanently restricted to late adulthood and never present during infancy, childhood, or adolescence.
- **C.** First-line management relies on immediate high-dose aspirin therapy in febrile infants.
- **D.** The condition is an entirely benign physiologic variant that never warrants laboratory or clinical evaluation.

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: A** — The philosophy of palliative care can be successfully integrated into any hospital setting, including the ICU, when the focus of care also includes the prevention or amelioration of suffering and improving

**Rationale:** **Pediatric Clinical Principle (Pediatric Palliative Care):** The philosophy of palliative care can be successfully integrated into any hospital setting, including the ICU, when the focus of care also includes the prevention or amelioration of suffering and improving comfort and quality of life.
