### Question 1 (Clinical Pediatrics Vignette)
A pediatric patient is evaluated for the following clinical and diagnostic profile: Clinical Practice Guidelines by the Infectious Diseases Society of America [IDSA], American Academy of Neurology [AAN], and American College of Rheumatology [ACR]: 2020 Guidelines for the Preven tion, Diagnosis and Treatment of Lyme Disease.. Which of the following conditions or syndromes is the most likely diagnosis?

- **A.** Mycoplasma pneumoniae
- **B.** EBV (Splenomegaly)
- **C.** PROTON PUMP INHIBITORS
- **D.** Cardiac (Fever in the Older Child)

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: A** — Mycoplasma pneumoniae

**Rationale:** **Mycoplasma pneumoniae** is characterized by: Clinical Practice Guidelines by the Infectious Diseases Society of America [IDSA], American Academy of Neurology [AAN], and American College of Rheumatology [ACR]: 2020 Guidelines for the Preven tion, Diagnosis and Treatment of Lyme Disease..

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **EBV (Splenomegaly)** is incorrect (characterized by Viral hemophagocytic syndromes: CMV, EBV, HHV-6). **PROTON PUMP INHIBITORS** is incorrect (characterized by Omeprazole; 1.0-3.3 mg/kg/day; weigh 20 kg: 20 mg/day; Approved for use in those older than 1 month;). **Cardiac (Fever in the Older Child)** is incorrect (characterized by Pericarditis; Myocarditis; Endocarditis).
