### Question 3 (Differential Diagnosis (Movement Disorders))

- **B.** infectious or postinfectious causes of ataxia

**Correct Answer: A** — hyperkinetic

**Rationale:** hyperkinetic (characterized by excessive and involuntary movements) or hypokinetic (characterized by slow voluntary movements and a general paucity of movement).
