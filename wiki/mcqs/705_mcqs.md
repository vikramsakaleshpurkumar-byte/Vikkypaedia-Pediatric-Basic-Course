- **D.** APECED/APS-1 (Cutaneous Fungal Infections)

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **CHARACTERISTICS (Mpox (Monkeypox))** is incorrect (characterized by Enanthem; Sometimes, lesions first form on; the tongue and in the mouth.; Macules; 1-2; Macular lesi). **Eventration (Diaphragmatic Hernia)** is incorrect (characterized by Lung hypoplasia was initially thought to be solely caused by the compression of the lung from the he). **APECED/APS-1 (Cutaneous Fungal Infections)** is incorrect (characterized by No; Neutralizing antiinterleukin-17A,; antiinterleukin-17F, and/or; antiinterleukin-22 autoantibodie).

### Question 2 (Pediatric Genetics & Molecular Etiology)
A child is diagnosed with **Disorders of the Mucous Membranes** after presenting with Periungual fibromas that appear in late childhood should suggest a diagnosis of tuberous sclerosis.. Pathogenic variants in which of the following genes are most classically implicated?

- **A.** MYBPC3
- **B.** ATP7B
- **C.** PTEN
- **D.** SHOX

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: C** — PTEN

**Rationale:** Pathogenic variants in **`PTEN`** (The Skin) are associated with **Disorders of the Mucous Membranes** (Periungual fibromas that appear in late childhood should suggest a diagnosis of tuberous sclerosis.).

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **ATP7B** is incorrect (associated with Wilson Disease (ATP7B, OMIM #277900)). **SHOX** is incorrect (associated with Disorders Involving Ion Transporters).

</details>

### Question 5 (Pediatric Board Mastery (Disorders of the Mucous Membranes))
When formulating an evidence-based diagnostic and management plan for **Disorders of the Mucous Membranes** (*Part XXIX: The Skin*), which of the following pediatric clinical statements is **ACCURATE**? (Item 1)
