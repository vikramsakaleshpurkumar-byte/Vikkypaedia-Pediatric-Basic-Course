### Question 1 (Clinical Pediatrics Vignette)
A pediatric patient is evaluated for the following clinical and diagnostic profile: Long QT syndromes*; Inborn errors of metabolism; Child abuse; Myocarditis; Ductal-dependent CHD. Which of the following conditions or syndromes is the most likely diagnosis?

- **A.** CORONARY ARTERIAL DISEASE
- **B.** CONDUCTION SYSTEM ABNORMALITY/ARRHYTHMIA
- **C.** MYOCARDIAL DISEASE
- **D.** SIDS (Sudden Death)

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: D** — SIDS (Sudden Death)

**Rationale:** **SIDS (Sudden Death)** is characterized by: Long QT syndromes*; Inborn errors of metabolism; Child abuse; Myocarditis; Ductal-dependent CHD.

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **CORONARY ARTERIAL DISEASE** is incorrect (characterized by Anomalous origin*; Anomalous tract (tunneled); Kawasaki disease; Periarteritis; Arterial dissection). **CONDUCTION SYSTEM ABNORMALITY/ARRHYTHMIA** is incorrect (characterized by Long QT syndromes*; Brugada syndrome; Proarrhythmic drugs; Wolff-Parkinson-White syndrome; Complete ). **MYOCARDIAL DISEASE** is incorrect (characterized by Myocarditis; Hypertrophic cardiomyopathy*; Dilated cardiomyopathy; Arrhythmogenic (right ventricular).

</details>

- **C.** CORRECTED OR UNOPERATED CHD
- **D.** SIDS (Sudden Death)

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: C** — CORRECTED OR UNOPERATED CHD

**Rationale:** **CORRECTED OR UNOPERATED CHD** is characterized by: Aortic stenosis; Tetralogy of Fallot; Transposition of great vessels (postoperative atrial switch); Mitral valve prolapse; Hypoplastic left heart syndrome; Eisenmenger syndrome.

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **AORTOPATHIES (DISSECTION, RUPTURED AORTA)** is incorrect (characterized by Marfan syndrome; Loeys-Dietz syndrome; Takayasu aortitis; Smooth muscle dysfunction syndrome; Vascul). **CONDUCTION SYSTEM ABNORMALITY/ARRHYTHMIA** is incorrect (characterized by Long QT syndromes*; Brugada syndrome; Proarrhythmic drugs; Wolff-Parkinson-White syndrome; Complete ). **SIDS (Sudden Death)** is incorrect (characterized by Long QT syndromes*; Inborn errors of metabolism; Child abuse; Myocarditis; Ductal-dependent CHD).

### Question 5 (Pediatric Board Mastery (Sudden Death))
When formulating an evidence-based diagnostic and management plan for **Sudden Death** (*Part XVIII: The Cardiovascular System*), which of the following pediatric clinical statements is **ACCURATE**? (Item 1)

- **A.** Ventricular fibrillation (VF), although the most common final cause of sudden death in adults, is only the final cause in 10–20% of children with SCD.
- **B.** Clinical manifestations are permanently restricted to late adulthood and never present during infancy, childhood, or adolescence.
- **C.** Systemic tetracyclines and fluoroquinolones are universally required as routine prophylaxis in all healthy newborns.
- **D.** First-line management relies on immediate high-dose aspirin therapy in febrile infants.

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: A** — Ventricular fibrillation (VF), although the most common final cause of sudden death in adults, is only the final cause in 10–20% of children with SCD.

**Rationale:** **Pediatric Clinical Principle (Sudden Death):** Ventricular fibrillation (VF), although the most common final cause of sudden death in adults, is only the final cause in 10–20% of children with SCD.
