### Question 1 (Clinical Pediatrics Vignette)
A pediatric patient is evaluated for the following clinical and diagnostic profile: Mean; 10; 26; Median; 2; 23; Range; 0-408; 1-120. Which of the following conditions or syndromes is the most likely diagnosis?

- **A.** TEC (Acquired Pure Red Blood Cell Anemia)
- **B.** AGE AT DIAGNOSIS, MALE (MO)
- **C.** DISORDERS OF MITOCHONDRIAL DNA MAINTENANCE
- **D.** Acquired defects (Hypofunction of the Ovaries)

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: B** — AGE AT DIAGNOSIS, MALE (MO)

**Rationale:** **AGE AT DIAGNOSIS, MALE (MO)** is characterized by: Mean; 10; 26; Median; 2; 23; Range; 0-408; 1-120.

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **TEC (Acquired Pure Red Blood Cell Anemia)** is incorrect (characterized by Male:female; 1:1; 1:3). **DISORDERS OF MITOCHONDRIAL DNA MAINTENANCE** is incorrect (characterized by Nucleotide pool maintenance). **Acquired defects (Hypofunction of the Ovaries)** is incorrect (characterized by • Hyperprolactinemia; • Pituitary tumors; • Pituitary infarction; • Infiltrative disorders (histiocy).
