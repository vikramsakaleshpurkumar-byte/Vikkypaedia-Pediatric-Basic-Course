- **A.** CTSA
- **B.** MTTP
- **C.** TGFBR1
- **D.** IDUA

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: C** — TGFBR1

**Rationale:** Pathogenic variants in **`TGFBR1`** (The Cardiovascular System) are associated with **TGFBR1 (Diseases of the Blood Vessels (aneurysms and Fistulas))** (Loeys-Dietz syndrome, FTAAD).
