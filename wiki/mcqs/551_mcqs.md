### Question 1 (Clinical Pediatrics Vignette)
A pediatric patient is evaluated for the following clinical and diagnostic profile: The median age at diagnosis is approximately 2 years, and >90% of cases are diagnosed in children <5 years old.. Which of the following conditions or syndromes is the most likely diagnosis?

- **A.** TESTING TO CONSIDER
- **B.** Meconium Ileus, Peritonitis, Intestinal Obstruction, and Gastroschisis
- **C.** INFLAMMATORY CAUSES
- **D.** Retinoblastoma

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: D** — Retinoblastoma

**Rationale:** **Retinoblastoma** (associated with **`RB1`**) is characterized by: The median age at diagnosis is approximately 2 years, and >90% of cases are diagnosed in children <5 years old..

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **TESTING TO CONSIDER** is incorrect (characterized by Gastrointestinal). **Meconium Ileus, Peritonitis, Intestinal Obstruction, and Gastroschisis** is incorrect (characterized by MECONIUM PLUGS Meconium plugs syndrome refers to intestinal obstruction, usu ally in the distal colo). **INFLAMMATORY CAUSES** is incorrect (characterized by Cimetidine; d-Penicillamine; Procainamide; l-Tryptophan; L-dopa).

### Question 2 (Pediatric Genetics & Molecular Etiology)
A child is diagnosed with **Retinoblastoma** after presenting with The median age at diagnosis is approximately 2 years, and >90% of cases are diagnosed in children <5 years old.. Pathogenic variants in which of the following genes are most classically implicated?

- **A.** DGUOK
- **B.** HPD
- **C.** RB1
- **D.** MKRN3

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: C** — RB1

**Rationale:** Pathogenic variants in **`RB1`** (Cancer and Benign Tumors) are associated with **Retinoblastoma** (The median age at diagnosis is approximately 2 years, and >90% of cases are diagnosed in children <5 years old.).

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **DGUOK** is incorrect (associated with DGUOK Deficiency (Hepatocerebral MDS)). **MKRN3** is incorrect (associated with Physiology of Puberty).
