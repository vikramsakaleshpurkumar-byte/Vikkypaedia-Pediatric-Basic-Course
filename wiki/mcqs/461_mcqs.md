### Question 1 (Clinical Pediatrics Vignette)
A pediatric patient is evaluated for the following clinical and diagnostic profile: Spontaneous rupture of subpleural blebs; Drug use (smoking cigarettes, marijuana, crack cocaine, use of; e-cigarettes); Valsalva maneuver. Which of the following conditions or syndromes is the most likely diagnosis?

- **A.** Secondary (underlying lung disease)
- **B.** Noniatrogenic (Pneumothorax)
- **C.** Primary Idiopathic (no underlying lung disease)
- **D.** Iatrogenic (Pneumothorax)

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: C** — Primary Idiopathic (no underlying lung disease)

**Rationale:** **Primary Idiopathic (no underlying lung disease)** is characterized by: Spontaneous rupture of subpleural blebs; Drug use (smoking cigarettes, marijuana, crack cocaine, use of; e-cigarettes); Valsalva maneuver.

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **Secondary (underlying lung disease)** is incorrect (characterized by Congenital lung disease; • Congenital pulmonary airway malformation; • Bronchogenic cysts; • Pulmona). **Noniatrogenic (Pneumothorax)** is incorrect (characterized by Penetrating trauma; Blunt trauma). **Iatrogenic (Pneumothorax)** is incorrect (characterized by Thoracotomy; Thoracoscopy, thoracentesis; Tracheostomy; Tube or needle puncture; Mechanical ventilat).
