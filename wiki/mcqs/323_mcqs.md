**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **Enterococcus** is incorrect (characterized by Endocarditis, bacteremia, brain abscess, and toxic shock syndrome caused by SDSE have all been descr). **CBZ, PB (Seizures in Childhood)** is incorrect (characterized by —; No; Epilepsy of infancy with; migrating focal seizures; Infant; No remission; Ominous). **Infection (Disorders of the Sweat Glands)** is incorrect (characterized by Defervescence; Chronic illness).
