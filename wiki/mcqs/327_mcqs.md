### Question 1 (Clinical Pediatrics Vignette)
A pediatric patient is evaluated for the following clinical and diagnostic profile: Food or drink contaminated with Entamoeba cysts and oral-anogenital sex are the most common means of infection.. Which of the following conditions or syndromes is the most likely diagnosis?

- **A.** COL1A1, COL1A2 (Osteogenesis Imperfecta)
- **B.** Amebiasis
- **C.** SODIUM (Other Abnormalities of Arginine Vasopressin Metabolism and Action)
- **D.** TUBULAR BONE (Osteomyelitis)

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: B** — Amebiasis

**Rationale:** **Amebiasis** is characterized by: Food or drink contaminated with Entamoeba cysts and oral-anogenital sex are the most common means of infection..

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **COL1A1, COL1A2 (Osteogenesis Imperfecta)** is incorrect (characterized by OMIM as OI type I). **SODIUM (Other Abnormalities of Arginine Vasopressin Metabolism and Action)** is incorrect (characterized by Systemic dehydration; Low; Low; Decreased effective; plasma volume; Low; Low; Primary salt loss (non). **TUBULAR BONE (Osteomyelitis)** is incorrect (characterized by Femur; 25; Tibia; 24; Humerus; 13; Phalanges; 5; Fibula; 4; Radius; 4; Ulna; 2; Metatarsal; 2; Clavi).

### Question 5 (Pediatric Board Mastery (Amebiasis))
When formulating an evidence-based diagnostic and management plan for **Amebiasis** (*Part XV: Infectious Diseases*), which of the following pediatric clinical statements is **ACCURATE**? (Item 1)

- **A.** Food or drink contaminated with Entamoeba cysts and oral-anogenital sex are the most common means of infection.
- **B.** Diagnostic confirmation relies solely on plain skull radiographs without biochemical, microbiologic, or clinical correlation.
- **C.** Inheritance is exclusively holandric (Y-linked) with 100% penetrance restricted to male neonates.
- **D.** The condition is an entirely benign physiologic variant that never warrants laboratory or clinical evaluation.

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: A** — Food or drink contaminated with Entamoeba cysts and oral-anogenital sex are the most common means of infection.

**Rationale:** **Pediatric Clinical Principle (Amebiasis):** Food or drink contaminated with Entamoeba cysts and oral-anogenital sex are the most common means of infection.
