A pediatric patient is evaluated for the following clinical and diagnostic profile: Purulent anterior nasal discharge; Purulent or discolored posterior nasal; discharge; Nasal congestion or obstruction; Facial congestion or fullness; Facial pain or pressure alone or; exacerbated by bending forward; Hyposmia or anosmia; Fever (for ac. Which of the following conditions or syndromes is the most likely diagnosis?

- **A.** Severe intestinal and extraintestinal disease
- **B.** Cardiovascular (Delirium)
- **C.** Abnormalities of the Lids
- **D.** MINOR SYMPTOMS (Sinusitis)

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: D** — MINOR SYMPTOMS (Sinusitis)

**Rationale:** **MINOR SYMPTOMS (Sinusitis)** is characterized by: Purulent anterior nasal discharge; Purulent or discolored posterior nasal; discharge; Nasal congestion or obstruction; Facial congestion or fullness; Facial pain or pressure alone or; exacerbated by bending forward; Hyposmia or anosmia; Fever (for acute sinusitis only); Headache; Ear pain, pressure, or; fullness; Halit.

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **Severe intestinal and extraintestinal disease** is incorrect (characterized by Drug of choice:; Metronidazole; 750 mg PO tid × 7-10 days; 35-50 mg/kg/day PO in 3 doses × 7-10 days). **Cardiovascular (Delirium)** is incorrect (characterized by Anemia, shock, vasculitis, hypertensive; encephalopathy). **Abnormalities of the Lids** is incorrect (characterized by Nelson Pediatric Symptom-Based Diagnosis. 2nd ed.).

### Question 2 (Clinical Pediatrics Vignette)

- **A.** CARDIOMYOPATHY (Nonimmune Hydrops)
- **B.** MISCELLANEOUS (Central Nervous System Vasculitis)
- **C.** MINOR SYMPTOMS (Sinusitis)
- **D.** Sinusitis

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: D** — Sinusitis

**Rationale:** **Sinusitis** (associated with **`STS`**) is characterized by: Exacerbation of asthma is a potentially serious complication of colds..

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **CARDIOMYOPATHY (Nonimmune Hydrops)** is incorrect (characterized by • Tumors; • Neoplasias; • Myopathies; • Cardiosplenic syndromes; • Hereditary cardiomyopathies). **MISCELLANEOUS (Central Nervous System Vasculitis)** is incorrect (characterized by Antiphospholipid antibodies syndrome, Hodgkin and non-Hodgkin; lymphomas, neurosarcoidosis, inflamma). **MINOR SYMPTOMS (Sinusitis)** is incorrect (characterized by Purulent anterior nasal discharge; Purulent or discolored posterior nasal; discharge; Nasal congesti).

### Question 5 (Pediatric Board Mastery (Sinusitis))
When formulating an evidence-based diagnostic and management plan for **Sinusitis** (*Part XVII: The Respiratory System*), which of the following pediatric clinical statements is **ACCURATE**? (Item 1)

- **A.** Clinical manifestations are permanently restricted to late adulthood and never present during infancy, childhood, or adolescence.
- **B.** Exacerbation of asthma is a potentially serious complication of colds.
