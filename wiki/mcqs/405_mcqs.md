### Question 1 (Clinical Pediatrics Vignette)
A pediatric patient is evaluated for the following clinical and diagnostic profile: CN1: 0% [condition], unconjugated bilirubin 20–45 mg/dL, no response to phenobarbital, needs phototherapy/liver transplant; CN2: <10% [condition], responds to phenobarbital. Which of the following conditions or syndromes is the most likely diagnosis?

- **A.** Neonatal Hemochromatosis / GALD
- **B.** Crigler-Najjar Type 1 vs Type 2 (UGT1A1)
- **C.** Wilson Disease (ATP7B, OMIM #277900)
- **D.** Dubin-Johnson vs Rotor Syndrome

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: B** — Crigler-Najjar Type 1 vs Type 2 (UGT1A1)

**Rationale:** **Crigler-Najjar Type 1 vs Type 2 (UGT1A1)** (associated with **`UGT1A1`**) is characterized by: CN1: 0% UGT1A1, unconjugated bilirubin 20–45 mg/dL, no response to phenobarbital, needs phototherapy/liver transplant; CN2: <10% UGT1A1, responds to phenobarbital.

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **Neonatal Hemochromatosis / GALD** is incorrect (characterized by Maternal anti-fetal hepatocyte IgG (C5b-9 complement); neonatal liver failure at birth with normal A). **Wilson Disease (ATP7B, OMIM #277900)** is incorrect (characterized by Age >3 yr; ceruloplasmin 40–100 µg, hepatic Cu >250 µg/g dry wt, Kayser-Fleischer rings, Coombs-neg ). **Dubin-Johnson vs Rotor Syndrome** is incorrect (characterized by Both cause benign conjugated hyperbilirubinemia with normal ALT/AST/GGT; Dubin-Johnson (ABCC2/MRP2) ).

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **Crigler-Najjar Type 1 vs Type 2 (UGT1A1)** is incorrect (characterized by CN1: 0% UGT1A1, unconjugated bilirubin 20–45 mg/dL, no response to phenobarbital, needs phototherapy). **Neonatal Hemochromatosis / GALD** is incorrect (characterized by Maternal anti-fetal hepatocyte IgG (C5b-9 complement); neonatal liver failure at birth with normal A). **Wilson Disease (ATP7B, OMIM #277900)** is incorrect (characterized by Age >3 yr; ceruloplasmin 40–100 µg, hepatic Cu >250 µg/g dry wt, Kayser-Fleischer rings, Coombs-neg ).
