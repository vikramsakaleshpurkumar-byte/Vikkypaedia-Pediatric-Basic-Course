### Question 1 (Clinical Pediatrics Vignette)
A pediatric patient is evaluated for the following clinical and diagnostic profile: Birth-12 mo: 24 cm/yr; 12-24 mo: 10 cm/yr; 24-36 mo: 8 cm/yr; 6 cm/yr; Slowly decelerates before pubertal onset; Height typically does not cross percentile lines; Sigmoid-shaped growth; Adolescent growth spurt accounts for about; 15% of adult height;. Which of the following conditions or syndromes is the most likely diagnosis?

- **A.** ADOLESCENCE (Assessment of Growth)
- **B.** Nutrition and gastrointestinal conditions
- **C.** Variation of normal
- **D.** Genetic conditions

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: A** — ADOLESCENCE (Assessment of Growth)

**Rationale:** **ADOLESCENCE (Assessment of Growth)** is characterized by: Birth-12 mo: 24 cm/yr; 12-24 mo: 10 cm/yr; 24-36 mo: 8 cm/yr; 6 cm/yr; Slowly decelerates before pubertal onset; Height typically does not cross percentile lines; Sigmoid-shaped growth; Adolescent growth spurt accounts for about; 15% of adult height; Peak height velocity; Girls: 8 cm/yr; Boys: 10 cm/yr.

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **Nutrition and gastrointestinal conditions** is incorrect (characterized by Malnutrition; Celiac disease; Inflammatory bowel disease). **Variation of normal** is incorrect (characterized by Constitutional tall stature; Familial tall stature). **Genetic conditions** is incorrect (characterized by Marfan syndrome; Klinefelter syndrome; Sotos syndrome).
