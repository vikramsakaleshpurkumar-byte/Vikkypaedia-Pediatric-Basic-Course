### Question 1 (Clinical Pediatrics Vignette)
A pediatric patient is evaluated for the following clinical and diagnostic profile: 3p23-25; Unknown—not confirmed. Which of the following conditions or syndromes is the most likely diagnosis?

- **A.** MUSCULAR DYSTROPHIES SECONDARY TO NUCLEAR ENVELOPE DEFECTS
- **B.** MUSCULAR DYSTROPHIES SECONDARY TO RNA METABOLISM DEFECTS
- **C.** LGMD 1H (Muscular Dystrophies)
- **D.** AR/AD (Muscular Dystrophies)

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: C** — LGMD 1H (Muscular Dystrophies)

**Rationale:** **LGMD 1H (Muscular Dystrophies)** is characterized by: 3p23-25; Unknown—not confirmed.

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **MUSCULAR DYSTROPHIES SECONDARY TO NUCLEAR ENVELOPE DEFECTS** is incorrect (characterized by Emery-Dreifuss MD X1; Xq28; Emerin). **MUSCULAR DYSTROPHIES SECONDARY TO RNA METABOLISM DEFECTS** is incorrect (characterized by Myotonic dystrophy 1 (DM1); 19q13.3; Myotonic dystrophy-associated protein; kinase (DMPK)). **AR/AD (Muscular Dystrophies)** is incorrect (characterized by LGMD 2K (R11-POMT1–related LGMD); 9q34.1; Protein-O-mannosyltransferase (POMT1)).

</details>

### Question 2 (Clinical Pediatrics Vignette)
A pediatric patient is evaluated for the following clinical and diagnostic profile: LGMD 2K (R11-the causative gene–related LGMD); 9q34.1; Protein-O-mannosyltransferase (the causative gene). Which of the following conditions or syndromes is the most likely diagnosis?

- **A.** MDC 1C (Muscular Dystrophies)
- **B.** MUSCULAR DYSTROPHIES SECONDARY TO NUCLEAR ENVELOPE DEFECTS
- **C.** MUSCULAR DYSTROPHIES SECONDARY TO RNA METABOLISM DEFECTS
- **D.** AR/AD (Muscular Dystrophies)

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: D** — AR/AD (Muscular Dystrophies)

**Rationale:** **AR/AD (Muscular Dystrophies)** (associated with **`AR, POMT1`**) is characterized by: LGMD 2K (R11-POMT1–related LGMD); 9q34.1; Protein-O-mannosyltransferase (POMT1).

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **MDC 1C (Muscular Dystrophies)** is incorrect (characterized by 19q13.3; Fukutin-related protein (FKRP)). **MUSCULAR DYSTROPHIES SECONDARY TO NUCLEAR ENVELOPE DEFECTS** is incorrect (characterized by Emery-Dreifuss MD X1; Xq28; Emerin). **MUSCULAR DYSTROPHIES SECONDARY TO RNA METABOLISM DEFECTS** is incorrect (characterized by Myotonic dystrophy 1 (DM1); 19q13.3; Myotonic dystrophy-associated protein; kinase (DMPK)).
