### Question 1 (Clinical Pediatrics Vignette)
A pediatric patient is evaluated for the following clinical and diagnostic profile: 4p-syndrome; Wolf-Hirschhorn syndrome; Acrocephalosyndactyly type I; Apert syndrome; Acrocephalosyndactyly type III; Saethre-Chotzen syndrome; Acrocephalosyndactyly type V; Pfeiffer syndrome; Anus imperforate with hand,; foot, and ear anomalies; Town. Which of the following conditions or syndromes is the most likely diagnosis?

- **A.** MALFORMATIONS LIMITED TO THE MEMBRANOUS LABYRINTH
- **B.** CHD7 (Congenital Malformations of the Ear)
- **C.** EPONYM (Congenital Malformations of the Ear)
- **D.** EYA1 (Congenital Malformations of the Ear)

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: C** — EPONYM (Congenital Malformations of the Ear)

**Rationale:** **EPONYM (Congenital Malformations of the Ear)** is characterized by: 4p-syndrome; Wolf-Hirschhorn syndrome; Acrocephalosyndactyly type I; Apert syndrome; Acrocephalosyndactyly type III; Saethre-Chotzen syndrome; Acrocephalosyndactyly type V; Pfeiffer syndrome; Anus imperforate with hand,; foot, and ear anomalies; Townes-Brocks syndrome; Arteriohepatic dysplasia; Alagille syndrome; Branc.

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **MALFORMATIONS LIMITED TO THE MEMBRANOUS LABYRINTH** is incorrect (characterized by Complete membranous labyrinthine dysplasia; Limited membranous labyrinthine dysplasia; Cochleosaccul). **CHD7 (Congenital Malformations of the Ear)** is incorrect (characterized by Hypoplastic or absent SCC; Coloboma, heart defects, choanal atresia, growth; retardation, genital/ur). **EYA1 (Congenital Malformations of the Ear)** is incorrect (characterized by Hypoplastic cochlea, enlarged VA; Malleus or incus dysplasia, aberrant facial nerve).

- **A.** MALFORMATIONS LIMITED TO THE MEMBRANOUS LABYRINTH

- **C.** FGFR2 (Congenital Malformations of the Ear)
- **D.** CHD7 (Congenital Malformations of the Ear)

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: A** — MALFORMATIONS LIMITED TO THE MEMBRANOUS LABYRINTH

**Rationale:** **MALFORMATIONS LIMITED TO THE MEMBRANOUS LABYRINTH** is characterized by: Complete membranous labyrinthine dysplasia; Limited membranous labyrinthine dysplasia; Cochleosaccular dysplasia (Scheibe); Cochlear basal turn dysplasia.

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **EPONYM (Congenital Malformations of the Ear)** is incorrect (characterized by 4p-syndrome; Wolf-Hirschhorn syndrome; Acrocephalosyndactyly type I; Apert syndrome; Acrocephalosynd). **FGFR2 (Congenital Malformations of the Ear)** is incorrect (characterized by Vestibular dysplasia, high-riding; jugular bulb; Stapes footplate fixation; Branchio-oto-renal). **CHD7 (Congenital Malformations of the Ear)** is incorrect (characterized by Hypoplastic or absent SCC; Coloboma, heart defects, choanal atresia, growth; retardation, genital/ur).

### Question 5 (Pediatric Board Mastery (Congenital Malformations of the Ear))
When formulating an evidence-based diagnostic and management plan for **Congenital Malformations of the Ear** (*Part XXVIII: The Ear*), which of the following pediatric clinical statements is **ACCURATE**? (Item 1)

- **A.** Inheritance is exclusively holandric (Y-linked) with 100% penetrance restricted to male neonates.
- **B.** First-line management relies on immediate high-dose aspirin therapy in febrile infants.
- **C.** An unusually prominent or “lop” ear results from lack of bending of the cartilage that creates the antihelix.
- **D.** Diagnostic confirmation relies solely on plain skull radiographs without biochemical, microbiologic, or clinical correlation.

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: C** — An unusually prominent or “lop” ear results from lack of bending of the cartilage that creates the antihelix.

**Rationale:** **Pediatric Clinical Principle (Congenital Malformations of the Ear):** An unusually prominent or “lop” ear results from lack of bending of the cartilage that creates the antihelix.
