### Question 5 (Pediatric Board Mastery (Plasma Transfusions))

**Rationale:** **Pediatric Clinical Principle (Plasma Transfusions):** PLASMA TRANSFUSION IN NEONATES In neonates, clotting times are “physiologically” prolonged because of developmental deficiency of clotting proteins; plasma should be transfused only after reference to normal values is adjusted for the birthweight and age of the infant (not to normal ranges for older children and adults).
