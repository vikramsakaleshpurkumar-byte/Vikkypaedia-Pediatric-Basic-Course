### Question 5 (Pediatric Board Mastery (Inflammatory Bowel Disease))
When formulating an evidence-based diagnostic and management plan for **Inflammatory Bowel Disease** (*Part XVI: The Digestive System*), which of the following pediatric clinical statements is **ACCURATE**? (Item 1)

- **A.** IBD is caused by dysregulated or inappropriate immune response to environmental factors in a genetically susceptible host.
- **B.** Inheritance is exclusively holandric (Y-linked) with 100% penetrance restricted to male neonates.
- **C.** The condition is an entirely benign physiologic variant that never warrants laboratory or clinical evaluation.
- **D.** First-line management relies on immediate high-dose aspirin therapy in febrile infants.

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: A** — IBD is caused by dysregulated or inappropriate immune response to environmental factors in a genetically susceptible host.

**Rationale:** **Pediatric Clinical Principle (Inflammatory Bowel Disease):** IBD is caused by dysregulated or inappropriate immune response to environmental factors in a genetically susceptible host.

</details>
