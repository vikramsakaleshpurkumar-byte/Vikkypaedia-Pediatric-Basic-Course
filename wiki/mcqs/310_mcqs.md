### Question 1 (Clinical Pediatrics Vignette)
A pediatric patient is evaluated for the following clinical and diagnostic profile: PREVENTION Environmental and fomite transmission of HAdV occurs readily; therefore simple measures such as handwashing and cleaning are likely to reduce spread.. Which of the following conditions or syndromes is the most likely diagnosis?

- **A.** 6 MO (AS PER WHO) (Adverse and Allergic Reactions to Drugs)
- **B.** Rhinoviruses
- **C.** TISSUE CULTURE (Cytomegalovirus)
- **D.** MYOPATHIES PRESENTING IN CHILDHOOD

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: B** — Rhinoviruses

**Rationale:** **Rhinoviruses** is characterized by: PREVENTION Environmental and fomite transmission of HAdV occurs readily; therefore simple measures such as handwashing and cleaning are likely to reduce spread..

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **6 MO (AS PER WHO) (Adverse and Allergic Reactions to Drugs)** is incorrect (characterized by Complementary feeding should begin; no later than 6 mo of age; • In the breastfed infant, high-iron;). **TISSUE CULTURE (Cytomegalovirus)** is incorrect (characterized by Virus isolation; Detection of virus in clinical material; Requires prolonged culture periods; low se). **MYOPATHIES PRESENTING IN CHILDHOOD** is incorrect (characterized by Congenital myopathies: nemaline myopathy, centronuclear; myopathy, central core; Endocrine-metabolic).
