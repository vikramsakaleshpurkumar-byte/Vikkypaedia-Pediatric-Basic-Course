### Question 1 (Clinical Pediatrics Vignette)

Which of the following conditions or syndromes is the most likely diagnosis?

- **A.** Respiratory Syncytial Virus
- **B.** SYNDROMES PREDISPOSING TO NEUROBLASTOMA
- **C.** Acquired defects (Hypofunction of the Ovaries)
- **D.** FETAL (Heart Failure)

**Correct Answer: A** — Respiratory Syncytial Virus
