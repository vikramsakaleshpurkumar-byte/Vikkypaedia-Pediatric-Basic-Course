### Question 1 (Clinical Pediatrics Vignette)
A pediatric patient is evaluated for the following clinical and diagnostic profile: Eponym; Wermer syndrome; Sipple syndrome; Gorlin; syndrome; –; Gene. Which of the following conditions or syndromes is the most likely diagnosis?

- **A.** IF ALC <1,000/μL (Leukopenia)
- **B.** FAMILIAL MTC (Multiple Endocrine Neoplasia Syndromes)
- **C.** ABCC9 (Epidemiology and Genetic Basis of Congenital Heart Disease)
- **D.** HIGH SPECIFICITY (Abused and Neglected Children)

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: B** — FAMILIAL MTC (Multiple Endocrine Neoplasia Syndromes)

**Rationale:** **FAMILIAL MTC (Multiple Endocrine Neoplasia Syndromes)** is characterized by: Eponym; Wermer syndrome; Sipple syndrome; Gorlin; syndrome; –; Gene.

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **IF ALC <1,000/μL (Leukopenia)** is incorrect (characterized by Repeat blood counts in 3-4 wk; Transient leukopenia (e.g., viral)). **ABCC9 (Epidemiology and Genetic Basis of Congenital Heart Disease)** is incorrect (characterized by 12p12.1; PDA, BAV, HCM, CoA,). **HIGH SPECIFICITY (Abused and Neglected Children)** is incorrect (characterized by Classic metaphyseal lesions; Rib fractures, especially posteromedial; Scapular fractures; Spinous pr).
