### Question 1 (Clinical Pediatrics Vignette)
A pediatric patient is evaluated for the following clinical and diagnostic profile: Hypohidrotic ectodermal dysplasia. Which of the following conditions or syndromes is the most likely diagnosis?

- **A.** EDA/NF-κB PATHWAY (Ectodermal Dysplasias)
- **B.** EDA1 (Ectodermal Dysplasias)
- **C.** NEMO/IKBKG (Ectodermal Dysplasias)
- **D.** TRAF6 (Ectodermal Dysplasias)

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: A** — EDA/NF-κB PATHWAY (Ectodermal Dysplasias)

**Rationale:** **EDA/NF-κB PATHWAY (Ectodermal Dysplasias)** (associated with **`EDA`**) is characterized by: Hypohidrotic ectodermal dysplasia.

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **EDA1 (Ectodermal Dysplasias)** is incorrect (characterized by Ectodysplasin (EDA); Membrane ligand; Hypohidrotic ectodermal dysplasia). **NEMO/IKBKG (Ectodermal Dysplasias)** is incorrect (characterized by NF-kB essential modulator; NF-kB activation; Incontinentia pigmenti (females)). **TRAF6 (Ectodermal Dysplasias)** is incorrect (characterized by TNF receptor–associated; factor 6; Activates IKK; Hypohidrotic ectodermal dysplasia; with immune def).

### Question 3 (Pediatric Genetics & Molecular Etiology)
A child is diagnosed with **EDA/NF-κB PATHWAY (Ectodermal Dysplasias)** after presenting with Hypohidrotic ectodermal dysplasia. Pathogenic variants in which of the following genes are most classically implicated?

- **A.** PYGL
- **B.** OCRL
- **C.** EDA
- **D.** POU1F1

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: C** — EDA

**Rationale:** Pathogenic variants in **`EDA`** (The Skin) are associated with **EDA/NF-κB PATHWAY (Ectodermal Dysplasias)** (Hypohidrotic ectodermal dysplasia).

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **POU1F1** is incorrect (associated with POU1F1 (PIT1) (Hypopituitarism)).
