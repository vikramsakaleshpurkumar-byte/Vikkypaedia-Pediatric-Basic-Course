### Question 1 (Clinical Pediatrics Vignette)
A pediatric patient is evaluated for the following clinical and diagnostic profile: Osteopetrosis, infantile form, with nervous system; involvement. Which of the following conditions or syndromes is the most likely diagnosis?

- **A.** TCIRG, CLCN, SNX10
- **B.** CLCN7 (Osteopetrosis and Other Disorders Involving Defective Bone Resorption)
- **C.** TCIRG1, TNFSF11, PLEKHM1, CLCN7
- **D.** OSTM1 (Osteopetrosis and Other Disorders Involving Defective Bone Resorption)

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: A** — TCIRG, CLCN, SNX10

**Rationale:** **TCIRG, CLCN, SNX10** is characterized by: Osteopetrosis, infantile form, with nervous system; involvement.

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **CLCN7 (Osteopetrosis and Other Disorders Involving Defective Bone Resorption)** is incorrect (characterized by Osteopetrosis with renal tubular acidosis). **TCIRG1, TNFSF11, PLEKHM1, CLCN7** is incorrect (characterized by Osteopetrosis, late-onset, dominant form). **OSTM1 (Osteopetrosis and Other Disorders Involving Defective Bone Resorption)** is incorrect (characterized by Osteopetrosis, infantile form, osteoclast-poor with; immunoglobulin deficiency).

</details>
