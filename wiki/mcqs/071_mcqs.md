### Question 1 (Clinical Pediatrics Vignette)
A pediatric patient is evaluated for the following clinical and diagnostic profile: Premature infants with hemolysis caused by vitamin E deficiency also often have elevated platelet counts.. Which of the following conditions or syndromes is the most likely diagnosis?

- **A.** Vitamin K Deficiency
- **B.** IL23R/AR (Defects of Innate Immunity)
- **C.** INTERVENTION (Ebola and Other Viral Hemorrhagic Fevers)
- **D.** Infections (Bone Structure, Growth, and Hormonal Regulation)

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: A** — Vitamin K Deficiency

**Rationale:** **Vitamin K Deficiency** (associated with **`TTPA`**) is characterized by: Premature infants with hemolysis caused by vitamin E deficiency also often have elevated platelet counts..

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **IL23R/AR (Defects of Innate Immunity)** is incorrect (characterized by NTM, BCG, and tuberculosis; Antimycobacterials). **INTERVENTION (Ebola and Other Viral Hemorrhagic Fevers)** is incorrect (characterized by 1; Patients with suspected, probable, or confirmed Ebola virus disease; Oral rehydration; 2; Patient). **Infections (Bone Structure, Growth, and Hormonal Regulation)** is incorrect (characterized by Toxocara canis (cat-scratch); Treponema pallidum (syphilis); Borrelia burgdorferi (Lyme disease); On).
