### Question 1 (Clinical Pediatrics Vignette)
A pediatric patient is evaluated for the following clinical and diagnostic profile: Diagnosis is usually made by an upper gastrointestinal series and upper endos copy.. Which of the following conditions or syndromes is the most likely diagnosis?

- **A.** Hiatal Hernia
- **B.** Inadequate Intake (Electrolyte and Acid-Base Disorders)
- **C.** Functional Causes (Hypofunction of the Testes)
- **D.** PULMONARY AND EXTRAPULMONARY INFECTION

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: A** — Hiatal Hernia

**Rationale:** **Hiatal Hernia** is characterized by: Diagnosis is usually made by an upper gastrointestinal series and upper endos copy..

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **Inadequate Intake (Electrolyte and Acid-Base Disorders)** is incorrect (characterized by Ineffective breastfeeding; Child neglect or abuse; Adipsia (lack of thirst)). **Functional Causes (Hypofunction of the Testes)** is incorrect (characterized by Malnutrition and/or malabsorption; Any chronic disease (e.g., IBD or asthma); Celiac disease; Eating). **PULMONARY AND EXTRAPULMONARY INFECTION** is incorrect (characterized by Except meningitis†; 2 mo of isoniazid, rifampin, pyrazinamide, and; ethambutol daily or twice weekly).
