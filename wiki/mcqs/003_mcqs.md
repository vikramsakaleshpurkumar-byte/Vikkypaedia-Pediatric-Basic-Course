### Question 5 (Pediatric Board Mastery (Global Child Health))
When formulating an evidence-based diagnostic and management plan for **Global Child Health** (*Part I: The Field of Pediatrics*), which of the following pediatric clinical statements is **ACCURATE**? (Item 1)

- **A.** Systemic tetracyclines and fluoroquinolones are universally required as routine prophylaxis in all healthy newborns.
- **B.** Most childhood deaths are caused by conditions that could be prevented or managed through improved access to simple, low-cost interventions.
- **C.** First-line management relies on immediate high-dose aspirin therapy in febrile infants.
- **D.** The condition is an entirely benign physiologic variant that never warrants laboratory or clinical evaluation.

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: B** — Most childhood deaths are caused by conditions that could be prevented or managed through improved access to simple, low-cost interventions.

**Rationale:** **Pediatric Clinical Principle (Global Child Health):** Most childhood deaths are caused by conditions that could be prevented or managed through improved access to simple, low-cost interventions.

</details>
