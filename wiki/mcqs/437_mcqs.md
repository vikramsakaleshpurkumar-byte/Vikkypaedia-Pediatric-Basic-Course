### Question 1 (Clinical Pediatrics Vignette)
A pediatric patient is evaluated for the following clinical and diagnostic profile: Congenital absence of tracheal-supporting cartilages. Which of the following conditions or syndromes is the most likely diagnosis?

- **A.** SDHA, SDHB, SDHC, SDHD, SDHAF1, SDHAF2
- **B.** TP53 (Soft Tissue Sarcomas)
- **C.** PRIMARY TRACHEOMALACIA
- **D.** HESX1 (Hypopituitarism)

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: C** — PRIMARY TRACHEOMALACIA

**Rationale:** **PRIMARY TRACHEOMALACIA** is characterized by: Congenital absence of tracheal-supporting cartilages.

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **SDHA, SDHB, SDHC, SDHD, SDHAF1, SDHAF2** is incorrect (characterized by Complex III subunits and assembly factors). **TP53 (Soft Tissue Sarcomas)** is incorrect (characterized by Mosaic variegated aneuploidy; syndrome). **HESX1 (Hypopituitarism)** is incorrect (characterized by GH deficiency, variable for others, small AP, EPP, optic nerve hypoplasia; septo-optic dysplasia).

</details>
