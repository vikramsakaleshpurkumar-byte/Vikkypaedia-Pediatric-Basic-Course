### Question 1 (Clinical Pediatrics Vignette)
A pediatric patient is evaluated for the following clinical and diagnostic profile: Fusiform/cystic dilatation of extrahepatic CBD; associated with anomalous pancreaticobiliary junction; treat with complete cyst excision + Roux-en-Y hepaticojejunostomy. Which of the following conditions or syndromes is the most likely diagnosis?

- **A.** Malignancy Risk in Choledochal Cysts
- **B.** Congenital Hepatic Fibrosis (CHF)
- **C.** Todani Type I Choledochal Cyst (80–90%)
- **D.** Caroli Disease (Todani Type V) vs Caroli Syndrome

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: C** — Todani Type I Choledochal Cyst (80–90%)

**Rationale:** **Todani Type I Choledochal Cyst (80–90%)** is characterized by: Fusiform/cystic dilatation of extrahepatic CBD; associated with anomalous pancreaticobiliary junction; treat with complete cyst excision + Roux-en-Y hepaticojejunostomy.

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **Malignancy Risk in Choledochal Cysts** is incorrect (characterized by High lifetime risk of cholangiocarcinoma if cyst wall is left in situ; internal drainage without exc). **Congenital Hepatic Fibrosis (CHF)** is incorrect (characterized by Ductal plate malformation causing presinusoidal portal hypertension (splenomegaly, esophageal varice). **Caroli Disease (Todani Type V) vs Caroli Syndrome** is incorrect (characterized by Caroli disease: isolated saccular intrahepatic duct dilatation ('central dot sign'); Caroli syndrome).

</details>

### Question 2 (Clinical Pediatrics Vignette)
A pediatric patient is evaluated for the following clinical and diagnostic profile: Caroli disease: isolated saccular intrahepatic duct dilatation ('central dot sign'); Caroli syndrome: intrahepatic ectasia + congenital hepatic fibrosis + ARPKD (the causative gene). Which of the following conditions or syndromes is the most likely diagnosis?

- **A.** Caroli Disease (Todani Type V) vs Caroli Syndrome
- **B.** Congenital Hepatic Fibrosis (CHF)
- **C.** Malignancy Risk in Choledochal Cysts
- **D.** Todani Type I Choledochal Cyst (80–90%)

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: A** — Caroli Disease (Todani Type V) vs Caroli Syndrome

**Rationale:** **Caroli Disease (Todani Type V) vs Caroli Syndrome** (associated with **`PKHD1`**) is characterized by: Caroli disease: isolated saccular intrahepatic duct dilatation ('central dot sign'); Caroli syndrome: intrahepatic ectasia + congenital hepatic fibrosis + ARPKD (PKHD1).

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **Congenital Hepatic Fibrosis (CHF)** is incorrect (characterized by Ductal plate malformation causing presinusoidal portal hypertension (splenomegaly, esophageal varice). **Malignancy Risk in Choledochal Cysts** is incorrect (characterized by High lifetime risk of cholangiocarcinoma if cyst wall is left in situ; internal drainage without exc). **Todani Type I Choledochal Cyst (80–90%)** is incorrect (characterized by Fusiform/cystic dilatation of extrahepatic CBD; associated with anomalous pancreaticobiliary junctio).

### Question 3 (Differential Diagnosis (Cystic Diseases of the Biliary Tract and Liver))
During a pediatric consultation regarding **Cystic Diseases of the Biliary Tract and Liver**, which clinical entity or concept is described by the following profile? "of the primary cilium (congenital hepatic fibrosis, Caroli disease, Caroli syndrome, and polycystic liver disease)."

- **A.** ductal plate malformations
- **B.** Congenital hepatic fibrosis
- **C.** Childbirth (Global Child Health)
- **D.** Caroli Disease (Todani Type V) vs Caroli Syndrome

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: A** — ductal plate malformations

**Rationale:** ductal plate malformations of the primary cilium (congenital hepatic fibrosis, Caroli disease, Caroli syndrome, and polycystic liver disease).

### Question 4 (Differential Diagnosis (Cystic Diseases of the Biliary Tract and Liver))
During a pediatric consultation regarding **Cystic Diseases of the Biliary Tract and Liver**, which clinical entity or concept is described by the following profile? "(choledochocele within the duodenal wall), Type IV (multiple extrahepatic and intrahepatic cysts [IVA] or multiple extrahepatic cysts [IVB]), and Type V (Caroli disease): multifocal segmental saccular ectasia of the large intrahepatic bile ducts."

- **A.** Abdominal ultrasound
- **B.** Type III
- **C.** ductal plate malformations
- **D.** Todani Type I Choledochal Cyst (80–90%)

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: B** — Type III

**Rationale:** Type III (choledochocele within the duodenal wall), Type IV (multiple extrahepatic and intrahepatic cysts [IVA] or multiple extrahepatic cysts [IVB]), and Type V (Caroli disease): multifocal segmental saccular ectasia of the large intrahepatic bile ducts.

### Question 5 (Pediatric Board Mastery (Cystic Diseases of the Biliary Tract and Liver))
When formulating an evidence-based diagnostic and management plan for **Cystic Diseases of the Biliary Tract and Liver** (*Part XVI: The Digestive System*), which of the following pediatric clinical statements is **ACCURATE**? (Item 1)

- **A.** Inheritance is exclusively holandric (Y-linked) with 100% penetrance restricted to male neonates.
- **B.** The condition is an entirely benign physiologic variant that never warrants laboratory or clinical evaluation.
- **C.** Diagnostic confirmation relies solely on plain skull radiographs without biochemical, microbiologic, or clinical correlation.
- **D.** When Type V intrahepatic biliary ectasia is combined with congenital hepatic fibrosis (CHF) and autosomal recessive polycystic kidney disease (ARPKD, PKHD1 [263200]), it is termed Caroli syndrome.

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: D** — When Type V intrahepatic biliary ectasia is combined with congenital hepatic fibrosis (CHF) and autosomal recessive polycystic kidney disease (ARPKD, PKHD1 [263200]), it is termed Caroli syndrome.

**Rationale:** **Pediatric Clinical Principle (Cystic Diseases of the Biliary Tract and Liver):** When Type V intrahepatic biliary ectasia is combined with congenital hepatic fibrosis (CHF) and autosomal recessive polycystic kidney disease (ARPKD, PKHD1 [263200]), it is termed Caroli syndrome.

</details>
