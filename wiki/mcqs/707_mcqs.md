- **B.** RAG1

**Correct Answer: B** — RAG1

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **FANCA** is incorrect (associated with SYNDROMES PREDISPOSING TO NEUROBLASTOMA). **COL11A1** is incorrect (associated with Syndromes with Oral Manifestations).

### Question 5 (Pediatric Board Mastery (Cutaneous Fungal Infections))
When formulating an evidence-based diagnostic and management plan for **Cutaneous Fungal Infections** (*Part XXIX: The Skin*), which of the following pediatric clinical statements is **ACCURATE**? (Item 1)

- **A.** Diagnosis is made by culturing the organism at 32–33°C (89.6–91.4°F).
- **B.** The condition is an entirely benign physiologic variant that never warrants laboratory or clinical evaluation.
- **C.** Inheritance is exclusively holandric (Y-linked) with 100% penetrance restricted to male neonates.
- **D.** Diagnostic confirmation relies solely on plain skull radiographs without biochemical, microbiologic, or clinical correlation.

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Rationale:** **Pediatric Clinical Principle (Cutaneous Fungal Infections):** Diagnosis is made by culturing the organism at 32–33°C (89.6–91.4°F).
