### Question 5 (Pediatric Board Mastery (Acute Care of Respiratory Distress and Failure))

- **A.** Systemic tetracyclines and fluoroquinolones are universally required as routine prophylaxis in all healthy newborns.

**Rationale:** **Pediatric Clinical Principle (Acute Care of Respiratory Distress and Failure):** Inspiratory stridor indicates airway obstruction above the thoracic inlet, whereas expiratory wheezing results from airway obstruction below the thoracic inlet.
