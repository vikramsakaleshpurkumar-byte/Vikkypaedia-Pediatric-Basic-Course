### Question 1 (Clinical Pediatrics Vignette)
A pediatric patient is evaluated for the following clinical and diagnostic profile: Each results in a disorder caused by haploinsufficiency of the respective gene products.. Which of the following conditions or syndromes is the most likely diagnosis?

- **A.** Intrahepatic Sinusoidal Portal Hypertension
- **B.** Disorders Involving Transcription Factors
- **C.** ENDOCRINE CAUSES (Rare Tumors)
- **D.** PRIMATE BITES (Animal and Human Bites)

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: B** — Disorders Involving Transcription Factors

**Rationale:** **Disorders Involving Transcription Factors** (associated with **`LMX1B, RUNX2, SHOX, SOX9, SRY`**) is characterized by: Each results in a disorder caused by haploinsufficiency of the respective gene products..

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **Intrahepatic Sinusoidal Portal Hypertension** is incorrect (characterized by Biliary atresia (#1), AIH, Wilson disease, CFLD; splenomegaly + varices + impaired hepatic synthetic). **ENDOCRINE CAUSES (Rare Tumors)** is incorrect (characterized by Carbohydrate intolerance; Hyperadrenergic spells; Hypoglycemia; Pancreatic tumors (e.g., insulinoma)). **PRIMATE BITES (Animal and Human Bites)** is incorrect (characterized by Bacteroides species; Fusobacterium species; Eikenella corrodens; Streptococcus species; Enterococcus).
