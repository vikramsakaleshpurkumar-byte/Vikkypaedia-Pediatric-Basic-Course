### Question 1 (Clinical Pediatrics Vignette)
A pediatric patient is evaluated for the following clinical and diagnostic profile: CLEARANCE OF FETAL LUNG FLUID In utero, lung fluid is actively secreted by epithelial cells into air spaces.. Which of the following conditions or syndromes is the most likely diagnosis?

- **A.** ECG (Brief Resolved Unexplained Events and Other Acute Events in Infants)
- **B.** Transition to Newborn Pulmonary Respiration
- **C.** Congenital Hypergonadotropic Hypogonadism
- **D.** NEUROLOGIC, 0–2 POINTSi

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: B** — Transition to Newborn Pulmonary Respiration

**Rationale:** **Transition to Newborn Pulmonary Respiration** is characterized by: CLEARANCE OF FETAL LUNG FLUID In utero, lung fluid is actively secreted by epithelial cells into air spaces..

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **ECG (Brief Resolved Unexplained Events and Other Acute Events in Infants)** is incorrect (characterized by Echocardiogram; Serum electrolytes, calcium,; magnesium; Metabolic/; genetic; Hypoglycemia; Inborn e). **Congenital Hypergonadotropic Hypogonadism** is incorrect (characterized by Down syndrome; Noonan syndrome). **NEUROLOGIC, 0–2 POINTSi** is incorrect (characterized by Glasgow Coma Scale score; >10; pupils reactivej; Glasgow Coma Scale score; Fixed pupils bilaterally).
