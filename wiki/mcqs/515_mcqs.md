### Question 1 (Clinical Pediatrics Vignette)
A pediatric patient is evaluated for the following clinical and diagnostic profile: TOXINS AND VENOMS Bacterial sepsis caused by Haemophilus influenzae, staphylococci, or streptococci may be complicated by accompanying hemolysis.. Which of the following conditions or syndromes is the most likely diagnosis?

- **A.** Pulmonary Agenesis and Aplasia (Congenital Disorders of the Lung)
- **B.** Polycythemia
- **C.** LQTS (Drowning and Submersion Injury)
- **D.** Anatomy (Gay, Lesbian, and Bisexual Adolescents)

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: B** — Polycythemia

**Rationale:** **Polycythemia** is characterized by: TOXINS AND VENOMS Bacterial sepsis caused by Haemophilus influenzae, staphylococci, or streptococci may be complicated by accompanying hemolysis..

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **Pulmonary Agenesis and Aplasia (Congenital Disorders of the Lung)** is incorrect (characterized by CLINICAL MANIFESTATIONS AND PROGNOSIS Unilateral agenesis or hypoplasia can have few symptoms and no). **LQTS (Drowning and Submersion Injury)** is incorrect (characterized by • Lapse in supervision; • Change in weather; • Unfamiliarity with or change(s) in; water conditions:). **Anatomy (Gay, Lesbian, and Bisexual Adolescents)** is incorrect (characterized by • Queer people all want to change their bodies with surgeries or hormones; • Anything about the body).
