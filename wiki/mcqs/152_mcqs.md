### Question 1 (Clinical Pediatrics Vignette)
A pediatric patient is evaluated for the following clinical and diagnostic profile: All adolescents, regardless of SHCN, are likely to benefit from the autonomy, continuity of care, and self-management skills that are facilitated by a successful transition.. Which of the following conditions or syndromes is the most likely diagnosis?

- **A.** CHARACTERISTICS (Mpox (Monkeypox))
- **B.** ORTHOPOXVIRUSES (Reactive and Postinfectious Arthritis)
- **C.** Cutaneous Defects
- **D.** Transitioning to Adult Care

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: D** — Transitioning to Adult Care

**Rationale:** **Transitioning to Adult Care** is characterized by: All adolescents, regardless of SHCN, are likely to benefit from the autonomy, continuity of care, and self-management skills that are facilitated by a successful transition..

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **CHARACTERISTICS (Mpox (Monkeypox))** is incorrect (characterized by Enanthem; Sometimes, lesions first form on; the tongue and in the mouth.; Macules; 1-2; Macular lesi). **ORTHOPOXVIRUSES (Reactive and Postinfectious Arthritis)** is incorrect (characterized by Variola virus (smallpox); Vaccinia virus; Parvoviruses). **Cutaneous Defects** is incorrect (characterized by The differential diagnosis in neonates includes transient neonatal pustular melanosis, erythema toxi).

### Question 5 (Pediatric Board Mastery (Transitioning to Adult Care))
When formulating an evidence-based diagnostic and management plan for **Transitioning to Adult Care** (*Part XI: Adolescent Medicine*), which of the following pediatric clinical statements is **ACCURATE**? (Item 2)

- **A.** The condition is an entirely benign physiologic variant that never warrants laboratory or clinical evaluation.
- **B.** Systemic tetracyclines and fluoroquinolones are universally required as routine prophylaxis in all healthy newborns.
- **C.** First-line management relies on immediate high-dose aspirin therapy in febrile infants.
- **D.** The process begins with the development of a transition policy and its dissemination to all families of young adolescents, ensuring families understand that transition planning will be an element of health

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: D** — The process begins with the development of a transition policy and its dissemination to all families of young adolescents, ensuring families understand that transition planning will be an element of health

**Rationale:** **Pediatric Clinical Principle (Transitioning to Adult Care):** The process begins with the development of a transition policy and its dissemination to all families of young adolescents, ensuring families understand that transition planning will be an element of health maintenance and chronic care management visits through out the adolescent years.
