### Question 4 (Differential Diagnosis (Autoimmune Hepatitis))
During a pediatric consultation regarding **Autoimmune Hepatitis**, which clinical entity or concept is described by the following profile? "howing interface hepatitis (piece-meal necrosis) with a dense portal lymphoplasmacytic infiltrate, emperipolesis, and hepatocyte rosettes, and (4) MRCP in all pediatric AIH patients to detect concomitant autoimmune sclerosing cholangitis (ASC)."

- **A.** azathioprine (1–2 mg/kg/day)
- **B.** percutaneous liver biopsy
- **C.** Autoimmune Sclerosing Cholangitis (ASC)
- **D.** Budesonide

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: B** — percutaneous liver biopsy

**Rationale:** percutaneous liver biopsy showing interface hepatitis (piece-meal necrosis) with a dense portal lymphoplasmacytic infiltrate, emperipolesis, and hepatocyte rosettes, and (4) MRCP in all pediatric AIH patients to detect concomitant autoimmune sclerosing cholangitis (ASC).
