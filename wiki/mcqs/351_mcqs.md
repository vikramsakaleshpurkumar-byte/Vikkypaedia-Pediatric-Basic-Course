### Question 1 (Clinical Pediatrics Vignette)
A pediatric patient is evaluated for the following clinical and diagnostic profile: Gastrointestinal function varies with maturity; what is a physiologic event in a newborn or infant might be a pathologic symptom at an older age.. Which of the following conditions or syndromes is the most likely diagnosis?

- **A.** INFLAMMATORY DISORDERS
- **B.** Normal Digestive Tract Phenomena
- **C.** HIV (Toxic Neuropathies)
- **D.** PRIMARY MICROCEPHALY

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: B** — Normal Digestive Tract Phenomena

**Rationale:** **Normal Digestive Tract Phenomena** is characterized by: Gastrointestinal function varies with maturity; what is a physiologic event in a newborn or infant might be a pathologic symptom at an older age..

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **INFLAMMATORY DISORDERS** is incorrect (characterized by Alopecia areata; Atopic dermatitis; Contact dermatitis; Lichen planus; Psoriasis). **HIV (Toxic Neuropathies)** is incorrect (characterized by Leprosy; Lyme disease; Rabies; Serum sickness; West Nile virus; Zika virus). **PRIMARY MICROCEPHALY** is incorrect (characterized by Isolated; microcephaly; Microcephaly vera (autosomal recessive; microcephaly); Autosomal dominant mi).
