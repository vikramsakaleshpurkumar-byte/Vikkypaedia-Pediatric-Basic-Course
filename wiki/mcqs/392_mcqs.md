### Question 1 (Clinical Pediatrics Vignette)
A pediatric patient is evaluated for the following clinical and diagnostic profile: Vesicoureteric reflux; Renal agenesis; Renal dysplasia; Ureteral duplication; Cryptorchidism; Hypospadias; Bicornuate uterus; Vaginal septa. Which of the following conditions or syndromes is the most likely diagnosis?

- **A.** CENTRAL NERVOUS SYSTEM
- **B.** CARDIOVASCULAR (Surgical Conditions of the Anus and Rectum)
- **C.** GENITOURINARY (Surgical Conditions of the Anus and Rectum)
- **D.** GASTROINTESTINAL (Surgical Conditions of the Anus and Rectum)

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: C** — GENITOURINARY (Surgical Conditions of the Anus and Rectum)

**Rationale:** **GENITOURINARY (Surgical Conditions of the Anus and Rectum)** is characterized by: Vesicoureteric reflux; Renal agenesis; Renal dysplasia; Ureteral duplication; Cryptorchidism; Hypospadias; Bicornuate uterus; Vaginal septa.

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **CENTRAL NERVOUS SYSTEM** is incorrect (characterized by Seizure (atonic, absence, myoclonic-astatic); Hyperekplexia; Stroke, transient ischemic attack; Frie). **CARDIOVASCULAR (Surgical Conditions of the Anus and Rectum)** is incorrect (characterized by Tetralogy of Fallot; Ventricular septal defect; Transposition of the great vessels; Hypoplastic left). **GASTROINTESTINAL (Surgical Conditions of the Anus and Rectum)** is incorrect (characterized by Tracheoesophageal fistula; Duodenal atresia; Malrotation; Hirschsprung disease).
