- **D.** Ductal Plate Malformation / Ciliopathies

**Correct Answer: B** — Hepatic Diverticulum Specification (Weeks 3–4)

**Rationale:** **Hepatic Diverticulum Specification (Weeks 3–4)** (associated with **`GATA4, PROX1`**) is characterized by: Endodermal ventral foregut induced by cardiac FGF2 and septum transversum BMP4; regulated by FOXA1/2, GATA4/6, PROX1.

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **Alagille Syndrome (Morphogenesis of the Liver and Biliary System)** is incorrect (characterized by JAG1 (94%) or NOTCH2 (2–4%) variants; bile duct paucity, butterfly vertebrae, posterior embryotoxon,). **Ductal Plate Remodeling (Weeks 8–32)** is incorrect (characterized by JAG1/NOTCH2, SOX9, HNF6, and HNF1B drive periportal hepatoblasts into polarized intrahepatic bile du). **Ductal Plate Malformation / Ciliopathies** is incorrect (characterized by PKHD1 (ARPKD / Caroli syndrome); fibrocystin defect on primary cilia causing ectatic intrahepatic bi).

### Question 3 (Pediatric Genetics & Molecular Etiology)

- **A.** SLC25A20
- **B.** GATA4
- **C.** FANCG
- **D.** TGFBR1

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: B** — GATA4

**Rationale:** Pathogenic variants in **`GATA4`** (The Digestive System) are associated with **Hepatic Diverticulum Specification (Weeks 3–4)** (Endodermal ventral foregut induced by cardiac FGF2 and septum transversum BMP4; regulated by FOXA1/2, GATA4/6, PROX1).

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **SLC25A20** is incorrect (associated with SLC25A20 (Defects in Metabolism of Lipids)). **TGFBR1** is incorrect (associated with TGFBR1 (Diseases of the Blood Vessels (aneurysms and Fistulas))).
