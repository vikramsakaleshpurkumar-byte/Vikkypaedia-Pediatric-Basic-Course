### Question 2 (Clinical Pediatrics Vignette)

- **A.** DIARRHEA-ASSOCIATED HUS
- **B.** THROMBOTIC THROMBOCYTOPENIC PURPURA (TTP)
- **C.** HUS SECONDARY TO SYSTEMIC INFECTIONS
- **D.** ATYPICAL HUS DUE TO COMPLEMENT DYSREGULATION

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: A** — DIARRHEA-ASSOCIATED HUS

**Rationale:** **DIARRHEA-ASSOCIATED HUS** is characterized by: STEC (Escherichia coli O157:H7); STEC (E .coli 0121 and 0104:H4); Non-STEC (Shigella dysenteriae type 1).

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **THROMBOTIC THROMBOCYTOPENIC PURPURA (TTP)** is incorrect (characterized by Inherited ADAMTS13 deficiency (pathogenic variants); Acquired ADAMTS13 deficiency (antibody-mediated). **HUS SECONDARY TO SYSTEMIC INFECTIONS** is incorrect (characterized by Neuraminidase (Streptococcus pneumoniae)). **ATYPICAL HUS DUE TO COMPLEMENT DYSREGULATION** is incorrect (characterized by Factor H deficiency (pathogenic variants, autoantibodies); Factor I deficiency (pathogenic variants)).
