### Question 1 (Clinical Pediatrics Vignette)
A pediatric patient is evaluated for the following clinical and diagnostic profile: Treatment should be directed toward prevention and, if possible, discontinuation of cyclosporine or phenytoin.. Which of the following conditions or syndromes is the most likely diagnosis?

- **A.** SYNDROMIC CMC (Cutaneous Fungal Infections)
- **B.** RIGHT ARCH AND LIGAMENTUM/DUCTUS
- **C.** Physiologic (Allergic Rhinitis)
- **D.** Dental Trauma

**Rationale:** **Dental Trauma** is characterized by: Treatment should be directed toward prevention and, if possible, discontinuation of cyclosporine or phenytoin..
