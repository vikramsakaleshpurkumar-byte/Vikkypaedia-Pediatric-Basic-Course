### Question 1 (Clinical Pediatrics Vignette)
A pediatric patient is evaluated for the following clinical and diagnostic profile: Intractable epilepsy, developmental regression, cortical blindness + fulminant liver failure triggered by valproic acid; valproate & liver transplant contraindicated. Which of the following conditions or syndromes is the most likely diagnosis?

- **A.** Alpers-Huttenlocher Syndrome (POLG, OMIM #203700)
- **B.** DGUOK Deficiency (Hepatocerebral MDS)
- **C.** TRMU Deficiency (Reversible Infantile Liver Failure)
- **D.** Reye Syndrome (Secondary Mitochondrial Injury)

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: A** — Alpers-Huttenlocher Syndrome (POLG, OMIM #203700)

**Rationale:** **Alpers-Huttenlocher Syndrome (POLG, OMIM #203700)** [#203700] (associated with **`POLG`**) is characterized by: Intractable epilepsy, developmental regression, cortical blindness + fulminant liver failure triggered by valproic acid; valproate & liver transplant contraindicated.

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **DGUOK Deficiency (Hepatocerebral MDS)** is incorrect (characterized by Most common neonatal mitochondrial liver failure; cholestasis, lactic acidosis (lactate:pyruvate >25). **TRMU Deficiency (Reversible Infantile Liver Failure)** is incorrect (characterized by Acute infantile liver failure with severe lactic acidosis at 1–6 months; high rate of spontaneous co). **Reye Syndrome (Secondary Mitochondrial Injury)** is incorrect (characterized by Post-influenza/varicella + aspirin exposure; vomiting, encephalopathy, cerebral edema, hyperammonemi).

### Question 3 (Pediatric Genetics & Molecular Etiology)
A child is diagnosed with **Alpers-Huttenlocher Syndrome (POLG, OMIM #203700)** after presenting with Intractable epilepsy, developmental regression, cortical blindness + fulminant liver failure triggered by valproic acid; valproate & liver transplant contraindicated. Pathogenic variants in which of the following genes are most classically implicated?

- **A.** JAK3
- **B.** POLG
- **C.** STS
- **D.** ASS1

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: B** — POLG

**Rationale:** Pathogenic variants in **`POLG`** (The Digestive System) are associated with **Alpers-Huttenlocher Syndrome (POLG, OMIM #203700)** (Intractable epilepsy, developmental regression, cortical blindness + fulminant liver failure triggered by valproic acid; valproate & liver transplant contraindicated).

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **JAK3** is incorrect (associated with T– B+ NK– SCID (Orientation to the Consideration of Inborn Errors of Immunity)). **STS** is incorrect (associated with Sinusitis).
