### Question 1 (Clinical Pediatrics Vignette)
A pediatric patient is evaluated for the following clinical and diagnostic profile: Polycythemia; Persistent hypoxia; Phlebotomy if symptomatic; Relative anemia; Nutritional deficiency; Iron replacement; CNS abscess; Right-to-left shunting; Antibiotics, drainage; CNS thromboembolic stroke; Right-to-left shunting or polycythemia; Ant. Which of the following conditions or syndromes is the most likely diagnosis?

- **A.** CLINICAL FINDINGS (General Principles of Treatment of Congenital Heart Disease)
- **B.** THERAPY (General Principles of Treatment of Congenital Heart Disease)
- **C.** COARCTATION OF AORTA
- **D.** RESIDUAL LESIONS (SHUNTS)

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: B** — THERAPY (General Principles of Treatment of Congenital Heart Disease)

**Rationale:** **THERAPY (General Principles of Treatment of Congenital Heart Disease)** is characterized by: Polycythemia; Persistent hypoxia; Phlebotomy if symptomatic; Relative anemia; Nutritional deficiency; Iron replacement; CNS abscess; Right-to-left shunting; Antibiotics, drainage; CNS thromboembolic stroke; Right-to-left shunting or polycythemia; Anticoagulation, phlebotomy; Low-grade DIC, thrombocytopenia; Polycythemi.

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **CLINICAL FINDINGS (General Principles of Treatment of Congenital Heart Disease)** is incorrect (characterized by Pericardial friction rub; Elevated C-reactive protein (CRP); Elevated erythrocyte sedimentation rate). **COARCTATION OF AORTA** is incorrect (characterized by Essential hypertension; Recoarctation; Aneurysm formation). **RESIDUAL LESIONS (SHUNTS)** is incorrect (characterized by Ventral septal defect; Atrial septal defect; Patent ductus arteriosus).
