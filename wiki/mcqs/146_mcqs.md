### Question 1 (Clinical Pediatrics Vignette)
A pediatric patient is evaluated for the following clinical and diagnostic profile: Morphine; 0.05 mg/kg/dose q3h; Increase dose 10–20%; 10% of stabilizing dose q24h; >1 mg/kg/day of morphine; Unable to wean for 2 days; Methadone; 0.1 mg/kg/dose q6h for; 4 doses; Increase to q4h if unable to; capture; 0.7 mg/kg/dose q12h × 2 doses,;. Which of the following conditions or syndromes is the most likely diagnosis?

- **A.** N/A (Fetal Alcohol Spectrum Disorder)
- **B.** ADD ADJUVANT THERAPY
- **C.** PHYSIOLOGIC (Diagnosis of Allergic Disease)
- **D.** Pulmonary Hypoplasia (Congenital Disorders of the Lung)

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: B** — ADD ADJUVANT THERAPY

**Rationale:** **ADD ADJUVANT THERAPY** is characterized by: Morphine; 0.05 mg/kg/dose q3h; Increase dose 10–20%; 10% of stabilizing dose q24h; >1 mg/kg/day of morphine; Unable to wean for 2 days; Methadone; 0.1 mg/kg/dose q6h for; 4 doses; Increase to q4h if unable to; capture; 0.7 mg/kg/dose q12h × 2 doses,; then 0.05 mg/kg/dose q12h × 2; 0.04 mg/kg/dose q12h × 2; 0.03 mg/kg/d.

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **N/A (Fetal Alcohol Spectrum Disorder)** is incorrect (characterized by Clonidine; 1.5 μg/kg/dose q3h; 25% dose escalation q24h; 10% every day). **PHYSIOLOGIC (Diagnosis of Allergic Disease)** is incorrect (characterized by Prematurity; Infants receiving hyperalimentation; Hereditary). **Pulmonary Hypoplasia (Congenital Disorders of the Lung)** is incorrect (characterized by CLINICAL MANIFESTATIONS AND PROGNOSIS Unilateral agenesis or hypoplasia can have few symptoms and no).

### Question 2 (Clinical Pediatrics Vignette)

- **A.** N/A (Fetal Alcohol Spectrum Disorder)
- **B.** Mixed IgE Mediated and Non–IgE Mediated
- **C.** External Ear (General Considerations and Evaluation of the Ear)
- **D.** GAC (Patterns of Genetic Transmission)

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: A** — N/A (Fetal Alcohol Spectrum Disorder)

**Rationale:** **N/A (Fetal Alcohol Spectrum Disorder)** is characterized by: Clonidine; 1.5 μg/kg/dose q3h; 25% dose escalation q24h; 10% every day.

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **Mixed IgE Mediated and Non–IgE Mediated** is incorrect (characterized by Cutaneous—atopic dermatitis, contact dermatitis; Gastrointestinal—allergic eosinophilic esophagitis ). **External Ear (General Considerations and Evaluation of the Ear)** is incorrect (characterized by External otitis; Cerumen impaction; Foreign body; Perichondritis; Preauricular cyst or sinus; Impact). **GAC (Patterns of Genetic Transmission)** is incorrect (characterized by 5; 6-7; —; Exon; Oculopharyngeal; muscular; dystrophy; Proximal limb weakness,; dysphagia, ptosis).
