### Question 1 (Clinical Pediatrics Vignette)
A pediatric patient is evaluated for the following clinical and diagnostic profile: Plantar warts (verruca plantaris), although similar to the common wart, are caused by HPV type 1 and are usually flush with the surface of the sole because of the constant pressure from weight bearing.. Which of the following conditions or syndromes is the most likely diagnosis?

- **A.** Chloroquine phosphate (Aralen and generics)
- **B.** CMV (Epidemiology of Infections)
- **C.** MAJOR CAUSES (Encephalopathies)
- **D.** Cutaneous Viral Infections

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: D** — Cutaneous Viral Infections

**Rationale:** **Cutaneous Viral Infections** (associated with **`ADA, ATM, DOCK8, GATA2, NEMO`**) is characterized by: Plantar warts (verruca plantaris), although similar to the common wart, are caused by HPV type 1 and are usually flush with the surface of the sole because of the constant pressure from weight bearing..

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **Chloroquine phosphate (Aralen and generics)** is incorrect (characterized by Dose 1: 600 mg base (1,000 mg salt) PO; Doses 2-4 (3 additional doses) at 6, 24, and 48; hr: 300 mg ). **CMV (Epidemiology of Infections)** is incorrect (characterized by Enteric bacteria; Group B Streptococci; Haemophilus influenzae). **MAJOR CAUSES (Encephalopathies)** is incorrect (characterized by Spastic diplegia (35%); Periventricular leukomalacia; Periventricular cysts or scars in white matter).

- **B.** OTC

- **D.** NF2

**Correct Answer: C** — ADA

### Question 5 (Pediatric Board Mastery (Cutaneous Viral Infections))
When formulating an evidence-based diagnostic and management plan for **Cutaneous Viral Infections** (*Part XXIX: The Skin*), which of the following pediatric clinical statements is **ACCURATE**? (Item 1)

- **A.** Systemic tetracyclines and fluoroquinolones are universally required as routine prophylaxis in all healthy newborns.
- **B.** Plantar warts (verruca plantaris), although similar to the common wart, are caused by HPV type 1 and are usually flush with the surface of the sole because of the constant pressure from weight bearing.
- **C.** Diagnostic confirmation relies solely on plain skull radiographs without biochemical, microbiologic, or clinical correlation.
- **D.** The condition is an entirely benign physiologic variant that never warrants laboratory or clinical evaluation.

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: B** — Plantar warts (verruca plantaris), although similar to the common wart, are caused by HPV type 1 and are usually flush with the surface of the sole because of the constant pressure from weight bearing.

**Rationale:** **Pediatric Clinical Principle (Cutaneous Viral Infections):** Plantar warts (verruca plantaris), although similar to the common wart, are caused by HPV type 1 and are usually flush with the surface of the sole because of the constant pressure from weight bearing.
