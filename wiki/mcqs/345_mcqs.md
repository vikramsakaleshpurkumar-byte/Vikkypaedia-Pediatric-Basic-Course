### Question 1 (Clinical Pediatrics Vignette)
A pediatric patient is evaluated for the following clinical and diagnostic profile: It is most common in Asia, Latin America, and Central Europe.. Which of the following conditions or syndromes is the most likely diagnosis?

- **A.** PLEKHM1 (Other Inherited Disorders of Skeletal Development)
- **B.** Enteroendocrine Cell Dysfunction
- **C.** Trichinellosis (Trichinella spiralis)
- **D.** HEMOPHILIA B (Hereditary Clotting Factor Deficiencies (Bleeding Disorders))

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: C** — Trichinellosis (Trichinella spiralis)

**Rationale:** **Trichinellosis (Trichinella spiralis)** is characterized by: It is most common in Asia, Latin America, and Central Europe..

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **PLEKHM1 (Other Inherited Disorders of Skeletal Development)** is incorrect (characterized by PLEKHM1-related ARO). **Enteroendocrine Cell Dysfunction** is incorrect (characterized by Enteric anendocrinosis (NEUROG3); Proprotein convertase 1/3 deficiency (PCSK1)). **HEMOPHILIA B (Hereditary Clotting Factor Deficiencies (Bleeding Disorders))** is incorrect (characterized by Hemarthrosis; 50-60 IU/kg factor VIII concentrate† on day 1, then; 20 IU/kg the following day.
