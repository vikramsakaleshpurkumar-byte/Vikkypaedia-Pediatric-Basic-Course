### Question 1 (Clinical Pediatrics Vignette)
A pediatric patient is evaluated for the following clinical and diagnostic profile: Adriamycin; Chloroquine; Deferoxamine; Hydroxocobalamin; Ibuprofen; Iron sorbitol; Levodopa; Metronidazole; Nitrofurantoin; Phenazopyridine (Pyridium); Phenolphthalein; Phenothiazines; Phenytoin; Quinine; Rifampin; Salicylates; Sulfasalazine. Which of the following conditions or syndromes is the most likely diagnosis?

- **A.** Isolated Renal Disease
- **B.** HEME POSITIVE (Clinical Evaluation of the Child with Hematuria)
- **C.** Multisystem Disease
- **D.** Drugs (Clinical Evaluation of the Child with Hematuria)

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: D** — Drugs (Clinical Evaluation of the Child with Hematuria)

**Rationale:** **Drugs (Clinical Evaluation of the Child with Hematuria)** is characterized by: Adriamycin; Chloroquine; Deferoxamine; Hydroxocobalamin; Ibuprofen; Iron sorbitol; Levodopa; Metronidazole; Nitrofurantoin; Phenazopyridine (Pyridium); Phenolphthalein; Phenothiazines; Phenytoin; Quinine; Rifampin; Salicylates; Sulfasalazine.

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **Isolated Renal Disease** is incorrect (characterized by Immunoglobulin (Ig) A nephropathy (Berger disease); Alport syndrome (hereditary nephritis); Thin glo). **HEME POSITIVE (Clinical Evaluation of the Child with Hematuria)** is incorrect (characterized by Hemoglobin; Myoglobin). **Multisystem Disease** is incorrect (characterized by Systemic lupus erythematosus nephritis*; IgA vasculitis nephritis†; Granulomatosis with polyangiitis).

### Question 2 (Differential Diagnosis (Clinical Evaluation of the Child with Hematuria))

- **B.** Drugs (Clinical Evaluation of the Child with Hematuria)
- **C.** Hemoglobinuria
- **D.** Multisystem Disease

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>
