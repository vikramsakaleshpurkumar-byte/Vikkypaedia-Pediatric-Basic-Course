### Question 1 (Clinical Pediatrics Vignette)
A pediatric patient is evaluated for the following clinical and diagnostic profile: The hallmark of actinomycosis is contiguous spread that fails to respect tissue or fascial planes.. Which of the following conditions or syndromes is the most likely diagnosis?

- **A.** Celiac disease
- **B.** Alpers-Huttenlocher Syndrome (POLG, OMIM #203700)
- **C.** Actinomyces
- **D.** HISPANIC (The Epidemiology of Adolescent Health Problems)

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: C** — Actinomyces

**Rationale:** **Actinomyces** is characterized by: The hallmark of actinomycosis is contiguous spread that fails to respect tissue or fascial planes..

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **Alpers-Huttenlocher Syndrome (POLG, OMIM #203700)** is incorrect (characterized by Intractable epilepsy, developmental regression, cortical blindness + fulminant liver failure trigger). **HISPANIC (The Epidemiology of Adolescent Health Problems)** is incorrect (characterized by Deaths; 45.4; 79.8; 62.8; 23.4§; 29.3; Births†; 11.4; 25.8; 29.2; 2.7§; 18.2; Obese‡‡; 12.5; 18.2; 7).

### Question 5 (Pediatric Board Mastery (Actinomyces))
When formulating an evidence-based diagnostic and management plan for **Actinomyces** (*Part XV: Infectious Diseases*), which of the following pediatric clinical statements is **ACCURATE**? (Item 1)

- **A.** The hallmark of actinomycosis is contiguous spread that fails to respect tissue or fascial planes.
- **B.** The condition is an entirely benign physiologic variant that never warrants laboratory or clinical evaluation.
- **C.** Clinical manifestations are permanently restricted to late adulthood and never present during infancy, childhood, or adolescence.
- **D.** Diagnostic confirmation relies solely on plain skull radiographs without biochemical, microbiologic, or clinical correlation.

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: A** — The hallmark of actinomycosis is contiguous spread that fails to respect tissue or fascial planes.

**Rationale:** **Pediatric Clinical Principle (Actinomyces):** The hallmark of actinomycosis is contiguous spread that fails to respect tissue or fascial planes.

</details>
