### Question 1 (Clinical Pediatrics Vignette)
A pediatric patient is evaluated for the following clinical and diagnostic profile: Some cases may be caused by an increased sensitivity to estrogens and/or relative androgen resistance in the affected tissue.. Which of the following conditions or syndromes is the most likely diagnosis?

- **A.** RAF1/CRAF (Signaling Pathway Disorders)
- **B.** Viral myocarditis: Linear subepicardial or midwall LGE, commonly
- **C.** Gynecomastia
- **D.** Anomalies of (Complex Vascular Anomalies)

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: C** — Gynecomastia

**Rationale:** **Gynecomastia** is characterized by: Some cases may be caused by an increased sensitivity to estrogens and/or relative androgen resistance in the affected tissue..

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **RAF1/CRAF (Signaling Pathway Disorders)** is incorrect (characterized by Phosphatase; Kinase; Same as Noonan syndrome, but with possible; development of multiple skin lentig). **Viral myocarditis: Linear subepicardial or midwall LGE, commonly** is incorrect (characterized by involving the basal inferolateral wall, basal anterior septum,; mid-inferolateral wall, and basal to). **Anomalies of (Complex Vascular Anomalies)** is incorrect (characterized by Origin; Course; Number; Length; Diameter (aplasia, hypoplasia, stenosis, ectasia / aneurysm); Valves).

</details>
