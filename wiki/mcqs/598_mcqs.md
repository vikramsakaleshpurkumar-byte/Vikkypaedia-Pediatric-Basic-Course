### Question 1 (Clinical Pediatrics Vignette)
A pediatric patient is evaluated for the following clinical and diagnostic profile: Pregnancy and lactation/nipple stimulation; Stress; Exercise; Sexual intercourse. Which of the following conditions or syndromes is the most likely diagnosis?

- **A.** Antidepressants (Hyperpituitarism)
- **B.** Antiemetics (Hyperpituitarism)
- **C.** Antipsychotics (Hyperpituitarism)
- **D.** PHYSIOLOGIC (Hyperpituitarism)

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: D** — PHYSIOLOGIC (Hyperpituitarism)

**Rationale:** **PHYSIOLOGIC (Hyperpituitarism)** is characterized by: Pregnancy and lactation/nipple stimulation; Stress; Exercise; Sexual intercourse.

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **Antidepressants (Hyperpituitarism)** is incorrect (characterized by Tricyclics; Monoamine oxidase inhibitors; Selective serotonin reuptake inhibitors). **Antiemetics (Hyperpituitarism)** is incorrect (characterized by Metoclopramide; Domperidone). **Antipsychotics (Hyperpituitarism)** is incorrect (characterized by Typical; Phenothiazines; Haloperidol; Atypical; Risperidone; Clozapine; Olanzapine).

### Question 5 (Pediatric Board Mastery (Hyperpituitarism))
When formulating an evidence-based diagnostic and management plan for **Hyperpituitarism** (*Part XXIV: The Endocrine System*), which of the following pediatric clinical statements is **ACCURATE**? (Item 1)

- **A.** Clinical manifestations are permanently restricted to late adulthood and never present during infancy, childhood, or adolescence.
- **B.** Diagnostic confirmation relies solely on plain skull radiographs without biochemical, microbiologic, or clinical correlation.
- **C.** In some cases, the pituitary tumors result from stimulation with hypothalamic-releasing hormones and in other instances, as in McCune-Albright syndrome (MAS), the tumor is caused by activating pathogenic v
- **D.** The condition is an entirely benign physiologic variant that never warrants laboratory or clinical evaluation.

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: C** — In some cases, the pituitary tumors result from stimulation with hypothalamic-releasing hormones and in other instances, as in McCune-Albright syndrome (MAS), the tumor is caused by activating pathogenic v

**Rationale:** **Pediatric Clinical Principle (Hyperpituitarism):** In some cases, the pituitary tumors result from stimulation with hypothalamic-releasing hormones and in other instances, as in McCune-Albright syndrome (MAS), the tumor is caused by activating pathogenic variants of the GNAS1 gene that codes for the α subunit of Gsα, a guanine nucleotidebinding protein.

</details>
