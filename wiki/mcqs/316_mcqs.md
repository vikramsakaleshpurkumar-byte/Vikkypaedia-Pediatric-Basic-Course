### Question 1 (Clinical Pediatrics Vignette)
A pediatric patient is evaluated for the following clinical and diagnostic profile: PREVENTION Dengue vaccines have been under development continuously since the 1970s.. Which of the following conditions or syndromes is the most likely diagnosis?

- **A.** PHYSICAL CHANGES (Adolescent Pregnancy)
- **B.** HEPATITIS B VIRUS (Domestic and International Adoption)
- **C.** DIAGNOSIS (Dermatologic Evaluation of the Patient)
- **D.** Yellow Fever

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: D** — Yellow Fever

**Rationale:** **Yellow Fever** is characterized by: PREVENTION Dengue vaccines have been under development continuously since the 1970s..

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **PHYSICAL CHANGES (Adolescent Pregnancy)** is incorrect (characterized by 2-3 wk after implantation: cervical softening and cyanosis.; 8 wk: uterus size of orange.; 12 wk: ut). **HEPATITIS B VIRUS (Domestic and International Adoption)** is incorrect (characterized by Hepatitis B surface antigen (HBsAg); Alla; Hepatitis B surface antibody (anti-HBs); Alla; Hepatitis ). **DIAGNOSIS (Dermatologic Evaluation of the Patient)** is incorrect (characterized by Systemic lupus; erythematosus; Any; Erythematous; patches and; plaques; palpable; purpura; livedo; r).
