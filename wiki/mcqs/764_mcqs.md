### Question 1 (Clinical Pediatrics Vignette)
A pediatric patient is evaluated for the following clinical and diagnostic profile: CLINICAL FEATURES AND DIAGNOSIS There are many examples of this pediatric condition throughout history.. Which of the following conditions or syndromes is the most likely diagnosis?

- **A.** Mass Psychogenic Illness
- **B.** (2010 TO PRESENT) (Overview of Pediatrics)
- **C.** TENSION PNEUMOTHORAX
- **D.** T12 (Meningomyelocele (Spina Bifida))

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: A** — Mass Psychogenic Illness

**Rationale:** **Mass Psychogenic Illness** is characterized by: CLINICAL FEATURES AND DIAGNOSIS There are many examples of mass psychogenic illness throughout history..

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **(2010 TO PRESENT) (Overview of Pediatrics)** is incorrect (characterized by Behavioral disorders/mental health; School problems; Mood and anxiety disorders; Adolescent suicide/). **TENSION PNEUMOTHORAX** is incorrect (characterized by One-way valve leak from the lung parenchyma or tracheobronchial; tree; Lung collapse with mediastina). **T12 (Meningomyelocele (Spina Bifida))** is incorrect (characterized by Totally paralyzed lower limbs; Standing brace, wheelchair; Wheelchair; Wheelchair, no ambulation).
