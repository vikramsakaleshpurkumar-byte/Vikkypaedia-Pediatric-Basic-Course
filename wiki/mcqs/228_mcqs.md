**Correct Answer: A** — CONDITION (Streptococcus pneumoniae (Pneumococcus))

**Rationale:** **CONDITION (Streptococcus pneumoniae (Pneumococcus))** is characterized by: Immunocompetent children; Chronic heart disease; Chronic lung disease†; Chronic kidney disease (excluding; dialysis and nephrotic syndrome); Chronic liver disease; Diabetes mellitus; Cerebrospinal fluid leaks; Cochlear implant; Children with; immunocompromising; conditions; HIV infection; Maintenance dialysis or nephro.

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **MANUFACTURER (Streptococcus pneumoniae (Pneumococcus))** is incorrect (characterized by Diphtheria CRM197 protein). **23F, 32F (Streptococcus pneumoniae (Pneumococcus))** is incorrect (characterized by Merck Sharp and Dohme (PCV15, Prevnar 15); Diphtheria CRM197 protein). **15B, 18C, 19A, 19F, 22F, 23F, 32F** is incorrect (characterized by Merck Sharp and Dohme (PCV20, Prevnar 20); None).
