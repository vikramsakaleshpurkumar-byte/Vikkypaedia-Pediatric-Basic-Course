### Question 1 (Clinical Pediatrics Vignette)
A pediatric patient is evaluated for the following clinical and diagnostic profile: Takes > 10 min to coordinate feeding with hunger cues; < 10 min for breastfeeds; < 10 mL of a feed. Which of the following conditions or syndromes is the most likely diagnosis?

- **A.** Nonpharmacologic interventions include rooming in, caregiver
- **B.** SLEEPS (Neonatal Abstinence Syndrome)
- **C.** EATS (Neonatal Abstinence Syndrome)
- **D.** CONSOLES (Neonatal Abstinence Syndrome)

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: C** — EATS (Neonatal Abstinence Syndrome)

**Rationale:** **EATS (Neonatal Abstinence Syndrome)** is characterized by: Takes > 10 min to coordinate feeding with hunger cues; < 10 min for breastfeeds; < 10 mL of a feed.

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **Nonpharmacologic interventions include rooming in, caregiver** is incorrect (characterized by presence, skin to skin contact, holding/cuddling, safe swaddling; (avoid face), feed on demand after). **SLEEPS (Neonatal Abstinence Syndrome)** is incorrect (characterized by < 1 hr after a feed). **CONSOLES (Neonatal Abstinence Syndrome)** is incorrect (characterized by Takes > 10 min to be consoled; Cannot stay consoled for 10 min).

</details>
