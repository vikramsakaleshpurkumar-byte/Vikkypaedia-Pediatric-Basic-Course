### Question 1 (Clinical Pediatrics Vignette)
A pediatric patient is evaluated for the following clinical and diagnostic profile: Several federal and hospi tal programs in the United States and elsewhere focus on prevention initiatives to reduce device-related HAI rates, most frequently central line–associated bloodstream infection (CLABSI), catheter-associated urinary tract in. Which of the following conditions or syndromes is the most likely diagnosis?

- **A.** QUALITY OF LIFE, BUT PREMATURE DEATH IS STILL POSSIBLE
- **B.** Infection Associated with Medical Devices
- **C.** 2 points) (Shock)
- **D.** ANATOMIC (Thrombotic Disorders in Children)

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: B** — Infection Associated with Medical Devices

**Rationale:** **Infection Associated with Medical Devices** is characterized by: Several federal and hospi tal programs in the United States and elsewhere focus on prevention initiatives to reduce device-related HAI rates, most frequently central line–associated bloodstream infection (CLABSI), catheter-associated urinary tract infection (CAUTI), and ventilator-associated pneumonia (VAP)..

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **QUALITY OF LIFE, BUT PREMATURE DEATH IS STILL POSSIBLE** is incorrect (characterized by Cystic fibrosis; Severe immunodeficiency; High-risk solid-organ transplant candidates and/or recipie). **2 points) (Shock)** is incorrect (characterized by Platelets ≥100 × 103/μL; Platelets 1.3; D-dimer ≤2 mg/L FEU; D-dimer >2 mg/L FEU; Fibrinogen ≥100 mg). **ANATOMIC (Thrombotic Disorders in Children)** is incorrect (characterized by Thoracic outlet obstruction (Paget-Schroetter syndrome); Iliac vein compression syndrome (May-Thurne).
