### Question 1 (Clinical Pediatrics Vignette)
A pediatric patient is evaluated for the following clinical and diagnostic profile: The most common presentation is respira tory distress caused by compression of the adjacent airways.. Which of the following conditions or syndromes is the most likely diagnosis?

- **A.** MALIGNANCY HISTIOCYTOSES
- **B.** Antimicrobials (Acne)
- **C.** Episodic Syndromes that May Be Associated with Migraine
- **D.** Obstructing Disorders of the Esophagus

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: D** — Obstructing Disorders of the Esophagus

**Rationale:** **Obstructing Disorders of the Esophagus** is characterized by: The most common presentation is respira tory distress caused by compression of the adjacent airways..

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **MALIGNANCY HISTIOCYTOSES** is incorrect (characterized by Primary, phenotypic subtypes; Secondary, phenotypic subtypes). **Antimicrobials (Acne)** is incorrect (characterized by Benzoyl peroxide, 2.5–10%; Applied once or twice daily; Benzoyl peroxide can bleach; clothing and be). **Episodic Syndromes that May Be Associated with Migraine** is incorrect (characterized by Recurrent gastrointestinal disturbance; Cyclical vomiting syndrome; Abdominal migraine; Benign parox).
