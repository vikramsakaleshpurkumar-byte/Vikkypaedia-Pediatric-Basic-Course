- **A.** Strongyloidiasis (Strongyloides stercoralis)

- **C.** DISEASE (Histiocytosis Syndromes of Childhood)

### Question 5 (Pediatric Board Mastery (Strongyloidiasis (Strongyloides stercoralis)))
When formulating an evidence-based diagnostic and management plan for **Strongyloidiasis (Strongyloides stercoralis)** (*Part XV: Infectious Diseases*), which of the following pediatric clinical statements is **ACCURATE**? (Item 1)

- **A.** Clinical manifestations are permanently restricted to late adulthood and never present during infancy, childhood, or adolescence.
- **B.** It is prevalent in regions with temperate climates and is the most common helminth infection in the United States.
- **C.** First-line management relies on immediate high-dose aspirin therapy in febrile infants.
- **D.** The condition is an entirely benign physiologic variant that never warrants laboratory or clinical evaluation.

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: B** — It is prevalent in regions with temperate climates and is the most common helminth infection in the United States.

**Rationale:** **Pediatric Clinical Principle (Strongyloidiasis (Strongyloides stercoralis)):** It is prevalent in regions with temperate climates and is the most common helminth infection in the United States.
