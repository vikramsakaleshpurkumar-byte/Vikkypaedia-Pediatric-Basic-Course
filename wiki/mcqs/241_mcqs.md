### Question 1 (Clinical Pediatrics Vignette)
A pediatric patient is evaluated for the following clinical and diagnostic profile: ETIOLOGY AND EPIDEMIOLOGY this pediatric condition is caused by Haemophilus ducreyi, a fastidious gramnegative bacillus.. Which of the following conditions or syndromes is the most likely diagnosis?

- **A.** PCR (Spotted Fever Group Rickettsioses)
- **B.** Chancroid (Haemophilus ducreyi)
- **C.** (EPP) (The Porphyrias)
- **D.** SUGGESTED DOSE (Candida)

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: B** — Chancroid (Haemophilus ducreyi)

**Rationale:** **Chancroid (Haemophilus ducreyi)** is characterized by: ETIOLOGY AND EPIDEMIOLOGY Chancroid is caused by Haemophilus ducreyi, a fastidious gramnegative bacillus..

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **PCR (Spotted Fever Group Rickettsioses)** is incorrect (characterized by Doxycycline; Flea-borne spotted; fever; Rickettsia felis; Flea bite; Opossums; Cats; Dogs; Western; ). **(EPP) (The Porphyrias)** is incorrect (characterized by Ferrochelatase (FECH); Autosomal recessive (most; commonly heteroallelic; with hypomorphic allele); ). **SUGGESTED DOSE (Candida)** is incorrect (characterized by Amphotericin B deoxycholate; Yes (multiple); 1 mg/kg/day; Amphotericin B lipid complex; Yes (single)).
