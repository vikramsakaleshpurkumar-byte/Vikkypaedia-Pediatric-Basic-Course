### Question 1 (Clinical Pediatrics Vignette)
A pediatric patient is evaluated for the following clinical and diagnostic profile: It is believed that the amenorrhea results from reduced energy avail ability, defined as energy intake minus expenditure.. Which of the following conditions or syndromes is the most likely diagnosis?

- **A.** MODERATE SPECIFICITY
- **B.** TEC (Acquired Pure Red Blood Cell Anemia)
- **C.** Nutrition and Endocrine Conditions in Athletes
- **D.** Alagille Syndrome (Morphogenesis of the Liver and Biliary System)

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: C** — Nutrition and Endocrine Conditions in Athletes

**Rationale:** **Nutrition and Endocrine Conditions in Athletes** is characterized by: It is believed that the amenorrhea results from reduced energy avail ability, defined as energy intake minus expenditure..

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **MODERATE SPECIFICITY** is incorrect (characterized by Multiple fractures, especially bilateral; Fractures of different ages; Epiphyseal separations; Verte). **TEC (Acquired Pure Red Blood Cell Anemia)** is incorrect (characterized by Male:female; 1:1; 1:3). **Alagille Syndrome (Morphogenesis of the Liver and Biliary System)** is incorrect (characterized by JAG1 (94%) or NOTCH2 (2–4%) variants; bile duct paucity, butterfly vertebrae, posterior embryotoxon,).
