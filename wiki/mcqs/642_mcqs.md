### Question 1 (Clinical Pediatrics Vignette)
A pediatric patient is evaluated for the following clinical and diagnostic profile: Varicella-zoster virus, HIV, hepatitis C virus, cytomegalovirus,; parvovirus B19. Which of the following conditions or syndromes is the most likely diagnosis?

- **A.** MISCELLANEOUS (Central Nervous System Vasculitis)
- **B.** BACTERIAL INFECTIONS
- **C.** VIRAL INFECTIONS (Central Nervous System Vasculitis)
- **D.** FUNGAL INFECTIONS (Central Nervous System Vasculitis)

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: C** — VIRAL INFECTIONS (Central Nervous System Vasculitis)

**Rationale:** **VIRAL INFECTIONS (Central Nervous System Vasculitis)** is characterized by: Varicella-zoster virus, HIV, hepatitis C virus, cytomegalovirus,; parvovirus B19.

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **MISCELLANEOUS (Central Nervous System Vasculitis)** is incorrect (characterized by Antiphospholipid antibodies syndrome, Hodgkin and non-Hodgkin; lymphomas, neurosarcoidosis, inflamma). **BACTERIAL INFECTIONS** is incorrect (characterized by Treponema pallidum, Borrelia burgdorferi, Mycobacterium; tuberculosis, Mycoplasma pneumoniae, Barton). **FUNGAL INFECTIONS (Central Nervous System Vasculitis)** is incorrect (characterized by Aspergillosis, mucormycosis, coccidioidomycosis, candidiasis).
