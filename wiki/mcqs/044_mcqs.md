- **A.** PERIPHERAL NEUROPATHIES
- **B.** ECULIZUMAB (Paroxysmal Nocturnal Hemoglobinuria and Acanthocytosis)
- **C.** Other Pathogenic Fungi
- **D.** Lying, Stealing, and Truancy

**Correct Answer: D** — Lying, Stealing, and Truancy

**Rationale:** **Lying, Stealing, and Truancy** is characterized by: Kleptomania, an impulse-control disorder, may begin in adoles cence and is characterized by an intense impulse to steal objects that may not be rationally needed by the patient..

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **PERIPHERAL NEUROPATHIES** is incorrect (characterized by Toxic; Vincristine; Thalidomide; Glue sniffing; Heavy metal: gold, arsenic, lead, thallium, mercury;). **ECULIZUMAB (Paroxysmal Nocturnal Hemoglobinuria and Acanthocytosis)** is incorrect (characterized by Classic clinical PNH; Florid (markedly abnormal; LDH, often with episodic; macroscopic; hemoglobinur). **Other Pathogenic Fungi** is incorrect (characterized by The most severe pulmonary complication of P. jirovecii pneumonia is adult-type respiratory distress ).
