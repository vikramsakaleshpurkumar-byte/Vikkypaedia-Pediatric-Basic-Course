### Question 1 (Clinical Pediatrics Vignette)

Which of the following conditions or syndromes is the most likely diagnosis?

- **A.** GnRH Deficiency and Defective Sense of Smell
- **B.** Congenital (Hypofunction of the Testes)
- **C.** Systemic Causes (Hypofunction of the Testes)
- **D.** Functional Causes (Hypofunction of the Testes)

**Correct Answer: B** — Congenital (Hypofunction of the Testes)

**Rationale:** **Congenital (Hypofunction of the Testes)** is characterized by: Follicle-stimulating hormone (FSH) and luteinizing hormone (LH); resistance; Pathogenic variants in steroid synthetic pathways; Gonadal dysgenesis; Klinefelter syndrome (47,XXY); Noonan syndrome (RASopathy genes); Cystic fibrosis (infertility).

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **GnRH Deficiency and Defective Sense of Smell** is incorrect (characterized by Kallmann syndrome). **Systemic Causes (Hypofunction of the Testes)** is incorrect (characterized by Hemochromatosis; Sarcoidosis; Histiocytosis; Thalassemia). **Functional Causes (Hypofunction of the Testes)** is incorrect (characterized by Malnutrition and/or malabsorption; Any chronic disease (e.g., IBD or asthma); Celiac disease; Eating).

### Question 5 (Pediatric Board Mastery (Hypofunction of the Testes))
When formulating an evidence-based diagnostic and management plan for **Hypofunction of the Testes** (*Part XXIV: The Endocrine System*), which of the following pediatric clinical statements is **ACCURATE**? (Item 1)

- **A.** The condition is an entirely benign physiologic variant that never warrants laboratory or clinical evaluation.
- **B.** First-line management relies on immediate high-dose aspirin therapy in febrile infants.
- **C.** Clinical manifestations are permanently restricted to late adulthood and never present during infancy, childhood, or adolescence.
- **D.** Maturation and closure of the epiphyses is estrogen-dependent, as demonstrated by a very tall 28-year-old, normally masculinized male with continued growth as a result of incomplete closure of the epiphyse

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: D** — Maturation and closure of the epiphyses is estrogen-dependent, as demonstrated by a very tall 28-year-old, normally masculinized male with continued growth as a result of incomplete closure of the epiphyse

**Rationale:** **Pediatric Clinical Principle (Hypofunction of the Testes):** Maturation and closure of the epiphyses is estrogen-dependent, as demonstrated by a very tall 28-year-old, normally masculinized male with continued growth as a result of incomplete closure of the epiphyses, who had complete estrogen insensitivity caused by an estrogen receptor defect.
