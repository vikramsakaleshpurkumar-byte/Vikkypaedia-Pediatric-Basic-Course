### Question 1 (Clinical Pediatrics Vignette)
A pediatric patient is evaluated for the following clinical and diagnostic profile: The first key to the management of temper tantrums and breathholding spells is to help parents intervene before the child is highly distressed.. Which of the following conditions or syndromes is the most likely diagnosis?

- **A.** Tantrums and Breath-Holding Spells
- **B.** DESCRIPTION (Trauma to the Genitourinary Tract)
- **C.** OSMORECEPTOR DYSFUNCTION
- **D.** COMMON (>50%) (Human Metapneumovirus)

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: A** — Tantrums and Breath-Holding Spells

**Rationale:** **Tantrums and Breath-Holding Spells** is characterized by: The first key to the management of temper tantrums and breathholding spells is to help parents intervene before the child is highly distressed..

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **DESCRIPTION (Trauma to the Genitourinary Tract)** is incorrect (characterized by 1; Renal contusion or subcapsular hematoma; 2; Nonexpanding perirenal hematoma, 1 cm; parenchymal la). **OSMORECEPTOR DYSFUNCTION** is incorrect (characterized by Granulomatous (histiocytosis, sarcoidosis); Neoplastic (craniopharyngioma, pinealoma, meningioma,; m). **COMMON (>50%) (Human Metapneumovirus)** is incorrect (characterized by Fever >38°C (100.4°F); Cough; Rhinitis, coryza; Wheezing; Tachypnea, retractions; Hypoxia (O2 satura).
