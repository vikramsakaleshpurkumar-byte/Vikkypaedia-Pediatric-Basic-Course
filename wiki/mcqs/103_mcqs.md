### Question 1 (Clinical Pediatrics Vignette)
A pediatric patient is evaluated for the following clinical and diagnostic profile: Molecular Diagnosis of CSS For those whom clinical diagnosis of CSS is strongly suspected, CSS gene sequencing and deletion/duplication panel is recommended.. Which of the following conditions or syndromes is the most likely diagnosis?

- **A.** MVK (Urticaria (Hives) and Angioedema)
- **B.** RIME (Common Lesions of the Oral Soft Tissues)
- **C.** MYOPATHIES (Guillain-Barré Syndrome)
- **D.** Genetics of Common Disorders

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: D** — Genetics of Common Disorders

**Rationale:** **Genetics of Common Disorders** (associated with **`CHD7, KAT6A, KAT6B, KMT2A`**) is characterized by: Molecular Diagnosis of CSS For those whom clinical diagnosis of CSS is strongly suspected, CSS gene sequencing and deletion/duplication panel is recommended..

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **MVK (Urticaria (Hives) and Angioedema)** is incorrect (characterized by (mevalonate; kinase)). **RIME (Common Lesions of the Oral Soft Tissues)** is incorrect (characterized by Mycoplasma and other agents;; predominant oral ulcerations; with scattered cutaneous lesions). **MYOPATHIES (Guillain-Barré Syndrome)** is incorrect (characterized by Periodic paralyses (hypokalemic or hyperkalemic); Dermatomyositis; Critical illness myopathy/polyneu).
