- **A.** 250 U (Measles)
- **B.** BPT (Psychotherapy)
- **C.** Neck pain (Chronic Overlapping Pain Conditions)

**Correct Answer: D** — Hypophosphatasia

**Rationale:** **Hypophosphatasia** (associated with **`ALPL, AR`**) is characterized by: AD, Autosomal dominant; AR, autosomal recessive; E, elevated; L, low; N, normal; V, variable; XL, X-linked; Y, yes..

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **250 U (Measles)** is incorrect (characterized by 10; 3; Hepatitis A prophylaxis (as Ig):; Contact prophylaxis). **BPT (Psychotherapy)** is incorrect (characterized by Behavioral classroom management; Behavioral peer interventions; Organization (executive function); t). **Neck pain (Chronic Overlapping Pain Conditions)** is incorrect (characterized by Medical comorbidities; History of ventriculoperitoneal shunt; Certain medications; Signs of systemic).

### Question 2 (Pediatric Genetics & Molecular Etiology)
A child is diagnosed with **Hypophosphatasia** after presenting with AD, Autosomal dominant; the causative gene, autosomal recessive; E, elevated; L, low; N, normal; V, variable; XL, X-linked; Y, yes.. Pathogenic variants in which of the following genes are most classically implicated?

- **A.** ALPL
- **B.** SFTPB
- **C.** LIS1
- **D.** MYO5A

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: A** — ALPL

**Rationale:** Pathogenic variants in **`ALPL`** (X-Linked) are associated with **Hypophosphatasia** (AD, Autosomal dominant; AR, autosomal recessive; E, elevated; L, low; N, normal; V, variable; XL, X-linked; Y, yes.).

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **SFTPB** is incorrect (associated with Respiratory Distress Syndrome (Hyaline Membrane Disease)). **LIS1** is incorrect (associated with ANKLE2, CIT, WDFY3).

</details>
