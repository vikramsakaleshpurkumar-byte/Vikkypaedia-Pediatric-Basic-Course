### Question 1 (Clinical Pediatrics Vignette)
A pediatric patient is evaluated for the following clinical and diagnostic profile: Central incisor; 3-4 mo in utero; 4 mo; 7.5 mo; Lateral incisor; 4.5 mo in utero; 5 mo; 8 mo; Canine; 5.5 mo in utero; 9 mo; 16-20 mo; First molar; 5 mo in utero; 6 mo; 12-16 mo; Second molar; 6 mo in utero; 10-12 mo; 20-30 mo. Which of the following conditions or syndromes is the most likely diagnosis?

- **A.** EVC, EVC2 (Development and Developmental Anomalies of the Teeth)
- **B.** Mandibular (Development and Developmental Anomalies of the Teeth)
- **C.** Maxillary (Development and Developmental Anomalies of the Teeth)
- **D.** GLI3 (Development and Developmental Anomalies of the Teeth)

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: C** — Maxillary (Development and Developmental Anomalies of the Teeth)

**Rationale:** **Maxillary (Development and Developmental Anomalies of the Teeth)** is characterized by: Central incisor; 3-4 mo in utero; 4 mo; 7.5 mo; Lateral incisor; 4.5 mo in utero; 5 mo; 8 mo; Canine; 5.5 mo in utero; 9 mo; 16-20 mo; First molar; 5 mo in utero; 6 mo; 12-16 mo; Second molar; 6 mo in utero; 10-12 mo; 20-30 mo.

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **EVC, EVC2 (Development and Developmental Anomalies of the Teeth)** is incorrect (characterized by 7/1 × 106; Hallermann-Streiff; Dyscephaly, hypotrichosis, micro-ophthalmia, cataracts,; beaked nose,). **Mandibular (Development and Developmental Anomalies of the Teeth)** is incorrect (characterized by Central incisor; 4.5 mo in utero; 4 mo; 6.5 mo; Lateral incisor; 4.5 mo in utero; 4½ mo; 7 mo; Canin). **GLI3 (Development and Developmental Anomalies of the Teeth)** is incorrect (characterized by >13 cases to date; 8:5 male to female; Wiedemann-Rautenstrauch; Endocrine dysfunction, aged facies, ).

### Question 5 (Pediatric Board Mastery (Development and Developmental Anomalies of the Teeth))
