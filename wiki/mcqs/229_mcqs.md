### Question 1 (Clinical Pediatrics Vignette)
A pediatric patient is evaluated for the following clinical and diagnostic profile: Hypotension plus two or more of the following:; Renal impairment; Coagulopathy; Hepatic involvement; Adult respiratory distress syndrome; Generalized erythematous macular rash; Soft tissue necrosis. Which of the following conditions or syndromes is the most likely diagnosis?

- **A.** GROUP A STREPTOCOCCAL INFECTION
- **B.** CLINICAL CRITERIA (Group A Streptococcus)
- **C.** DEFINITE CASE (Group A Streptococcus)
- **D.** PROBABLE CASE (Group A Streptococcus)

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: B** — CLINICAL CRITERIA (Group A Streptococcus)

**Rationale:** **CLINICAL CRITERIA (Group A Streptococcus)** is characterized by: Hypotension plus two or more of the following:; Renal impairment; Coagulopathy; Hepatic involvement; Adult respiratory distress syndrome; Generalized erythematous macular rash; Soft tissue necrosis.

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **GROUP A STREPTOCOCCAL INFECTION** is incorrect (characterized by Carditis; Polyarthritis; Erythema marginatum; Subcutaneous nodules; Chorea). **DEFINITE CASE (Group A Streptococcus)** is incorrect (characterized by Clinical criteria plus group A Streptococcus from a normally sterile; site). **PROBABLE CASE (Group A Streptococcus)** is incorrect (characterized by Clinical criteria plus group A Streptococcus from a nonsterile site).

### Question 5 (Pediatric Board Mastery (Group A Streptococcus))
When formulating an evidence-based diagnostic and management plan for **Group A Streptococcus** (*Part XV: Infectious Diseases*), which of the following pediatric clinical statements is **ACCURATE**? (Item 1)

- **A.** Types 1, 12, 28, 4, 3, and 2 (in that order) are the most common causes of uncomplicated streptococcal pharyngitis in the United States.
- **B.** Inheritance is exclusively holandric (Y-linked) with 100% penetrance restricted to male neonates.
- **C.** Systemic tetracyclines and fluoroquinolones are universally required as routine prophylaxis in all healthy newborns.
- **D.** Clinical manifestations are permanently restricted to late adulthood and never present during infancy, childhood, or adolescence.

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: A** — Types 1, 12, 28, 4, 3, and 2 (in that order) are the most common causes of uncomplicated streptococcal pharyngitis in the United States.

**Rationale:** **Pediatric Clinical Principle (Group A Streptococcus):** Types 1, 12, 28, 4, 3, and 2 (in that order) are the most common causes of uncomplicated streptococcal pharyngitis in the United States.
