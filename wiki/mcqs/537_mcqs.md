### Question 1 (Clinical Pediatrics Vignette)
A pediatric patient is evaluated for the following clinical and diagnostic profile: Cystic LMs are the most common congenital lymphatic anomalies.. Which of the following conditions or syndromes is the most likely diagnosis?

- **A.** DIAGNOSES INCLUDE (Marfan Syndrome)
- **B.** Anatomy and Function of the Lymphatic System
- **C.** FULFILLS AT LEAST TWO OF THE FOLLOWING
- **D.** GASTROINTESTINAL (Overweight and Obesity)

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: B** — Anatomy and Function of the Lymphatic System

**Rationale:** **Anatomy and Function of the Lymphatic System** (associated with **`EPHB4, GLA, KRAS, NRAS, PIK3CA`**) is characterized by: Cystic LMs are the most common congenital lymphatic anomalies..

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **DIAGNOSES INCLUDE (Marfan Syndrome)** is incorrect (characterized by 1. Ectopia lentis ± systemic score AND FBN1 pathogenic variant not; known to associate with aortic a). **FULFILLS AT LEAST TWO OF THE FOLLOWING** is incorrect (characterized by a. Chronic cytopenia(s) detected on at least two occasions over at; least 3 months†; b. Reduced marr). **GASTROINTESTINAL (Overweight and Obesity)** is incorrect (characterized by Gallbladder disease; Abdominal pain, vomiting, jaundice; Ultrasound; Nonalcoholic fatty liver diseas).
