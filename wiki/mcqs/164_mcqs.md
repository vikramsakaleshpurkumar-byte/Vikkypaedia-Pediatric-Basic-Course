### Question 1 (Clinical Pediatrics Vignette)
A pediatric patient is evaluated for the following clinical and diagnostic profile: Common gamma chain (the causative gene, X-linked), the causative gene. Which of the following conditions or syndromes is the most likely diagnosis?

- **A.** T– B+ NK– SCID (Orientation to the Consideration of Inborn Errors of Immunity)
- **B.** COMBINED IMMUNODEFICIENCIES WITH SYNDROMIC OR OTHER FEATURES
- **C.** COMBINED IMMUNODEFICIENCIES, GENERALLY LESS SEVERE
- **D.** THYMIC DEFECTS WITH ADDITIONAL CONGENITAL ANOMALIES

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: A** — T– B+ NK– SCID (Orientation to the Consideration of Inborn Errors of Immunity)

**Rationale:** **T– B+ NK– SCID (Orientation to the Consideration of Inborn Errors of Immunity)** (associated with **`IL2RG, JAK3`**) is characterized by: Common gamma chain (IL2RG, X-linked), JAK3.

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **COMBINED IMMUNODEFICIENCIES WITH SYNDROMIC OR OTHER FEATURES** is incorrect (characterized by With thrombocytopenia; Wiskott-Aldrich syndrome; DNA repair defects; Ataxia telangiectasia, Nijmegen). **COMBINED IMMUNODEFICIENCIES, GENERALLY LESS SEVERE** is incorrect (characterized by MHC class I and II defects; T-cell receptor defects; DOCK8, IL21, and IL21R, and others). **THYMIC DEFECTS WITH ADDITIONAL CONGENITAL ANOMALIES** is incorrect (characterized by DiGeorge, velocardiofacial syndrome; CHARGE syndrome).

### Question 3 (Pediatric Genetics & Molecular Etiology)
A child is diagnosed with **T– B+ NK– SCID (Orientation to the Consideration of Inborn Errors of Immunity)** after presenting with Common gamma chain (the causative gene, X-linked), the causative gene. Pathogenic variants in which of the following genes are most classically implicated?

- **A.** COL1A1
- **B.** GNPAT
- **C.** ATP7B
- **D.** IL2RG

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: D** — IL2RG

**Rationale:** Pathogenic variants in **`IL2RG`** (X-Linked) are associated with **T– B+ NK– SCID (Orientation to the Consideration of Inborn Errors of Immunity)** (Common gamma chain (IL2RG, X-linked), JAK3).

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **COL1A1** is incorrect (associated with COL1A1, COL1A2 (Osteogenesis Imperfecta)). **ATP7B** is incorrect (associated with Wilson Disease (ATP7B, OMIM #277900)).
