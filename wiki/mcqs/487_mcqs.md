### Question 4 (Differential Diagnosis (Rheumatic Heart Disease))
During a pediatric consultation regarding **Rheumatic Heart Disease**, which clinical entity or concept is described by the following profile? "Mitral insufficiency is the result of structural changes that may include some loss of valvular substance and/or changes to the subvalvular appa ratus, including elongation of the chordae, both of which can lead to valve dysfunction."

- **A.** Treatment
- **B.** Pulmonary Valve Disease
- **C.** Clinical Manifestations
- **D.** PATTERNS OF VALVULAR DISEASE Mitral Insufficiency

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: D** — PATTERNS OF VALVULAR DISEASE Mitral Insufficiency

**Rationale:** PATTERNS OF VALVULAR DISEASE Mitral Insufficiency Mitral insufficiency is the result of structural changes that may include some loss of valvular substance and/or changes to the subvalvular appa ratus, including elongation of the chordae, both of which can lead to valve dysfunction.
